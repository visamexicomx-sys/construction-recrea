Luxury villas in Punta Paraíso

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Punta Paraíso is a low-density seafront between Playa del Carmen and Puerto Aventuras, with protected coastal vegetation along the shore. A luxury villa here starts with a documented boundary survey, because the protected strip decides where anything can be built.

Survey before any earth moves

Protected coastal vegetation runs along the front, and the federal environmental file needs a documented boundary and vegetation survey before any clearing. We commission it first, mark the protected strip on site and design the villa to sit entirely behind it.

Rocky shore, high water table

The ground is limestone and sand with a high water table close to the sea. Foundations are designed for both, the pool is anchored against uplift, and waterproofing is detailed from the negative side so the lower floor stays dry.

Low density, high privacy

With few neighbours and the highway well back, privacy is the main asset. We orient the house to the sea, screen the entrance from the access road and keep lighting low and shielded towards the beach.

Punta Paraíso sits with the coastal front row: severe salt, a high water table and SEMARNAT on the coastal strip. The survey and environmental file come first; large glazing and pool terraces push towards the top.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Punta Paraíso: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Solidaridad
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT on the coastal strip
  • Ground: limestone and sand
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: off the federal highway
  • Development regime: low density

Frequently asked questions

Why does Punta Paraíso need a boundary survey before design?

Because protected coastal vegetation runs along the front, and the environmental file must document it before any clearing. The survey fixes where the villa can sit.

Is Punta Paraíso private?

Yes: low density and distance from the highway make it one of the more private seafronts on this stretch.
