What a Recrea Bathroom Includes

Full Waterproofing

Cementitious or liquid membrane over the whole shower and floor, flood-tested for 24 hours before any tile goes down.

Ventilation

Ducted extractors sized for the room, because an open window is not enough in 85% humidity.

Vanities & Lighting

Custom vanities from our carpentry shop, mirrors with LED lighting and fittings rated for hard water.

Water and fittings for a bathroom in Xpu-Há

Many properties depend on wells and cisterns with hard, sometimes brackish water, so filtration and softening are part of most bathroom and maintenance work. That is why we size softeners, filters and pumps with the bathroom, and choose fittings that tolerate scale.

Humidity, salt and finishes in Xpu-Há

Exposure on the beachfront is strong: salt, sun and wind reach terraces, pools and facades every day, which shortens the life of paint, wood and metal without marine-grade specifications. Grout, paint, extraction and metal fittings are specified for these conditions so the bathroom still looks new after several seasons.

Rules, licences and administration in Xpu-Há

Licences come from Solidaridad; the beach is under ZOFEMAT and many lots sit near mangroves and cenotes, so landscaping and exterior works must respect environmental limits.

Rentals and scheduling in Xpu-Há

Most villas are luxury rentals with high season in winter, so we work in short windows and leave everything guest-ready.

Materials, crews and access in Xpu-Há

Xpu-Há is about 30 minutes from our workshops; access roads to the beach are narrow, so deliveries are planned with small trucks.
