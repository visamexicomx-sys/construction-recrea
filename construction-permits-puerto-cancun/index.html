Construction Permits in Puerto Cancun: What to Know

Puerto Cancún is a marina development on the water, and that doubles the counter: on top of the Benito Juárez municipal file, the canal edge, the moorings and any work over water fall under federal concession and the development's own rulebook. The typical delay here comes from doing things in the wrong order. Owners who file with the town hall first and the committee second usually end up correcting the facade, the heights or the material palette and refiling, losing their place in the review queue.

The committee ruling comes before the municipal file

In Puerto Cancún the process runs on two tracks, and the order matters. The development's design committee rules on height, storeys, setbacks, facade palette, plant location and trees; that ruling is worth having before filing with Benito Juárez, because a scheme approved by the city and refused by the committee gets redrawn from scratch. Allow four to eight weeks for the internal review and a construction bond — typically 50,000 to 150,000 MXN — recovered after the exit inspection, together with the condition survey of roads signed off before you start.

Canal, marina and golf: the additional consents

Lots fronting the canal or marina add procedures that do not apply inland: any work on the canal edge, a retaining wall, revetment or dock carries its own permit and, depending on the case, environmental review. Before designing terraces cantilevered over water, confirm in writing what is permitted on that specific frontage. On golf frontage, the mandatory setback and the course's access and maintenance easements are examined in the same committee ruling. None of this prevents building, but all of it is scheduled alongside the municipal licence rather than after it.

The permit file in Puerto Cancún: what actually goes in

There is no single procedure for the Riviera Maya. In Puerto Cancún the competent authority is the Benito Juárez municipality, the environmental regime is municipal + ZOFEMAT on the marina frontage, and that decides which documents enter the file and in what order.

The water table next to the canal is high and salt exposure by the basin is severe. That is not a cosmetic detail: the structural calculations attached to the licence have to justify concrete cover and anchorage, and the municipal reviewer asks for it.

The file for Puerto Cancún, in order

  1. Land-use certificate (Benito Juárez municipality)
  2. ZOFEMAT concession for the federal maritime-land strip
  3. Construction licence with a Perito Responsable de Obra (P.R.O.) registered in Benito Juárez
  4. Water and drainage feasibility and contract (CAPA/CONAGUA as applicable)
  5. CFE electrical feasibility and service connection
  6. Sign-off from the development’s design committee or management
  7. Notice of completion and certificate of occupancy

What shapes the process in Puerto Cancún

The ground is limestone and sand and the water table is high next to the canal, which weighs on the soil study that accompanies the licence application. Access is gated access with registration and the development regime is development rulebook, design committee.

  • The federal maritime-land strip is concessioned and paid for annually; it does not travel with the deed. The survey and the application are filed separately and carry their own response time.
  • Municipal process only: the town hall sets the calendar, not an environmental authority. This is the fastest scenario in the region.
  • High water table: the foundation sits higher and the pool needs anchoring against uplift.
  • Severe salt exposure: 316 stainless throughout the hardware, concrete cover above the code minimum and six-monthly maintenance. 304 pits within two years at this distance from the sea.
  • Sand lowers bearing capacity; the study sets the thickness of the compacted platform before any pour.
  • Design committee or community management: the project is approved twice — committee first, municipality second. Add 4–8 weeks and one round of facade changes.

The document that decides it in Puerto Cancún

The design committee's approval, together with the survey of the water's edge. The committee reviews massing, facade and materials, and the municipality asks for that ruling inside the package. If the project also touches the canal — dock, breakwater, edge retaining wall — a separate concession process opens, with its own drawings and its own calculations. Each runs at its own pace and none waits for the others, so the real start date is set by the slowest of the three, not by the municipal licence.

Puerto Cancún compared with Puerto Aventuras

The two share marina logic, but Puerto Cancún sits inside Benito Juárez and is read against Cancún’s urban planning, with its demanding design committee and internal rulebook. Puerto Aventuras belongs to Solidaridad and its main friction is operational: access, hours and dues. In Puerto Cancún a project is won or lost at the facade review; in Puerto Aventuras, in the day-to-day coordination of the site.

In Cancún it is not a DRO, it is a Perito Responsable de Obra

Same role, different name, and it is worth knowing before you call the counter. The Solidaridad code, which governs Playa del Carmen, speaks of a Director Responsable de Obra (DRO). The Benito Juárez municipality, which is Cancún, registers and publishes its professionals as Perito Responsable de Obra (P.R.O.), alongside the Corresponsable de Estructura and Corresponsable de Instalaciones roles for projects that require them by size or use.

In practice the function is identical: they sign the project, answer legally for code compliance and keep the site logbook. What changes is the register: a professional listed in Solidaridad is not automatically authorised in Benito Juárez, and vice versa. That is why a file assembled in Playa del Carmen cannot be filed as-is in Cancún.

We work with a professional listed on the register that matches the municipality, and bring in the co-responsible specialists when the project calls for them. In a Cancún filing, that registration is the first thing the town hall checks.
