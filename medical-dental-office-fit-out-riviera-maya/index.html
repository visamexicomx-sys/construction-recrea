Medical and Dental Office Fit-Out in the Riviera Maya

A consultorio is an office with hospital-grade details: water and drainage at every chair, compressed air and vacuum for dentistry, X-ray shielding, washable hygienic finishes, accessibility and a layout that passes the health authority. We fit out doctors’ and dentists’ offices in plazas, medical towers and houses in Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Chetumal. For hospitals and larger clinics see our clinic construction pages.

Quick answer: About $20,000–$40,000 MXN/m² in 2026, so a 30 m² consultorio runs roughly $0.6M–$1.2M before equipment.
2026 references (Cancún base): medical or dental fit-out $20,000–$40,000 MXN/m²; a 30 m² consultorio $0.6M–$1.2M; a 120 m² dental clinic with 3 chairs $2.4M–$4.8M before equipment. Playa +4%, Tulum +13%, Chetumal −8%.

What a Consultorio Needs

Water at Every Chair

Supply, drainage and sinks where clinical work happens.

Air & Vacuum

Compressor and suction lines for dental chairs, in a ventilated plant room.

X-Ray Shielding

Lead or baryte shielding and the documentation the licence requires.

Hygienic Finishes

Washable, joint-free surfaces and coved skirtings.

Accessibility

Step-free access, door widths and an accessible toilet.

Health Licences

Layout and documents ready for the health authority’s review.

Plan the installations before the layout

In a consultorio the expensive mistakes are under the floor: drainage that cannot reach a chair, a compressor in a room without ventilation, an X-ray room in the wrong place. We start with the clinical flow and the equipment list, place the wet and technical points, then design the reception, waiting area and finishes. In a plaza or tower we confirm drainage and power capacity before you sign.

Licences

Besides the fit-out licence and Civil Protection, medical and dental offices have health-authority requirements, and dental X-ray equipment has its own radiation-safety requirements. We build to them and prepare the drawings and records your licence needs.
