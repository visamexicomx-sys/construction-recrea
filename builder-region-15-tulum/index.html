Building in Region 15, Tulum: What to Know

Region 15 is one of Tulum’s fastest-growing residential zones with the best entry price — more affordable than Aldea Zama and La Veleta, with strong investment and vacation-rental demand. Being in the municipality of Tulum, every project requires SEMA environmental authorization ($50,000–$150,000 MXN, 2–4 months); jungle lots need a land-clearing permit with reforestation, and many include a cistern and water treatment. As a builder in Region 15, we handle SEMA and coordinate everything friction-free — ideal for investors who don’t live here.
