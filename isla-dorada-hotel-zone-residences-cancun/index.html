Isla Dorada, Cancún Hotel Zone (lagoon islands): renovation and fit-out of residences

Isla Dorada is the gated nautical residential on man-made islands in Nichupté lagoon: houses on private lots with around 15 m of lagoon frontage, 24-hour security, controlled access and a club house with gym, spa and courts. You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked.

What is built here

Mostly single-family houses, many from the 1990s and 2000s, with boats at their own docks; the typical project is a complete renovation or a rebuild with pool, terraces and a new dock.
