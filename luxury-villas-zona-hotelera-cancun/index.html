Luxury villas in Zona Hotelera, Cancún

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Cancún’s hotel zone is a strip of sand between the Caribbean and the Nichupté lagoon, with hotel density fixed by the FONATUR plan and federal frontage on both sides. Luxury here is a residence on one of the few private lots, a penthouse or the renovation of an existing villa.

Federal frontage on both sides

Sea-side lots carry the maritime zone; lagoon-side lots carry frontage on Nichupté, with its mangrove and its own rules. We draw both lines and the FONATUR land use before design, because they decide what a lot can hold.

Renovation is often the project

Many owners here buy an existing house or apartment and transform it. We survey the structure, check corrosion of reinforcement in older buildings, and plan a renovation that upgrades waterproofing, glazing and services to current coastal standards.

Built for wind and salt

The strip takes the full force of hurricanes and salt from both waters. Impact-rated or shuttered glazing, 316 stainless, tied roof slabs and corrosion-protected structure are the baseline for new work and renovations alike.

The hotel zone sits high on the coastal scale: severe salt, a very high water table and federal frontage on two sides. Structural repair of older buildings and impact glazing weigh on renovations; new residences reach the upper bands.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Zona Hotelera, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession)
  • Ground: sand and dune over limestone
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: established urban street grid
  • Development regime: hotel use, tightly capped rules

A strip between sea and lagoon: hotel density is fixed by the FONATUR plan and there is federal frontage on both sides.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build a private residence in Cancún’s hotel zone?

On the few lots with residential use under the FONATUR plan, yes. Most luxury projects here are renovations of existing houses and penthouses.

What do you check before renovating an older building in the hotel zone?

Reinforcement corrosion, waterproofing, glazing and services. Salt from sea and lagoon ages buildings fast, so the structural survey comes first.
