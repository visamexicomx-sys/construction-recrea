Luxury villas in Región 12, Tulum

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Región 12 is one of Tulum’s numbered urban regions, with its own cadastral numbering, streets still being built out and services that arrive unevenly. For a luxury villa it offers space at a lower price than the established districts, if the paperwork and services are checked lot by lot.

Cadastral numbering and the paper trail

Tulum’s regions use their own cadastral numbering, and lots have changed hands quickly during the boom. We check that the plot number, boundaries and registered title match on the ground before design, with a boundary survey signed by the surveyor.

Services arrive unevenly

Power, water and drainage reach some streets years before others. We confirm the CFE feasibility and the water and drainage situation for the lot, and design a cistern, treatment plant or transformer where they are needed, so the villa is habitable when it is finished.

Building the street’s first good house

Many neighbours are still empty lots. We design the villa to stay private as the street fills in, with courtyards and screened terraces, and choose a structure that can be built efficiently with mainland crews and paved-road deliveries where possible.

Región 12 sits in the urban band: karst, low salt and no committee premium. A transformer, a treatment plant or road works for the access can add to the figure on some lots.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Región 12, Tulum: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst under low jungle
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: streets still being built out
  • Development regime: municipal density, no committee

Frequently asked questions

Is Región 12 a good place to build a villa?

It offers larger lots at a lower price than central Tulum. The key is to confirm the title, the boundaries and the services for your specific lot before buying.

Who provides electricity in Región 12?

CFE, but the network does not reach every street yet. We request the feasibility early and, where needed, budget a transformer or line extension.
