Solar Panels in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Riviera Maya has sun almost year-round and one of Mexico’s highest CFE bills — from A/C, the pool pump and DAC tariffs. That gives solar here a 3–5 year ROI and then 20+ years of nearly free electricity. The key is correct sizing to your consumption and the system type: CFE interconnection (net metering) for maximum savings, or hybrid with batteries if you want autonomy during outages and hurricanes. Mounting must be hurricane-proof with reinforced structure. We coordinate solar with the electrical installation and, if you wish, with a backup generator — all under one roof, in any city.
