Corrosion Protection and Repair of Steel in Playa del Carmen

In Playa del Carmen, almost every building is within walking distance of the sea, and rust shows up first where people look most: balcony and rooftop railings, spiral stairs, rooftop pergolas and equipment frames, parking canopies. Boutique hotels and condos around Quinta Avenida and in Playacar repaint every year or two and still lose the battle. We inspect, repair and protect with marine systems that last.

Repainting is not protecting

Many Playa properties repaint rusty railings every season with a single coat over loose rust. It looks fine for a few months and fails by the next rainy season. Real protection starts with removing the rust properly, then a zinc-rich primer, an epoxy build coat and a UV-resistant topcoat. Where railings are beyond repair, we replace them with 316 stainless or galvanized and painted steel.

Working around guests and neighbours

In central Playa, buildings are close together and streets are busy. We use mechanical cleaning or contained blasting, low-odour coatings and early working hours, and phase work by floor or area so hotels and condos keep operating.
