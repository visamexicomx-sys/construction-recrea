Puerto Cancún rules we handle for you

  • Community approval first. Puerto Cancún's residential areas have their own architectural guidelines; drawings are usually reviewed by the community before the municipal licence is requested, so we prepare them to those rules from day one.
  • Municipal licence. Construction licences come from Benito Juárez with DRO-signed drawings, plus Protección Civil and utility feasibility; we file and follow them up.
  • Tower administration. In condo towers, works need the administration's approval, set work hours, lift booking for materials, deposits and insurance certificates; our crews are registered and insured (IMSS).
  • Gate and deliveries. Access is controlled, so crews, trucks and deliveries are scheduled and registered with security in advance.
  • Waterfront. Docks on the marina or canals and anything on the beach need a federal (ZOFEMAT) concession and environmental review, plus the marina's own rules.

Each community and building applies its own rules; we confirm them with the administration before quoting.
