Hotel Construction in Cancún: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Cancún has the largest hotel stock in Mexico’s Caribbean, but new projects look different from the 1980s towers of the Hotel Zone. Today we build 3-star and business hotels downtown near the airport road, Avenida Tulum and the Tren Maya station, boutique hotels in Puerto Cancún and Malecón Tajamar, and redevelopments of older properties in the Hotel Zone.

Cancún 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $9,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $12,000–$17,000; boutique and 4-star $17,000–$24,000; 5-star and luxury $24,000–$40,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

City hotels for a year-round market

Cancún is not only beach: airline crews, business travellers, medical tourists and Tren Maya passengers fill city hotels all year. These projects win on location, efficient room modules and speed; we often use steel structures to open a season earlier.

Hotel Zone redevelopment

Free beachfront land in the Hotel Zone is almost gone, so most new beach rooms come from demolishing or repositioning older hotels. We assess the existing structure, the federal-zone concession and the density allowed before the owner decides between renovation and rebuild.

Benito Juárez rules

Density, height and parking come from the Benito Juárez urban development programme; the municipal licence needs DRO-signed drawings, Protección Civil and, near the lagoon or sea, environmental review. Aguakan’s connection capacity is checked at the start.
