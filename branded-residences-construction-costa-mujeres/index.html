Branded Residences Construction in Costa Mujeres

Residences carrying a hotel brand sell for a premium of 25–40% in Costa Mujeres, and the brand inspects everything. We build branded residences to the operator’s technical and design standards: luxury finishes, quiet systems, amenity levels, service corridors and back-of-house for the operator, and a handover the brand signs off — for developers working with international hospitality brands.

Quick answer: Branded Residences Construction in Costa Mujeres — 2026 prices: Construction, luxury tower or low-rise, per m²: $42,000–$72,000 MXN; Per residence (≈180 m² gross): $7.6M–$13M MXN; Amenity levels: spa, gym, pools, owners’ lounge: $18M–$54M MXN; 40-residence project: $360M–$660M MXN.

What We Deliver

Brand Standards

Brand Sign-Off

Building to a brand

The operator’s manual covers everything from slab thicknesses for acoustics to the lux level in corridors. Mock-up residences are built and approved before production, and the brand’s technical services inspect at milestones. We price the manual line by line so the premium is known before sales launch.

Built for operation

Branded residences are serviced like a hotel: service lifts, housekeeping stores, staff routes, deliveries and an owners’ lounge. We design those routes with the operator so the building runs like the brand’s hotels.

Building in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is Cancún’s newest coastal corridor: brand residences, condo towers next to the resorts and gated communities. Coastal setbacks, hurricane design and premium specs push costs about 20% above downtown Cancún.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Isla Mujeres; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
