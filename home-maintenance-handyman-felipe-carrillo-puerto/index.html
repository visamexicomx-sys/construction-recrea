What wears out first in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Being inland, there is no salt, but heat and rainy-season humidity are high, so ventilation, anti-mould paint and good roof waterproofing matter most. Our checklist puts these problems first: rust, sealant, roof membrane, AC drains and exterior finishes.

Water systems to keep running in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Water comes from the municipal network and wells, often hard, so filters and simple softeners are common recommendations. Pumps, cisterns, filters and softeners are checked on every visit, because when they fail the property stops working.
