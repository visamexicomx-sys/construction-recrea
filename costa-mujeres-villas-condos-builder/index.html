Owners here usually build new or finish a unit delivered by the developer, and need several trades at once: design, permits, structure, pool, dock, interiors. Recrea delivers it all under one fixed-price contract, with an English-speaking project manager, weekly photo and video reports and milestone payments.

Costa Mujeres rules we handle for you

  • Right municipality. Most of Costa Mujeres, Playa Mujeres and Isla Blanca belong to the continental zone of the Isla Mujeres municipality, while Puerto Juárez belongs to Benito Juárez; we file the licence with DRO-signed drawings where the lot actually sits.
  • Community approval. Playa Mujeres and other gated developments review drawings against their architectural guidelines before the licence, and control access for crews and trucks.
  • Tower administration. New condo towers apply fit-out manuals: approved drawings, contractor registration, insurance, deposits and set hours; our crews are registered and insured (IMSS).
  • Coast and lagoon. Anything on the beach or on Chacmuchuch lagoon sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review; mangrove and turtle-nesting beaches are protected.
  • Services. North of Punta Sam water, power and sewer are not available everywhere; we design wells, water treatment, septic systems and solar when the lot needs them.

Each community and building applies its own rules; we confirm them with the administration before quoting.
