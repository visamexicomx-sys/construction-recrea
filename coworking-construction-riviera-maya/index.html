Coworking Construction in the Riviera Maya: Reliability Is the Product

Members here are on North American or European hours, on video calls most of the day, and they leave within a week over a dropped connection or a room too hot to think in. We build coworking spaces in Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Cancún around that: redundant internet and power, A/C with redundancy, acoustic zoning and real call booths, then the design that sells memberships.

Quick answer: Fit-out runs about $12,000–$25,000 MXN/m² (Playa about 4% above Cancún). At 9–12 m² per member that is roughly $110,000–$300,000 MXN per member, before furniture and the generator.
2026 planning figures: 6–8 m² per member for hot-desk density, 9–12 m² for a comfortable mixed model, 12–18 m² where private offices dominate; one call booth per 8–12 members. Fit-out $12,000–$25,000 MXN/m², about $110,000–$300,000 per member at 9–12 m² (derived estimate).

What We Build

Internet Redundancy

Two providers by separate physical routes with automatic failover.

Backup Power

Generator with automatic transfer for the floor, lifts and A/C — the highest-value item.

A/C With Redundancy

Several units per zone so one failure degrades a room, not the floor.

Call Booths

Built-in booths with their own ventilation and sealed doors.

Acoustic Zoning

Quiet, working, social and call zones; partitions to the slab.

Kitchen & Terrace

A real kitchen and a shaded terrace usable as working space.

Acoustics around calls

Open plan with hard tropical finishes — polished concrete, glass, chukum — is the worst room for people on calls, and it is the default look here. Zone by noise: a quiet zone with no calls, a general zone, a social zone and enclosed call space. Put absorbent panels on the ceiling (mineral wool, not foam, which does not survive the humidity), soft floors or rugs, and a low background hum. Booths need their own air supply, or they are unusable within fifteen minutes.

Where coworking works

Playa del Carmen near Quinta Avenida and in the residential areas, Tulum in Aldea Zama and La Veleta, and Cancún near the Hotel Zone and Puerto Cancún. Members pay for comfort and reliability more than for a low price, so space per member and air quality pay back.
