Bathroom Remodel in Pok-Ta-Pok, Cancún

Old house bathrooms in Pok-Ta-Pok get new plumbing, waterproofing, stone and walk-in showers, often with an outdoor shower to the garden.

Houses from the 1970s–1990s on generous lots and small condo buildings with lagoon views; many have been updated piecemeal and now need a complete renovation of MEP, glazing and finishes.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, golf island

The lagoon side is sheltered from waves but humid, with mangrove, mosquitoes and fluctuating water levels; docks, seawalls and gardens need lagoon-specific detailing.

Leaks in towers are expensive

A failed shower in a tower damages the unit below; we waterproof, flood-test and document every bathroom so the administration and your insurer have proof.

Water, scale and salt

Cancún water is hard, so we choose fittings and glass treatments that tolerate scale and add filtration where needed.

Rules in this sector

Houses follow the residential association's rules and a Benito Juárez licence with DRO for exterior or structural works; anything on the shore sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review. Bathroom works in towers need the administration's approval and coordination with the unit below for any plumbing change.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
