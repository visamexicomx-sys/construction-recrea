HVAC in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

In the hot, humid Riviera Maya climate, air conditioning is a home’s biggest electricity consumer — so the right unit and calculation change everything. A precise heat-load calculation (by orientation, windows, insulation and m²) avoids oversized units that waste energy. High-efficiency inverter units (high SEER) cut consumption by up to 40%. Good design combines air conditioning, cross-ventilation and insulation — coordinated with the electrical and the build from the project stage.
