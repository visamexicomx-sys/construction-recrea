Bathroom Remodel in Playa Magna: walk-in showers, stone and full waterproofing

We remodel bathrooms in Playa Magna buildings and villas from the slab up: demolition, new plumbing, full waterproofing, large-format tile or stone, walk-in showers, glass and extraction. Playa Magna is an established residential community in Playa del Carmen, in the Solidaridad municipality: family houses on standard lots with every service at the street, close to schools and shops, with its own rulebook.

Quick answer: Reference ranges in Playa Magna: Refresh (Fixtures, vanity, mirror, paint): $35,000–$70,000; Standard remodel 4–6 m² (Plumbing, waterproofing, porcelain, glass): $95,000–$180,000; Master bathroom (Large-format, double vanity, rain shower): $180,000–$300,000; Premium / spa (Stone, freestanding tub, steam): $300,000–$450,000+.

What we do

Demolition and plumbing

New supply and drain lines, valves and pressure checks.

Waterproofing

Membranes on floors and shower walls, flood-tested before tiling.

Tile and stone

Large-format porcelain, marble and natural stone.

Walk-in showers

Linear drains, rain heads and frameless glass.

Extraction

Quiet fans and humidity control against mould.

Spa bathrooms

Freestanding tubs, steam showers and outdoor showers.

Leaks in buildings are expensive

A failed shower in a building damages the unit below; we waterproof, flood-test and document every bathroom so the administration and your insurer have proof.

Water, scale and salt

Riviera Maya water is hard, so we choose fittings and glass treatments that tolerate scale and add filtration where needed.

Playa Magna rules we handle

Bathroom works in buildings need the administration's approval and coordination with the unit below for any plumbing change.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
