Villa and hotel construction in Costa Mujeres

Boutique hotels and rental villas north of Cancún: development guidelines, municipal licence, construction and a handover ready to operate.

Costa Mujeres is dominated by large all-inclusive resorts, which leaves a clear gap: small, design-led properties and rental villas for guests who want the same beach without the wristband. Building one starts with the development’s rules.

Check the rental rules before the design

Many residential developments here restrict or regulate short-term rentals, minimum stays or commercial use. Before anyone draws a hotel or a rental villa, we read the development’s regulations and the Isla Mujeres land-use plan, because a building that cannot legally operate is the most expensive mistake on this coast.

Competing with the resorts next door

A boutique property wins on what the resorts cannot offer: privacy, architecture and service. That means fewer, larger keys, outdoor space for every room and quiet mechanical systems. We put the budget into those and keep back-of-house efficient, since staff and supplies come from Cancún.

A full coastal specification

The dune is exposed to wind and salt year-round and the water table is high. We use 316 stainless, coastal-grade window systems, ventilated roofs and waterproofing designed for a high water table under pools and cisterns; the development committee also reviews colours and materials.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Costa Mujeres

For a boutique hotel or rental villa in Costa Mujeres we work in a range of MXN 20,000 to 36,000 per built m² (roughly USD 1,090 – 1,960/m²). Freight is low, but development finish standards and full marine exposure keep the figure in the coastal range.

A ten to twenty-four key property takes twelve to sixteen months of construction. The development’s design review adds four to eight weeks at the start and runs in parallel with the municipal licence.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Costa Mujeres: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Isla Mujeres (continental zone)
  • Environmental regime: development guidelines + municipal + ZOFEMAT/NOM-022 where applicable
  • Ground: dune sand over limestone
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A fast-growing resort coast north of Cancún: development rules on top of municipal ones, and full marine exposure.

  • Ask the developer for the architectural guidelines and the list of approved contractors before you commission a design.
  • Allow for the development’s review committee in the programme and file it in parallel with the municipal licence.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run a rental villa in a Costa Mujeres development?

It depends on the development: some allow short-term rental freely, some regulate it and some forbid it. We check its regulations before design, together with the municipal land-use plan.

Does it make sense to build a small hotel next to the large resorts?

Yes, if it offers what they do not: privacy, design and personal service. The beach and the airport access are the same; the guest is different.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
