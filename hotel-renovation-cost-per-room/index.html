How Much Does It Cost to Renovate a Hotel? Cost per Room and per Key (2026)

The three levels of hotel renovation — soft refresh, full guest room and bathroom, repositioning — priced per key in 2026, with what each includes, the cost of public areas, pools and facades, and the factor for each town in Quintana Roo.

Quick answer: How Much Does It Cost to Renovate a Hotel? Cost per Room and per Key (2026) — prices: Soft refresh: paint, soft goods, lighting, artwork: $60,000–$150,000 MXN; Case goods and seating (FF&E): $80,000–$250,000 MXN; Flooring: LVT, porcelain or engineered wood: $25,000–$70,000 MXN; Bathroom: tub to walk-in shower, waterproofing, fixtures: $120,000–$280,000 MXN.
2026, Cancún base, per key: soft refresh $150,000–$350,000 MXN; full guest room and bathroom $400,000–$900,000; repositioning $900,000–$2.2M. Working around an open hotel adds 15–30%.

The Three Levels

Soft Refresh

Paint, soft goods, lighting, artwork, minor FF&E — every 5–7 years.

Full Room + Bathroom

Case goods, flooring, bathroom, A/C — every 10–15 years.

Repositioning

Layout, facade, MEP and public areas — a new category or brand.

Public Areas

Lobby, restaurants and bars at $15,000–$35,000 per m².

Pools & Decks

$0.4M–$1.8M per pool, timed to low season.

Facades

Concrete repair, balconies and impact windows after salt and hurricanes.

What each level includes

  • Soft refresh: paint, carpets or LVT, curtains, bedding, lighting, artwork and some loose furniture. Rooms are out of order for days.
  • Full guest room and bathroom: everything in the soft refresh plus case goods, flooring, the bathroom down to the waterproofing, A/C and controls. 3–5 weeks per room in floor-by-floor production.
  • Repositioning: changes to layout and room count, facade and balconies, chillers, hot water, electrical and fire systems, and the public areas — usually for a new brand or category.

Hidden costs in coastal hotels

Corroded pipes, failed bathroom and balcony waterproofing, rusted rebar and undersized A/C are what turn a refresh into a full renovation. Open sample rooms before fixing the budget.
