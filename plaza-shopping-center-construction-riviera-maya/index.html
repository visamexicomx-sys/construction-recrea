Plazas and Shopping Centres in the Riviera Maya: Build, Renovate, Fit Out

One builder for the whole life of a retail property: new plazas and shopping centres, renovation of existing ones while tenants trade, fit-out of retail units for brands and franchises, remodels of operating stores and the maintenance that keeps the plaza leased. In Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Puerto Morelos and Puerto Aventuras.

Quick answer: In 2026 the shell runs about $9,000–$16,000 MXN per leasable m² in Cancún, 4–13% more in Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos, Puerto Aventuras and Tulum, plus $25,000–$70,000 per parking space. For an 800 m² neighbourhood plaza the shell alone is $7.2M–$12.8M MXN; with parking and services, about $9M–$18M before land and VAT.
2026 budget guide (Cancún base): new plaza shell $9,000–$16,000 MXN per leasable m²; shopping centre $14,000–$28,000/m² built; retail fit-out $5,500–$45,000/m²; store remodel $1,800–$18,000/m²; plaza facade refresh $1,500–$4,500 per m² of facade. Playa +4%, Puerto Morelos +9%, Puerto Aventuras +11%, Tulum +13%.

Who we work for

  • Landowners and developers turning a lot on an avenue or the highway into a leased plaza.
  • Plaza owners and administrators who need to reposition an older centre, fix leaks and power, or keep it maintained.
  • Brands, franchises and local businesses opening or remodelling a store, café, clinic or office in a plaza or on a street.
  • Investors abroad who need one accountable contractor and weekly reports instead of managing trades.

How we work

Feasibility first: land use, parking ratio, access, services and permits decide what can be built or opened, so we check them before design. Then a line-item, fixed-price budget, a programme built around leases and trading, our own workshop for joinery and storefronts, and weekly dated photo and video reports until the keys or the opening.
