New Condo Fit-Out in Aldea Zama: from developer delivery to rental-ready

Bought a new apartment in a Aldea Zama building? We take it from the developer's delivery, whether shell (obra gris) or basic finishes, to a finished, furnished and rental-ready home, with one design and one fixed price. Aldea Zama is the planned residential district between Tulum town and the beach, in the Tulum municipality: low-rise condo buildings, boutique villas and jungle lots on paved streets, with its own development rules on density, heights and design.

What we do

Design for the unit

Layout, materials, lighting and furniture plan made for your floor plan and view.

Shell to finished

Floors, walls, ceilings, kitchens, baths, closets and doors from obra gris.

Developer-finish upgrades

Replace standard finishes with stone, wood, lighting and custom carpentry.

A/C, electrical and smart

Inverter A/C, lighting scenes, smart locks and cameras ready for rentals.

Furniture and appliances

Full furnishing package delivered and installed, from beds to kitchenware.

Rental-ready set-up

Linens, photos, inventory and handover to your property manager.

Before or after delivery

The best moment to call us is before the developer delivers: we review the plans, the delivery specification and the building's fit-out manual, so changes are planned and nothing has to be demolished twice.

Snag list and developer warranty

At delivery we inspect the unit with you or for you, record defects for the developer's warranty and only then start the fit-out, so responsibilities stay clear.

Aldea Zama rules we handle

New buildings apply a fit-out manual: approved drawings, protected common areas, working hours, contractor registration and sometimes a deposit; we follow it from the first day.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
