Hotel Sustainability Retrofits: Solar, Water and Certification

Energy and water are rising costs, and guests, brands and lenders increasingly ask for measurable sustainability. On a coast with strong sun and fragile aquifers, the best retrofits pay back quickly: solar on roofs and car parks, heat recovery for hot water, efficient chillers, water reuse for irrigation and laundry, and the documentation for LEED or EDGE.

2026 costs: rooftop or carport solar $16,000–$24,000 MXN per kWp installed; heat recovery for hot water $800,000–$3M MXN; water reuse systems $1.5M–$6M MXN; certification support priced per project.

What We Implement

Solar PV

Rooftop and parking canopies sized to daytime loads, anchored for hurricanes.

Heat Recovery

Chiller heat recovered for hot water, cutting gas or diesel.

Water Efficiency

Low-flow fittings, leak detection and smart irrigation.

Water Reuse

Greywater and treated wastewater for irrigation, laundry pre-wash and toilets.

Lighting & Controls

LED retrofits and occupancy controls in rooms and public areas.

LEED / EDGE Support

Measurements, documentation and upgrades for certification.

Projects that pay back

We model energy and water use from the hotel’s bills and BMS data, rank measures by payback, and implement in that order. Solar and heat recovery often pay back in 3–6 years at Riviera Maya tariffs; water reuse protects the hotel against supply cuts and reduces municipal water costs.
