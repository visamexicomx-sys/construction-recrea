Commercial construction in Chan Chemuyil

Works, services and licensing for retail, restaurants and offices with a single contractor.

Chan Chemuyil is a residential community with thousands of residents, many of them working at Xel-Há, Akumal and the resorts of the corridor. Its commercial demand is everyday: grocery, pharmacy, food, services and small offices.

Neighbourhood retail for a working community

Residents shop where they live rather than driving to Tulum or Playa. Small units with parking at the community entrance or on the main internal streets do best. We check access and parking for the use, and plan for delivery vans on streets that are not always paved.

SEMA first, then Tulum

Even a commercial lot needs the state SEMA authorisation before the municipality of Tulum opens a licence file, starting from the vegetation on the lot. We keep the trees that help shade the frontage and the parking, which also lowers the cooling load.

Karst and rainy-season access

The ground is karst with voids, so the slab and any pits are designed after a soil study. Pours and deliveries are planned for the dry months; unpaved access can stop concrete trucks after a heavy rain.

Budget and programme for a commercial unit in Chan Chemuyil

Commercial shell: MXN 9,500 to 15,500 per m². Fit-out by use: MXN 8,500 to 20,000 per m². Moderate salt keeps the specification below the coast; access surcharges for freight and concrete pumping are included.

A 100–250 m² fit-out takes three to five months. The SEMA authorisation and the Tulum municipal licence take eight to fourteen weeks together; Protección Civil and COFEPRIS for food close at the end.

Scope: from land use to handover with the unit ready to open

We cover the full cycle: land-use and feasibility checks, executive and services design, licence filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical, plumbing, air conditioning, extraction, voice and data, finishes, fixed furniture and signage. Handover comes with as-built drawings, calculation records and the files Protección Civil asks for.

Where the use handles food, we resolve the grease trap, extraction and washing at design stage rather than as a late adjustment: it is the item that causes the most retrofits and the most expensive to correct once the unit is finished.

How we control cost, programme and opening

A line-item budget with quantities and unit rates, changes signed before execution and payments against verified progress. The programme is organised by work fronts so services, finishes and equipment do not obstruct each other, and the opening date is set once the critical filings are resolved, not when the contract is signed.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Most of our clients live outside Mexico and run the business remotely: the system is built for that.

The ground in Chan Chemuyil: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst with native vegetation
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: moderate

Large lots with mature trees: the vegetation inventory drives where the house can sit.

  • State SEMA authorisation comes before the municipal licence: without it the town hall will not open a file.
  • Moderate salt exposure: 316 stainless outdoors, 304 acceptable indoors.

Frequently asked questions

Is there demand for retail in Chan Chemuyil?

Yes, for everyday services: a stable resident population working in the nearby parks and resorts shops locally. Location near the entrance and parking matter most.

Why does a shop need SEMA authorisation?

Because the lot carries native vegetation, and in the Tulum municipality the state SEMA authorisation comes before the municipal licence for any construction.

Can you open in phases so trading starts earlier?

Yes, where the use and Protección Civil allow it. We define an operable core — access, washrooms, emergency exits and the services on that front — and execute the rest afterwards at hours that do not interfere. It has to be planned from the design stage; improvising it mid-build is expensive.

What filings go with the construction licence?

Depending on the use: land-use certificate, municipal construction licence, Protección Civil sign-off for occupancy and exits, the COFEPRIS operating notice where food is handled, and contracts or load increases with CFE and the water operator. We handle them inside the same contract.
