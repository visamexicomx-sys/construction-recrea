Dry-Stack Marina, Boat Storage & Boatyard Construction in Puerto Aventuras

Wet slips in Puerto Aventuras are scarce and boats suffer in the sun and storms. We build dry-stack marinas, boat storage buildings and boatyards for marina developers and operators: hurricane-rated rack buildings, launch ramps and forklift aprons, travel-lift pits, repair workshops and wash pads that keep paint and oil out of the water.

Quick answer: Dry-Stack Marina, Boat Storage & Boatyard Construction in Puerto Aventuras — 2026 prices: Dry-stack building, per rack slot: $200,000–$389,000 MXN; Launch ramp and forklift apron: $6.7M–$16.7M MXN; Travel-lift pit and haul-out: $8.9M–$22.2M MXN; Boatyard workshop, per m²: $10,000–$18,000 MXN.

More boats on less coast

A dry stack stores three to four times more boats per metre of shore than wet slips and protects them from sun and storms. We design racks, forklift reach and launch rhythm together so the marina can launch boats as fast as owners arrive.

Hurricanes and the water

Rack buildings face hurricane wind, and boatyards must keep paint and oil out of the sea. We design structures for the wind zone and wash pads with separators that pass environmental inspection.

Building in Puerto Aventuras

Puerto Aventuras is a gated marina community with its own HOA rules for design, works hours and access. Projects are small, salt exposure is high and costs run about 11% above Cancún.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Solidaridad; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
