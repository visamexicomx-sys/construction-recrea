House & Villa Renovation in Isla Dorada, Cancún

Many Isla Dorada houses date from the 1990s and 2000s; owners renovate them completely or rebuild around the existing structure with a new pool, terraces and dock.

Mostly single-family houses, many from the 1990s and 2000s, with boats at their own docks; the typical project is a complete renovation or a rebuild with pool, terraces and a new dock.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, gated nautical residential

Lagoon humidity, mangrove and boat use shape the details: seawall and dock maintenance, waterproofing, pool equipment rooms and corrosion-resistant metalwork.

What a Hotel Zone house usually needs

Many houses here date from the 1980s to the 2000s: solid structure, but old wiring and plumbing, single glazing and decades of salt on concrete and steel. A renovation usually combines a design update with concrete repair, new MEP and impact glazing that protect the investment for decades.

Renovating without losing the season

We phase the works around rentals and your visits, keep the site clean and secured, and close noisy stages within the hours set by the building or the residents' association.

Rules in this sector

Works need the residential's internal approval and access control for crews and trucks, a municipal licence with DRO, and a ZOFEMAT concession for docks or seawalls. Exterior changes need a municipal licence from Benito Juárez with DRO and the residents' association where there is one; anything on the lagoon shore (docks, terraces, seawalls) sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review because of the mangrove.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
