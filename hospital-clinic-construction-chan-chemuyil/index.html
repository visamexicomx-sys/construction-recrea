Clinic and medical facility construction in Chan Chemuyil

Design, construction and services with the health file resolved before opening.

Chan Chemuyil has a large resident population, many of them working at Xel-Há, Akumal and the resorts, and few health services of its own. A first-level clinic here serves families every day, not tourists once.

Primary care for a working community

Families need general practice, paediatrics, dentistry, a laboratory sample point and a pharmacy without a long trip to Tulum or Playa. Opening hours that fit shift work and a layout that handles mornings with many patients matter more than high-end finishes.

SEMA, trees and a cool building

The state SEMA authorisation comes before the Tulum municipal licence and starts from the vegetation on the lot. We keep the trees that shade the waiting areas and parking, which reduces the cooling load in a building that runs air conditioning all day.

Karst and reliable services

The ground is karst with voids, so the slab, the septic or treatment system and any cisterns are designed after a soil study. A generator with automatic transfer and water storage keep the clinic working through outages, which are common in the rainy season.

Budget and programme for a clinic in Chan Chemuyil

Clinic fit-out: MXN 17,000 to 31,000 per m². Moderate salt keeps the specification below the coast; generator, water storage and treatment are part of the figure, as are access surcharges on unpaved streets.

A 120–250 m² clinic takes five to eight months. SEMA and the Tulum licence take eight to fourteen weeks together; the COFEPRIS sanitary licence closes at the end.

Scope: from concept to the sanitary licence

We cover concept and executive design with full engineering, municipal filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical with back-up, plumbing, air conditioning and extraction, medical gases where applicable, sanitary finishes, fixed furniture and signage. We accompany the health file through to inspection, coordinating the facility’s health officer.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets and equipment manuals.

Why the order of filings decides the opening date

The municipal licence authorises building; the sanitary licence or notice authorises operating, and it is resolved on completed works with equipment installed and running. So the opening date is set when the health file closes, not when the construction contract is signed. We plan both in parallel so the second does not begin when the first ends.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Many of our clients are doctors or investors who do not live in the area and need to follow the works remotely.

  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst with native vegetation
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: moderate

Frequently asked questions

Is there demand for a clinic in Chan Chemuyil?

Yes: a large resident population with limited local health services and long trips to Tulum or Playa. Primary care, paediatrics, dentistry and a pharmacy are the core.

Which permits does a clinic need here?

The state SEMA authorisation, the Tulum municipal licence and the COFEPRIS sanitary licence, plus Protección Civil. We file them in the right order.

Do you accompany the COFEPRIS process?

We handle the technical file: drawings, calculation records, specifications for finishes and services, medical gases and biological waste handling, and we respond to inspection observations. The facility’s health officer remains the signatory, and we work coordinated with that role from concept stage.

Can a clinic be fitted out in a unit that is already leased?

Yes, and it is the most common case. What has to be verified before signing the lease is clear height, electrical capacity, the possibility of extraction, the position of drainage stacks and space for the waste store. A two-hour review before signing avoids months of trouble afterwards.
