Luxury villas in Región 15, Tulum

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Región 15 is Tulum’s investment zone with a lower entry price, where many buyers have bought lots for villas and small rental projects. It rewards careful buyers: services must be checked parcel by parcel, and the quality of the build is what separates a villa from the crowd.

Lower entry price, more homework

Land is cheaper here because services and roads are still arriving. We verify CFE supply, water and drainage for the exact parcel, and price any transformer, treatment plant or access works into the first budget rather than discovering them during construction.

Built to rent well

Many villas here are investment properties. We design for guest-proof durability — robust finishes, quiet air conditioning, a pool that is easy to maintain, secure storage for the owner — and for efficient running costs, which decide the real return.

Unpaved stretches and the rainy season

Some internal roads are unpaved. We place concrete pours and heavy deliveries in the dry months and keep a buffer in the programme for the rainy season, when access can close for days.

Región 15 sits in the urban band: karst, low salt and no committee premium, with infrastructure costs that vary by parcel. Investment specifications usually sit in the middle of the band.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Región 15, Tulum: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst under low jungle
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: internal roads, stretches unpaved
  • Development regime: no committee

Frequently asked questions

Is Región 15 a good investment zone for a villa?

It has a lower entry price, and well-built villas rent well. The return depends on confirming services for the parcel and on building for durability and low running costs.

What can go wrong with a cheap lot in Región 15?

Missing power or drainage, unclear boundaries or an access road that closes in the rain. We check all three before you buy or design.
