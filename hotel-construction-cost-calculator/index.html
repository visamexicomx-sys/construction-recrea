Boutique Hotel Construction Cost Calculator

Investors ask us one question first: what does it cost per key? The answer depends on the zone, the room size, the level of finish and the amenities that make the hotel sell. Enter your concept and get a realistic 2026 range for Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum and the rest of Quintana Roo.

How it works: rooms × room size, plus 35% for lobby, circulation and back-of-house, at the 2026 rate for the level you choose, adjusted to the zone; pool, restaurant and spa are added as packages.

What Moves the Price

Room Count & Size

Keys and room size drive built area, which drives most of the cost.

Level of Finish

From mid-range boutique to design hotel, the rate per m² can double.

Zone

Beach and island locations cost more than inland or city sites.

Pool & Amenities

Pool, restaurant and spa are what guests pay for and what budgets underestimate.

Permits

Environmental, municipal, Protección Civil and tourism registration take months; plan them early.

Utilities

Water, wastewater treatment and power capacity can be decisive outside the urban networks.

What the estimate includes

The range covers design and engineering, structure, envelope, MEP, finishes and site works for the built area, plus the amenity packages you select. It excludes land, FF&E (furniture, fixtures and equipment), permit fees, utility connections, wastewater treatment plants and VAT. We turn it into a fixed price after a site visit.
