Bases for concrete and pavers in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Inland, away from the coast, the ground is firm limestone with red soils in places, generally good for road bases, but rock outcrops and occasional sinkholes still need attention. Base thickness, joints and edge restraints are designed for this ground so slabs and pavers stay level.
