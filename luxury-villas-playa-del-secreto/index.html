Luxury Villa Construction in Playa del Secreto

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Playa del Secreto is a very low-density beachfront in Puerto Morelos, north of the town, with private homes on sand over limestone and the reef close offshore. A luxury villa here is quiet, front-row and built under rules that changed when Puerto Morelos became its own municipality in 2016.

Confirm the jurisdiction first

Puerto Morelos separated from Benito Juárez in 2016, and some older lot documents still refer to the former municipality. We confirm the current jurisdiction, cadastral record and land use before design, so the licence is filed with the right authority the first time.

Very low density, very private

Few neighbours and a long beach make privacy the main asset. We design the villa to open fully to the sea while screening it from the access road, and keep beach-facing lighting shielded for the turtle season.

Front-row specification

Sand over limestone, a very high water table and severe salt call for a raised floor, raft or reinforced footings on a compacted platform, 316 stainless, sealed glazing and a pool anchored against uplift. Anything in the federal strip stays demountable.

Playa del Secreto sits with the front-row coast: severe salt, a very high water table and federal frontage, with very low density. The environmental file and jurisdiction check come first.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Playa del Secreto: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Puerto Morelos
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession)
  • Ground: sand over limestone
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: road off the federal highway
  • Development regime: very low density residential

Frequently asked questions

Why check the municipality for a lot in Playa del Secreto?

Because Puerto Morelos became independent from Benito Juárez in 2016, and older documents may still name Cancún. The licence must be filed with Puerto Morelos.

Is Playa del Secreto quiet?

Yes: it is one of the lowest-density beachfronts on the coast, with private homes rather than hotels.
