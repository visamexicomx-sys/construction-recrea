House & Villa Renovation in Pok-Ta-Pok, Cancún

Pok-Ta-Pok houses from the 1970s–1990s sit on generous golf and lagoon lots; we reposition them keeping the structure: new MEP, glazing, terraces and finishes.

Houses from the 1970s–1990s on generous lots and small condo buildings with lagoon views; many have been updated piecemeal and now need a complete renovation of MEP, glazing and finishes.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, golf island

The lagoon side is sheltered from waves but humid, with mangrove, mosquitoes and fluctuating water levels; docks, seawalls and gardens need lagoon-specific detailing.

What a Hotel Zone house usually needs

Many houses here date from the 1980s to the 2000s: solid structure, but old wiring and plumbing, single glazing and decades of salt on concrete and steel. A renovation usually combines a design update with concrete repair, new MEP and impact glazing that protect the investment for decades.

Renovating without losing the season

We phase the works around rentals and your visits, keep the site clean and secured, and close noisy stages within the hours set by the building or the residents' association.

Rules in this sector

Houses follow the residential association's rules and a Benito Juárez licence with DRO for exterior or structural works; anything on the shore sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review. Exterior changes need a municipal licence from Benito Juárez with DRO and the residents' association where there is one; anything on the lagoon shore (docks, terraces, seawalls) sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review because of the mangrove.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
