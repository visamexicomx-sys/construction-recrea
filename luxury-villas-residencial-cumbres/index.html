Luxury villas in Residencial Cumbres, Cancún

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Residencial Cumbres is where established Cancún families build their best house: a gated community with schools and shops nearby, good ground and neighbours who care about standards. A luxury villa here is a modern family home with premium finishes, smart systems and a private garden.

Modern architecture within the rulebook

The community’s rulebook sets colours, heights and fences, but leaves room for contemporary design. We work with flat roofs, large glazing and stone or wood accents that pass the administration’s review without compromise.

Smart, quiet and efficient

Premium here means comfort every day: home automation, whole-house water filtration, quiet inverter air conditioning, acoustic insulation between bedrooms and solar panels sized for real consumption.

Replacing an older house

Many luxury projects replace a 1990s house on a good lot. We plan demolition carefully, reuse what is sound where it makes sense and keep the neighbours on side throughout.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build a modern villa within the Residencial Cumbres rules?

Yes. The rulebook controls colours, heights and fences, not style. We design contemporary villas that pass the administration’s review.

Is it worth demolishing an older house in Residencial Cumbres?

Often, when the lot is good and the old structure limits the plan. We assess both options before you decide.
