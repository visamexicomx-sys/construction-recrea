How Much Does It Cost to Build an Apartment Building in Mexico? (2026)

Construction cost of apartment and condo buildings in Quintana Roo in 2026 — per m² and per unit by tier, worked budgets from 4 to 60 units, the soft costs developers forget and the price in ten towns. Figures from our own quotes.

Quick answer: How Much Does It Cost to Build an Apartment Building in Mexico? (2026) — prices: 4 units: $12M–$20M MXN; 6–8 units: $22M–$38M MXN; 12–16 units with amenities: $45M–$70M MXN; 60-unit tower: $216M–$342M MXN.
2026, Cancún base: standard low-rise $16,000–$24,000 MXN per m²; premium with lift and rooftop $24,000–$34,000; tower $24,000–$38,000; luxury beachfront $35,000–$55,000. A 6–8 unit building $22M–$38M MXN.

What We Deliver

Gross vs Saleable

Saleable area is 75–85% of gross; price per unit includes common areas.

Parking

Surface $25,000–$70,000 per space; structured $180,000–$400,000.

Lifts

Above 3–4 storeys a lift and fire protection become mandatory.

Rooftop

Pools and decks add structure and waterproofing cost.

Town Factor

Islands and remote towns add 9–30% for logistics.

Soft Costs

Design, permits, regime and contingency: 13–21%.

Soft costs on top of construction

  • Design and engineering: 5–8% of construction.
  • Permits, DRO and impact studies: 2–4%.
  • Condominium regime and notary: 0.5–1%.
  • Utility connections (CFE, water): priced per project, often $0.5M–$3M.
  • Contingency: 5–8%.
  • Land and VAT: separate.
