Scheduling facade works on Isla Mujeres

Day-trippers and long weekends fill the island; small hotels close for a few weeks in autumn for maintenance. Exterior works are phased by room stack inside these windows, with agreed noise hours and photo reports for owners and insurers.

Access and staging on Isla Mujeres

Materials and equipment cross by car ferry from Punta Sam; island streets are narrow, so we plan small trucks, golf-cart logistics and on-site storage. Suspended platforms, scaffolding and material storage are planned to fit these conditions without blocking guest areas.

Water, power and staff on Isla Mujeres

Water arrives from the mainland and power through a submarine cable, so storage tanks, back-up power and efficient systems are standard requests.
