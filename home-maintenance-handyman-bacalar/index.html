Home Maintenance in Bacalar: preventive plans, repairs and hurricane prep

We maintain houses, condos and villas in Bacalar with one team for repairs, scheduled preventive visits and a photo report after each one. Tourism peaks on holidays and weekends, so hotels schedule works in quieter weeks.

Bacalar, 2026 references: Handyman visit: $630–$1,200; House or condo plan: $3,400–$7,300/month; Villa with pool and garden: $7,300–$14,600/month.

What Our Maintenance Covers

Repairs & Handyman

Doors, locks, fixtures, sealant, small carpentry and everything that wears out.

Plumbing & Leaks

Leaks, pressure pumps, water heaters, cisterns, filters and softeners.

Roof & Facade

Waterproofing inspection, crack repair, paint touch-ups and rust treatment.

What wears out first in Bacalar

There is no sea salt, but the lagoon keeps humidity high and the sun is strong, so wood, decks and paint need UV and moisture protection. Our checklist puts these problems first: rust, sealant, roof membrane, AC drains and exterior finishes.

Water systems to keep running in Bacalar

Water is from the municipal system or wells, and wastewater must never reach the lagoon, which affects every bathroom and kitchen project. Pumps, cisterns, filters and softeners are checked on every visit, because when they fail the property stops working.

Homes and owners in Bacalar

Bacalar has lagoon-front houses, boutique hotels and eco-cabins along the Laguna de los Siete Colores, plus village homes in town, with demand for bathrooms, decks, gardens and maintenance.

Rules, licences and administration in Bacalar

Licences come from Bacalar municipality, and the lagoon shore is protected, so works near the water, landscaping and wastewater must respect environmental rules.

Materials, crews and access in Bacalar

Bacalar is about three and a half hours from our workshops; we plan stays on site and buy many materials in Chetumal, which keeps costs close to the southern average.
