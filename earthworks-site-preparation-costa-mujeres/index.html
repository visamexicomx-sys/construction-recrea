Earthworks & Site Prep in Costa Mujeres: clearing, rock excavation and compacted fills

Every project in Costa Mujeres starts with the platform: clearing, rock, fills and levels done right decide whether floors crack, pools leak and roads sink. The corridor is low-lying sand and coastal soils over limestone, with wetlands inland; roads here need geotextiles or thicker granular bases, and rock is often deeper than further south.

Costa Mujeres, 2026 references: Clearing and stripping: $20–$50 / m²; Excavation in limestone: $340–$780 / m³; Compacted sascab fill: $380–$620 / m³.

What We Do

Clearing & Stripping

Removal of jungle and organic soil, preserving trees marked for protection and chipping vegetation on site.

Compacted Fills & Platforms

Sascab and crushed stone in controlled layers, compacted and lab-tested, for buildings and roads.

Levels & Drainage

Grading so rain moves away from buildings, toward planned infiltration points.

Clearing permits in Costa Mujeres

Works fall under Isla Mujeres municipality on the mainland, and most projects sit inside master plans with their own environmental conditions for mangroves, dunes and turtle-nesting beaches, which restrict lighting and works near the coast. We confirm these authorisations before any machine enters the site.

Levels and runoff in Costa Mujeres

Flat ground and a high water table make drainage the main design issue; grades, swales and retention areas replace deep infiltration wells, which can fail when groundwater is close to the surface. Platforms are graded so that rain moves away from buildings toward planned infiltration points.

What gets built in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is a new resort corridor: long hotel access roads, large car parks, service roads and the infrastructure of master-planned residential and golf developments, much of it built from scratch on undeveloped land.

Plants, materials and access in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is about 30 minutes from Cancún’s plants and quarries, but long single access roads and resort security gates control truck movements and set delivery hours.
