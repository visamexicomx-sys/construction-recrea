Villa and hotel construction in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Small hotels, guest-houses and eco-lodges in the Maya zone: title check, SEMA and municipal filings, construction and an operable handover.

Felipe Carrillo Puerto has never had beach tourism, and that is its opening: Tren Maya passengers, travellers on the 307 between Tulum and Felipe Carrillo Puerto, visitors to Sian Ka’an and the Maya communities all need somewhere to sleep that is not a resort.

Who stays here

Business travellers and government visitors fill rooms during the week; cultural and nature tourists, cyclists and Tren Maya passengers on weekends and in season. A small hotel with parking, good air conditioning and a restaurant works for both; an eco-lodge on the edge of town sells the forest and the cenotes.

Title before design

Hotel lots must have full ownership and a registered title before anything is designed, because much of the land around the town is ejido. We check the file with your notary, then the SEMA authorisation and the municipal licence, in that order.

Cool without the sea breeze

Inland heat is the operating cost that decides a hotel’s margin. Orientation to the east wind, shaded corridors, high ceilings, insulated roofs and efficient split systems keep rooms comfortable and the electricity bill reasonable.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

For a small hotel or eco-lodge in Felipe Carrillo Puerto we work in a range of MXN 13,500 to 25,000 per built m² (roughly USD 735 – 1,360/m²), the lowest in the state for hospitality, thanks to local crews and low salt exposure.

A ten to thirty key property takes ten to fourteen months of construction. Title check, SEMA and the municipal licence come first; sanitary filings and Protección Civil close at the end, on completed works.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Felipe Carrillo Puerto: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Felipe Carrillo Puerto
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (state) + municipal; CONANP inside Sian Ka’an
  • Ground: karst limestone with thin red soil
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low

Much of the land is ejido: before buying, confirm full ownership (dominio pleno) and a registered title. The eastern part of the municipality lies in the Sian Ka’an biosphere reserve.

  • Ejido land: confirm dominio pleno and a title registered in the Public Property Registry before paying anything; a transfer of ejido rights is not a deed.
  • Inland heat without sea breeze: orientation, deep eaves, cross-ventilation and roof insulation do more than extra air-conditioning tonnage.

Frequently asked questions

Is there hotel demand in Felipe Carrillo Puerto?

Yes, and it is growing: business and government travel during the week, plus Tren Maya passengers, travellers between Tulum and Felipe Carrillo Puerto and visitors to Sian Ka’an and the Maya communities.

Can I build an eco-lodge near Sian Ka’an?

Inside the reserve, only with CONANP authorisation and under its management programme; outside it, the state SEMA authorisation and the municipal licence apply. We check which regime covers the lot first.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
