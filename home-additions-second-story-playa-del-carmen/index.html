Adding On or Building a Second Story: What to Know

Adding on is one of the best investments — more square meters and value without buying another lot. But a second story or structural addition isn’t improvised: you must verify that the existing foundation and structure can carry the new weight, especially on the Riviera Maya’s karst soil. A serious structural calculation and, if needed, reinforcement, prevent cracks and settling. Every structural addition requires a license, DRO and (in Tulum) SEMA — interior finishes do not. We give a fixed line-item budget and coordinate calculation, permits and construction — ideal for owners who do not live here.
