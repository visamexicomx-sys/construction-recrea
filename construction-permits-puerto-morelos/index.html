The permit file in Puerto Morelos: what actually goes in

There is no single procedure for the Riviera Maya. In Puerto Morelos the competent authority is the Puerto Morelos municipality, the environmental regime is SEMARNAT (reef national park) + ZOFEMAT, and that decides which documents enter the file and in what order.

Near the coast the water table is high and front-row salt exposure is severe. Concrete cover, 316 steel and pool anchoring are justified in the calculations attached to the licence.

The file for Puerto Morelos, in order

  1. Land-use certificate (Puerto Morelos municipality)
  2. Federal Environmental Impact Statement — SEMARNAT
  3. ZOFEMAT concession for the federal maritime-land strip
  4. Construction licence with a registered DRO (responsible building official)
  5. Water and drainage feasibility and contract (CAPA/CONAGUA as applicable)
  6. CFE electrical feasibility and service connection
  7. Notice of completion and certificate of occupancy

What shapes the process in Puerto Morelos

The ground is limestone, mangrove to the west and the water table is high near the coast, which weighs on the soil study that accompanies the licence application. Access is Highway 307 and village streets and the development regime is its own municipality since 2016.

  • Federal SEMARNAT filing: the environmental resolution is the project’s critical path. Budget 4–8 months before the first excavation.
  • The federal maritime-land strip is concessioned and paid for annually; it does not travel with the deed. The survey and the application are filed separately and carry their own response time.
  • High water table: the foundation sits higher and the pool needs anchoring against uplift.
  • Severe salt exposure: 316 stainless throughout the hardware, concrete cover above the code minimum and six-monthly maintenance. 304 pits within two years at this distance from the sea.
  • Mangrove is protected under federal law, so the real buildable footprint can end up well below the deeded area.
