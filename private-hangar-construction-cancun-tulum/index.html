Private and Business Aviation Hangars in Cancún and Tulum

Private jets and turboprops bring villa owners, resort guests and charter clients straight to the coast. They need a hangar against sun, salt and hurricanes, an apron and often an FBO lounge. Hangars are built on airport land, under a contract with the airport operator; we build them to the operator’s standards for the tenant who holds that contract.

Quick answer: It is priced per project: the steel shell is in the range of an industrial building, $6,500–$12,000 MXN/m², plus the hangar door, the apron, fire protection and the operator’s requirements.

What We Build

Steel Hangars

Clear-span steel frames sized to the aircraft wingspan and tail height.

Hangar Doors

Sliding, bi-fold or hydraulic doors, designed for hurricane wind.

Aprons & Taxiways

Concrete aprons and links to the airport’s taxiway, to the operator’s spec.

FBO Lounges

Passenger lounge, crew rest, offices and customs-ready areas.

Maintenance Shops

Workshops, parts stores and compressed air.

Fire Protection

Detection and suppression per the operator and civil protection.

Working inside the airport

Inside the fence everything goes through the operator: the lease, the location, the design approval, the access of workers and trucks to the airside, and the schedule. We plan the works around those rules, with badges, escorted access and night windows when needed, so the airport keeps operating.

Why the coast is hard on hangars

Salt air corrodes steel and door mechanisms, the sun bakes the roof and hurricanes load the big door openings. We specify galvanised and painted steel, insulated roofs, and doors and frames calculated for the local design wind.
