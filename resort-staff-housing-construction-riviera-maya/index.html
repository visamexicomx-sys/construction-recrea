Staff Housing and Employee Facilities for Hotels and Resorts

Resorts in Costa Mujeres, Tulum and the coastal corridor compete for staff as hard as for guests. Long commutes and expensive rents drive turnover. Good staff housing, canteens and locker rooms help hotels hire and keep teams. We build them efficiently, close to the property, to last.

2026 costs: staff housing blocks $9,000–$14,000 MXN/m², roughly $150,000–$280,000 MXN per bed depending on room type; staff canteens $14,000–$22,000 MXN/m²; locker rooms and staff areas $10,000–$18,000 MXN/m².

What We Build for Hotel Teams

Staff Housing

Shared rooms, studios and family units with durable, low-maintenance finishes.

Staff Canteens

Kitchens and dining rooms sized for shift changes.

Locker Rooms & Staff Areas

Changing rooms, showers, uniforms and training rooms.

Transport Hubs

Shaded bus stops, parking and bicycle storage.

Efficient Systems

Solar hot water, efficient A/C and water reuse to keep running costs low.

Community Spaces

Shade, sports courts and green areas that make staff housing liveable.

Efficient and durable

Staff housing must cost little to build and less to run. We use repetitive modules, steel or concrete frames suited to the site, durable finishes and solar hot water, and plan maintenance access from the start. Where land allows, we phase blocks so the first residents move in while the rest is built.
