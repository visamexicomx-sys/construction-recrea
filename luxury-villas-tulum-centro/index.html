Luxury villas in Tulum Centro

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Central Tulum is the town itself: shops, restaurants and the main avenue, with urban lots, party walls and live street logistics. A luxury property here is usually a townhouse villa or a boutique residence that brings the jungle inside its walls.

Building between party walls

Urban lots share walls with neighbours, so foundations, waterproofing and drainage must protect both sides. We survey neighbouring structures before excavation, document their condition and design the new walls so that rain and noise stay on our side.

A garden inside the walls

With no views to borrow, the villa creates its own: a courtyard garden, a plunge pool, a roof terrace with planting and shade. We work the section so that light and air reach every room and the street noise stays outside.

Working in a busy town

Deliveries, concrete pumping and cranes happen on a street with traffic and pedestrians. We plan them with the municipality and neighbours, keep the site hoarding clean and work within permitted hours.

Tulum Centro sits in the urban band: karst, low salt and no committee premium. Party-wall works, protection of neighbours and restricted logistics can add to the figure.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build a luxury villa in central Tulum?

Yes, usually as a courtyard townhouse or small residence on an urban lot. The value lies in location, and the design creates privacy and greenery inside its walls.

What is different about building between party walls?

Neighbouring structures must be surveyed and protected, and foundations and waterproofing designed for both sides. We document neighbours’ condition before excavation.
