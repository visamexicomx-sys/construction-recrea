Villa Renovation in Playa Mujeres

The first Playa Mujeres villas are now old enough to reposition: new glazing towards the golf or canal, updated MEP, bigger terraces and outdoor kitchens.

New villas on golf and canal lots, lots still waiting for construction, and condo buildings with golf or sea views. Clients are mostly North American and Mexico City owners building a second home or a rental villa.

The setting: Gated community: golf, marina and canals

Golf and canal lots are sheltered from the surf but humid and close to the water table; foundations, pool shells and docks are designed for fill soils and salt.

What a Costa Mujeres villa usually needs

Many villas here were built in the 2010s with good structure but finishes, glazing and A/C that now look dated next to new towers. Salt air attacks aluminium, metal and sealants, so a renovation usually combines a design update with envelope work that protects the investment for the next decade.

Renovating without losing the season

We phase the works around rentals and your visits, keep the site clean and secured inside the community, and close noisy stages within the allowed working hours.

Rules in this sector

The community's architectural review (setbacks, heights, façades, roofs, colours) comes before the Isla Mujeres municipal licence with DRO; crews and trucks are registered with security, and docks follow the marina's rules. Exterior changes (facade, colours, terraces, pool) normally need the community's architectural approval and a municipal licence with DRO; interior works need notice to security and registered crews.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
