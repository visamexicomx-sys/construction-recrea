Drywall & Ceilings on Cozumel: partitions, false ceilings and LED coves

We build drywall partitions, false ceilings and lighting coves on Cozumel with moisture-resistant boards and galvanised framing made for the coast. Cozumel has family homes in San Miguel, waterfront condos and villas along the west coast and many dive-tourism rentals, with steady demand for bathrooms, floors, paint and storm repairs.

Cozumel, 2026 references: Standard partition: $790–$1,200; Moisture-resistant partition: $950–$1,300; Flat false ceiling: $590–$920.

What We Build in Drywall

Partitions

New rooms, closets and office divisions with acoustic insulation and reinforcement for TVs and cabinets.

Wet & Exterior Areas

Green board and cement board for bathrooms, kitchens, terraces and soffits.

Acoustic Ceilings

Mineral fibre and perforated boards for offices, restaurants and home cinemas.

Where drywall makes sense on Cozumel

The island takes salt and wind from every side and has been hit by strong hurricanes, so metal, windows, paint and garden structures need marine-grade materials and hurricane fixing. It is the fastest way to add rooms, hide AC ducts and build ceiling details in this kind of property.

Moisture-resistant systems on Cozumel

Works are licensed by Cozumel municipality; the coast is under ZOFEMAT and parts of the island are protected, and condo buildings have their own administration rules. Board type, framing and ventilation are chosen for this humidity so ceilings do not sag or stain.

Materials, crews and access on Cozumel

Crews and materials cross on the car ferry from Playa del Carmen; we load full trucks, plan stays of several days and keep the most-used materials in stock on site.

Rentals and scheduling on Cozumel

Dive and cruise tourism keeps rentals busy all year, so we usually renovate one unit or room at a time.

Water supply on Cozumel

Water on the island is hard and supply can be irregular, so cisterns, softeners and filters are standard and fittings must tolerate scale.
