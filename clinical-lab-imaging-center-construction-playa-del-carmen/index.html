Clinical Laboratory & Diagnostic Imaging Centre Construction in Playa del Carmen

Lab chains and imaging groups are opening branches across Playa del Carmen to keep up with residents, medical tourism and hospitals that outsource diagnostics. We build clinical laboratories, diagnostic imaging centres and collection branches: lab fit-outs with controlled HVAC, MRI rooms with RF shielding, CT and X-ray rooms shielded to regulation and the documents for the sanitary licence.

Equipment drives the room

MRI, CT and lab analysers each come with strict site requirements: slab loads, vibration, shielding, cooling, power quality and rigging paths. We work from the vendor's site planning guide from day one, so the room passes the vendor's acceptance and the equipment goes in on the date booked.

Shielding and licences

Radiation rooms need a shielding study and registration; labs need biosafety, waste handling and documented flows. We include the studies, build to them and deliver the file for the sanitary licence.

Building in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the second market and the busiest for small and mid-size condo buildings: downtown near Quinta Avenida, Playacar, Centro Maya, the north and the Corasol corridor. Lots are narrow, municipal height limits are strict, and buyers are largely foreign investors who expect rooftop amenities and rental-ready units.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Solidaridad; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
