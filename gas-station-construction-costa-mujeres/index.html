Gas station construction and refurbishment in Costa Mujeres

Double-wall tanks, forecourt, canopy and the CRE-ASEA file with a single contractor.

Costa Mujeres is growing faster than its services, and fuel is one of them: resort fleets, staff commuting along the coast road, new residents and traffic towards the Punta Sam ferry all need stations that are not yet there.

Growth along the coast road

Every new resort and development adds service vehicles, staff transport and residents’ cars along the road north of Cancún. The best sites are junctions with good visibility and access for tankers; we check traffic, access and the road junction before any layout.

Tanks in a high water table

The ground is dune sand over limestone with water close to the surface. Tank excavations need dewatering and shoring, tanks are anchored against buoyancy, and containment and drainage are designed so that a heavy storm does not flood the forecourt or reach the mangrove.

Licences from Isla Mujeres and the federation

Land use and construction are licensed by the municipality of Isla Mujeres on its continental side; the CRE permit and ASEA authorisation are federal. Inside or next to a development, its guidelines on image and access also apply. We run all of these files in parallel.

Budget and programme for a station in Costa Mujeres

2026 reference: a new two- to three-island station with double-wall tanks, dispensers, canopy, store and forecourt works, between MXN 13 and 26 million in construction and installation, excluding land and brand rights. Dewatering, anchoring and the coastal specification add to a mainland figure.

A realistic total programme is nine to fourteen months. The federal permits set the pace; construction and installation take four to six months once they are granted.

Scope: from the permit to a station dispensing fuel

We cover concept and executive design with services engineering, calculation records and specifications for the ASEA file, integration of the CRE retail permit, land use and the municipal licence, Protección Civil sign-off, management of the highway junction, civil works, tanks and lines, dispensers, canopy, convenience store, forecourt works and commissioning tests.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, calculation records, tightness certificates and equipment manuals.

Why the calendar is built from the filings

The civil works of a station are predictable: four to eight months depending on size. What is not predictable are the CRE permit, the ASEA authorisation and the highway junction, which run in months and do not depend on the contractor. So we start the file before the final executive design and run the three fronts in parallel rather than chaining them.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. We work with investors and operators who do not live in the area and need to follow both works and filings remotely.

The ground in Costa Mujeres: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Isla Mujeres (continental zone)
  • Environmental regime: development guidelines + municipal + ZOFEMAT/NOM-022 where applicable
  • Ground: dune sand over limestone
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A fast-growing resort coast north of Cancún: development rules on top of municipal ones, and full marine exposure.

  • Ask the developer for the architectural guidelines and the list of approved contractors before you commission a design.
  • Allow for the development’s review committee in the programme and file it in parallel with the municipal licence.

Frequently asked questions

Is there demand for a new station in Costa Mujeres?

Yes: resorts, developments and residents are growing faster than the services along the coast road. A visible junction with easy tanker access is the key to a good site.

What does a high water table change for a station?

Excavations need dewatering and shoring, tanks must be anchored against buoyancy and containment must be designed for heavy rain. We include all of it in the design and the budget.

Do you only do the works, or the permit file too?

Both, and in practice they are inseparable: the drawings, calculation records and specifications the ASEA file requires are the same ones that govern the works. We assemble the file, respond to observations and coordinate the CRE retail permit with its holder.

What tests are handed over at the end?

Tightness certificates for tanks and lines, verification of the interstitial monitoring system, dispenser tests, as-built drawings, calculation records and manuals. Without that package the station cannot demonstrate compliance, and it is the first thing asked for in any later review.
