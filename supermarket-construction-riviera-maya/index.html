Supermarket & Convenience Store Construction in the Riviera Maya

For chains, franchisees and independent grocers opening in the Riviera Maya. We build the building, the refrigeration and the services that keep a supermarket selling: insulated cold rooms, refrigerated display cases, loading docks, backup power that protects the cold chain and parking that meets the municipality’s ratio — to the chain’s standards and opening date.

What We Deliver

Cold Chain

Cold rooms, freezers and display cases with remote condensing units.

Backup Power

Generator and transfer switch sized to keep refrigeration running.

Loading & Back of House

Docks, receiving, storage and staff areas planned for deliveries.

Floors

Industrial slabs and finishes for carts, pallets and cleaning.

Parking & Access

Parking ratio, entries and exits approved by the municipality.

Chain Standards

We build to the brand’s manual: we have built for 7-Eleven and GoMart.

Refrigeration and power decide the budget

A supermarket is a refrigerated warehouse that sells. Cold rooms, cases and condensing units can cost as much as the building, and a power cut can spoil the stock in hours, so the electrical service, generator and transfer switch are sized for the refrigeration first. In this heat, roof insulation and condensing units in the shade pay back every month.

Permits and opening

Land use for commerce, construction licence with DRO, Civil Protection, environmental and sanitary requirements, CFE capacity and parking ratio. We coordinate with the chain’s project manager so fixtures, IT and stocking start on the agreed date.
