Pool Construction in Isla Dorada, Cancún

Isla Dorada pools sit between the house and the dock; we design them for the water table and the lagoon edge, often with an infinity edge facing the water.

Mostly single-family houses, many from the 1990s and 2000s, with boats at their own docks; the typical project is a complete renovation or a rebuild with pool, terraces and a new dock.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, gated nautical residential

Lagoon humidity, mangrove and boat use shape the details: seawall and dock maintenance, waterproofing, pool equipment rooms and corrosion-resistant metalwork.

Groundwater and soil

The water table in Cancún is shallow and the ground is sand over limestone, so shells are designed against uplift and drained correctly during construction.

Equipment that survives salt

Pumps, heaters and fittings are placed in ventilated rooms and specified for coastal air; salt chlorination is set up correctly to avoid corrosion.

Rules in this sector

Works need the residential's internal approval and access control for crews and trucks, a municipal licence with DRO, and a ZOFEMAT concession for docks or seawalls. New pools need a municipal licence; rooftop pools also need the tower administration and structural calculations, and lagoon-front pools must stay out of the federal zone.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
