Home renovation in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Felipe Carrillo Puerto’s houses are solid, often older concrete homes in a town that is growing again with the Tren Maya. Renovation here means modernising family houses, adding floors and rooms, and making homes cooler in an inland climate without sea breeze.

Cooler houses without the sea breeze

Many older houses were built with flat concrete roofs and small windows that trap heat. We insulate or add a ventilated second skin to the roof, open cross-ventilation, add shaded verandas and upgrade to efficient air conditioning, which lowers bills more than any finish.

Adding floors and rooms

Families grow, and many houses gain a second floor or rental rooms. We check that the existing structure can carry the new load, investigate the karst under any new foundation and design extensions that work with the house rather than on top of it.

Title and permits

For extensions and structural changes, the municipality asks for a clean title; on former ejido land, full ownership must be registered first. We check the paperwork with your notary before the licence is filed.

Realistic budget and programme in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

A full renovation in Felipe Carrillo Puerto runs at MXN 5,000 to 11,000 per m² renovated (roughly USD 270 – 600/m²), the lowest we quote, thanks to local crews and low salt; extensions are priced like new construction for the added area.

A typical renovation takes two to four months. Structural changes and extensions need the Felipe Carrillo Puerto municipal licence.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Felipe Carrillo Puerto: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Felipe Carrillo Puerto
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (state) + municipal; CONANP inside Sian Ka’an
  • Ground: karst limestone with thin red soil
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: highway 307 and the junction with 184 and 295; Tren Maya station
  • Development regime: inland town on largely ejido land: confirm full ownership

Much of the land is ejido: before buying, confirm full ownership (dominio pleno) and a registered title. The eastern part of the municipality lies in the Sian Ka’an biosphere reserve.

Frequently asked questions

How can I make my house in Felipe Carrillo Puerto cooler?

Insulate or ventilate the roof slab, open cross-ventilation, add shaded verandas and install efficient inverter air conditioning. The roof makes the biggest difference.

Do I need a clean title to extend my house?

For a municipal licence, yes. On former ejido land, full ownership (dominio pleno) must be registered first; we check the documents before filing.

Do I need a permit to renovate?

Repairs and finishes usually need only a notice or a minor-works permit; structural changes, extensions and façade changes need a municipal licence, and condominiums need the administration’s approval. We confirm which applies before quoting.

What warranty do you give on a renovation?

One year on finishes and installations and five years on structural repairs and waterproofing we execute, with photographs of hidden work and manuals for new equipment.
