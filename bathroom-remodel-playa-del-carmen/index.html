Bathroom Remodeling in Playa del Carmen: Showers That Don’t Leak, Walls That Don’t Grow Mould

In the Caribbean a bathroom fails for two reasons: water getting behind the tile and humidity with nowhere to go. We strip the room to the slab, rebuild the plumbing, waterproof the whole wet zone, slope the floor to the drain, add real extraction and then finish with the tile, glass and fittings you choose. One crew, one fixed quote, usually done in two to three weeks.

Quick answer: In 2026 a full remodel of a typical 4–6 m² bathroom costs $95,000–$180,000 MXN including demolition, new plumbing, waterproofing, porcelain tile, a walk-in shower and glass. A simple refresh without demolition starts around $35,000 MXN.
2026 prices: refresh (fixtures, vanity, paint, no demolition) $35,000–$70,000 MXN; full remodel of a 4–6 m² bathroom including demolition, new plumbing, waterproofing, porcelain tile, walk-in shower and glass $95,000–$180,000 MXN; premium master bath with large-format stone, double vanity and freestanding tub $220,000–$450,000 MXN.

What a Recrea Bathroom Includes

Full Waterproofing

Cementitious or liquid membrane over the whole shower and floor, flood-tested for 24 hours before any tile goes down.

New Plumbing

PPR or PEX supply lines, proper traps and venting, and pressure-tested pipes behind every wall we close.

Tile & Stone

Porcelain, large-format slabs, chukum or natural stone, laid flat with levelling systems and epoxy grout in wet zones.

Walk-In Showers

Curbless showers with linear drains, tempered glass panels and niches built into the wall.

Ventilation

Ducted extractors sized for the room, because an open window is not enough in 85% humidity.

Vanities & Lighting

Custom vanities from our carpentry shop, mirrors with LED lighting and fittings rated for hard water.

Why bathrooms on this coast need a different spec

Water here is hard and full of calcium, the air carries salt, and the walls are concrete block that soaks up moisture. That is why cheap fittings scale up in months, grout turns black and paint blisters above the shower. We specify fittings with ceramic cartridges, epoxy or high-performance grout in the wet zone, anti-fungal paint and a proper exhaust duct, and we install a water softener or filter when the house does not have one. The result is a bathroom that still looks new after five rental seasons.

How the work runs, step by step

  • Visit and design: measurements, layout options and a materials board; 3D views for full remodels.
  • Demolition of tile, fixtures and screeds, with debris taken away the same day.
  • Rough-in: new supply and drain lines, electrical points, pressure test.
  • Waterproofing and screed with correct falls to the drain, then a 24-hour flood test.
  • Finishes: tile, glass, vanity, fittings, silicone and final clean, with a written warranty.
