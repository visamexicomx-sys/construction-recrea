Kitchen Remodel in Isla Dorada, Cancún

Isla Dorada houses get chef's kitchens and an outdoor kitchen by the dock, with stone and hardware specified for humidity.

Mostly single-family houses, many from the 1990s and 2000s, with boats at their own docks; the typical project is a complete renovation or a rebuild with pool, terraces and a new dock.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, gated nautical residential

Lagoon humidity, mangrove and boat use shape the details: seawall and dock maintenance, waterproofing, pool equipment rooms and corrosion-resistant metalwork.

Cabinets that survive coastal humidity

Standard melamine swells near the sea; we use moisture-resistant boards, sealed edges, stainless hinges and ventilation behind appliances so doors stay straight.

Condo or villa

In towers we work within the building's hours and plumbing risers; in villas we can move walls, add islands and outdoor kitchens.

Rules in this sector

Works need the residential's internal approval and access control for crews and trucks, a municipal licence with DRO, and a ZOFEMAT concession for docks or seawalls. In towers, kitchen works need the administration's approval, especially for gas lines, extraction and plumbing changes.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
