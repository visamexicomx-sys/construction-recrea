Pool Construction in Selvamar: villa pools, infinity edges and rooftop plunge pools

We build and renovate pools for Selvamar villas and penthouses: reinforced concrete shells, infinity edges, rooftop plunge pools, efficient equipment, lighting and decks, with the community's approval. Selvamar is a residential community in Playa del Carmen built around protected green areas, in the Solidaridad municipality: house and villa lots between preserved jungle strips, with a development rulebook and a management that reviews setbacks and trees.

What we do

Villa pools

Concrete shells, steps, benches and sun shelves.

Infinity edges

Pools between the garden and the preserved jungle.

Rooftop plunge pools

Structural review, waterproofing and equipment on the roof.

Equipment

Variable-speed pumps, salt chlorination, heat pumps and automation.

Renovation

Resurfacing, new tile, leaks repaired and equipment replaced.

Decks

Non-slip stone or porcelain decks that stay cool.

Groundwater and soil

The water table in Playa del Carmen is shallow and the ground is sand over limestone, so shells are designed against uplift and drained correctly during construction.

Equipment that survives salt

Pumps, heaters and fittings are placed in ventilated rooms and specified for coastal air; salt chlorination is set up correctly to avoid corrosion.

Selvamar rules we handle

New pools and changes to the exterior need the community's approval and a municipal licence; rooftop pools also need the building administration and structural calculations.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
