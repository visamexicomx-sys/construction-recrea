Building in Playacar, Playa del Carmen: villas, condos and renovation, one contract

Playacar is the gated community on the south side of Playa del Carmen, in the Solidaridad municipality: Phase 1 by the beach with hotels and beach villas, Phase 2 around the Playacar golf course with residential streets and condos, with its own residents’ association and construction rules. Owners here build new villas, finish condos delivered by developers and renovate homes for rental, usually from abroad. We handle the design review, the licence and the works under one fixed-price contract.

Quick answer: A new premium villa costs about $30,000–$40,000 MXN per m² of construction and an ultra-luxury villa $40,000–$50,000+ MXN per m². A 350 m² villa is roughly $10.5–17.5 million MXN before land and fees.

Playacar rules we handle for you

  • Design review first. Playacar reviews every project against its own architectural rules (heights, setbacks, materials, colours, roofs) before the municipality sees it; we draw to those rules from day one.
  • Solidaridad licence. Land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings, plus Protección Civil and utility feasibility, filed and followed by us.
  • Works rules. Access is controlled: crews, trucks and deliveries are registered, works hours are fixed, and deposits and insurance certificates are required; our crews are registered and insured (IMSS).
  • Two phases, two sets of rules. Beach-side Phase 1 and golf-side Phase 2 have different lot sizes, heights and association rules; we confirm which apply to your lot before drawing.
  • Environment. Vegetation removal, protected areas and turtle-safe lighting near the beach follow municipal and environmental rules; we file the papers before works start.

Rules differ by lot and change over time; we confirm them with the Playacar administration before quoting.
