Eco-Lodges & Retreats in Tulum: What to Know

Tulum is the wellness-tourism capital of the Americas: yoga retreats, eco-lodges and holistic centers draw travelers who pay premium rates for an authentic jungle experience. A successful project pairs sustainable architecture (natural, low-impact materials), off-grid systems (many lots have no utilities) and a design that respects the jungle and cenotes — which is also a requirement of the SEMA environmental authorization. The key is a builder who masters Tulum’s environmental rules, karst soil and low-impact techniques. We build turnkey — design, SEMA permits, construction, cabins, shala, spa and off-grid — on a fixed-price contract with warranty, so your retreat operates from day one.
