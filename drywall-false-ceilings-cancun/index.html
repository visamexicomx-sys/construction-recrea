Drywall & Ceilings in Cancún: partitions, false ceilings and LED coves

We build drywall partitions, false ceilings and lighting coves in Cancún with moisture-resistant boards and galvanised framing made for the coast. Cancún has the largest housing stock in the state: Hotel Zone condos, family houses in Supermanzanas, gated communities in the south and new towers in Puerto Cancún, with constant demand for bathroom remodels, floors, paint and maintenance.

Where drywall makes sense in Cancún

Hotel Zone buildings face open sea on one side and the lagoon on the other, while inland neighbourhoods are hot and humid; salt protection matters on the coast, mould prevention everywhere. It is the fastest way to add rooms, hide AC ducts and build ceiling details in this kind of property.

Moisture-resistant systems in Cancún

Licences come from Benito Juárez municipality; condo towers require building-administration approval, insurance certificates and set work hours, and Hotel Zone buildings often restrict noisy work to weekdays. Board type, framing and ventilation are chosen for this humidity so ceilings do not sag or stain.

Water supply in Cancún

Aguakan supplies the city, but the water is hard and pressure varies by sector, so softeners, filters and pressure pumps are common upgrades in any bathroom or kitchen job.

Rentals and scheduling in Cancún

Many Hotel Zone and downtown units are short-term rentals, so we phase work between guests and coordinate lifts and loading bays with building management.

Materials, crews and access in Cancún

Cancún is about 50 minutes from our workshops and has the best supply of tile, fittings, plants and materials in the region, which keeps lead times short.
