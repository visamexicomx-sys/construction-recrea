Apartment & Condo Building Construction in Playa del Carmen: From Lot to Deeds

For developers, investors and landowners who want a condo or apartment building in Playa del Carmen: 4 to 40 units, rooftop amenities, parking and the installations buyers and rental managers expect. We start with the arithmetic of density, height and parking that decides how many units the lot really supports, then permit, build and deliver units ready to deed under a condominium regime — on a fixed price and a date your presales can rely on.

Density and parking decide the unit count

Before the first sketch we run the numbers that set the project: units per hectare, the land-use coefficient, maximum height and the parking spaces the municipality requires per unit. On most lots in this region parking, not the floor area, is the real limit. We size the building backwards from that arithmetic so the units you sell are units you can permit.

Built for presales and rental managers

Developers here sell off plan, so the construction schedule is a sales tool: we agree milestones that match buyer payments, deliver a show unit early, and report weekly with dated photos buyers can see. Units are designed for short-term rental — durable finishes, lock-offs, storage for operators — because that is how most buyers will use them.

Building in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the second market and the busiest for small and mid-size condo buildings: downtown near Quinta Avenida, Playacar, Centro Maya, the north and the Corasol corridor. Lots are narrow, municipal height limits are strict, and buyers are largely foreign investors who expect rooftop amenities and rental-ready units.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Solidaridad; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
