Road Construction in Xpu-Há: private roads, streets and access roads

We build private roads, subdivision streets, access roads and yards in Xpu-Há as one package, from survey and earthworks to pavement, drainage and signage. Xpu-Há has long private access roads through the jungle to beach hotels, villas and condos, many still in sascab, plus car parks and service yards for resorts along the highway.

Xpu-Ha, 2026 references: Unpaved access road: $360–$660; Asphalt road, light traffic: $950–$1,600; Asphalt road, heavy traffic: $1,400–$2,300.

Drainage & Infiltration

Crossfall, gutters, kerb inlets and infiltration wells, since most of the region has no storm sewer.

Private & Access Roads

Access roads to villas, hotels, ranches and resorts, including long runs through jungle with passing places and turning areas.

The ground under a road in Xpu-Há

Between the highway and the beach the ground is limestone with cenotes and thin soil, changing to sand near the coast; sascab roads here rut and wash out quickly without a proper base and crossfall. The pavement design and the base thickness are set for these conditions after the soil study, not copied from another site.

Permits for a road in Xpu-Há

Licences come from Solidaridad; most access roads cross private or ejido land under easements, and widening or paving them needs agreements with every owner along the route. We map these approvals during design so that construction starts with every connection and clearing authorised.

Rain and drainage in Xpu-Há

Unpaved roads lose material with every storm and turn into channels; crossfall, side drains and permeable or sealed surfaces stop the yearly cycle of regrading.

Plants, materials and access in Xpu-Há

About 30 minutes from our yard; hot-mix trucks reach the highway easily but narrow private roads limit the size of pavers and rollers, so long roads are paved in shorter daily sections.
