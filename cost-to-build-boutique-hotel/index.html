How Much Does It Cost to Build a Boutique Hotel? Mexico’s Caribbean in 2026

A boutique hotel of 8–30 keys is the most common hotel project in the Riviera Maya. Here is what it costs per m², per key and in total, with 12 and 20-key budgets in ten towns and the items owners usually forget.

Quick answer: How Much Does It Cost to Build a Boutique Hotel? Mexico’s Caribbean in 2026 — prices: Construction per m²: $17,000–$24,000 MXN; Construction per key (≈60 m²): $1,020,000–$1,440,000 MXN; FF&E and OS&E per key: $250,000–$600,000 MXN; 12 keys (≈720 m²), construction: $12.2M–$17.3M MXN.
2026, Cancún base: construction $17,000–$24,000 MXN per m², about $1.0M–$1.4M per key at ≈60 m²; FF&E and OS&E $250,000–$600,000 per key. 12 keys: $12.2M–$17.3M of construction; 20 keys: $20.4M–$28.8M.

What Drives the Cost

Area per Key

Suites, terraces and plunge pools push it from 45 to 80+ m² per key.

Restaurant & Bar

A real restaurant adds kitchen, extraction and staff areas.

Pool & Rooftop

Rooftop pools need structure and waterproofing designed for them.

Water & Energy

Off-grid towns need treatment, cisterns, solar and backup power.

Logistics

Islands and remote towns add 12–30% for barges and crews.

FF&E

Custom joinery and local craft vs. catalogue furniture.

What owners forget

  • Fire detection, wastewater treatment, backup power, laundry and staff areas — same systems as big hotels over fewer keys.
  • FF&E bought late, at retail prices, with long lead times.
  • Design, permits and supervision: 8–12% of construction.
  • Pre-opening: hiring, training, systems and launch, 3–6%.
  • Contingency of 5–10%.
