Plumbing in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

Water in the Riviera Maya is hard and scaly, and many areas lack reliable pressure or municipal drainage — so plumbing here needs specific solutions: a cistern with pressurization for constant pressure, water treatment (softener, osmosis) to protect equipment and health, and on jungle lots, a septic tank or biodigester. We use quality heat-resistant CPVC/PPR piping, leave accessible clean-outs and coordinate the plumbing with the build and DRO-signed drawings.
