Industrial Warehouses in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Cancun–Playa del Carmen corridor is one of southeast Mexico’s fastest-growing logistics markets: tourism, construction and retail drive demand for bodegas, distribution warehouses and logistics centers. A well-built warehouse — long-span steel structure, high-strength industrial floor, docks and solid electrical — leases fast or supports your operation for decades. What matters is the correct industrial land use, the permits (license, DRO, Civil Protection) and an engineered foundation on the karst soil. We build turnkey — design, permits, structure, floor, offices and installations — on a fixed-price contract with warranty, for own use or lease investment.
