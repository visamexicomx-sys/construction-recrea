Renovating in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Riviera Maya is a corridor of distinct markets: beachfront condos in the Cancun Hotel Zone, eco-chic villas in Tulum, marina condos in Puerto Aventuras and residential homes in Playa del Carmen. The common climate — rain, humidity and salt air — demands anti-corrosion finishes and reinforced waterproofing everywhere. Interior renovations don’t require a permit; structural changes do (license, DRO and, in Tulum, SEMA). As a regional builder, we give a fixed line-item budget and coordinate plumbing, electrical and finishes under one roof — ideal for owners who rent or don’t live here.
