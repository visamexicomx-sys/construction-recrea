Commercial construction in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

Works, services and licensing for retail, restaurants and offices with a single contractor.

Malecón Tajamar’s mixed-use zoning brings ground-floor retail, cafés, clinics and offices to the base of its residential towers, beside Puerto Cancún. Commercial work here means fit-outs that serve the people living upstairs and passers-by on the lagoon walk.

Units at the foot of towers

Most commercial space sits in tower podiums, so the condo regime, the building’s risers and its fire systems set the limits. We agree extraction routes, grease traps and AC condenser locations with the administration before the lease is signed.

Walk-in trade on the malecón

Cafés, ice-cream shops, gyms and small restaurants live off the lagoon walk. Shopfronts, terraces and signage are designed for the promenade while respecting municipal rules on use of the public way.

Water under the floor

Podium slabs sit close to a very high water table. Before laying floors for a kitchen or a clinic, we check slab moisture and drainage falls, because damp floors ruin finishes and fail health inspections.

Budget and programme for a commercial unit in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

Commercial fit-out in Malecón Tajamar runs at MXN 11,000 to 26,000 per m², depending on use; restaurants and clinics sit at the top. Terraces on the promenade are priced separately.

A 60–200 m² fit-out takes two to four months. Add four to six weeks for the condo’s approval, the Benito Juárez licence, Protección Civil and COFEPRIS where food or health is involved.

Scope: from land use to handover with the unit ready to open

We cover the full cycle: land-use and feasibility checks, executive and services design, licence filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical, plumbing, air conditioning, extraction, voice and data, finishes, fixed furniture and signage. Handover comes with as-built drawings, calculation records and the files Protección Civil asks for.

Where the use handles food, we resolve the grease trap, extraction and washing at design stage rather than as a late adjustment: it is the item that causes the most retrofits and the most expensive to correct once the unit is finished.

How we control cost, programme and opening

A line-item budget with quantities and unit rates, changes signed before execution and payments against verified progress. The programme is organised by work fronts so services, finishes and equipment do not obstruct each other, and the opening date is set once the critical filings are resolved, not when the contract is signed.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Most of our clients live outside Mexico and run the business remotely: the system is built for that.

The ground in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: municipal + SEMARNAT where mangrove remains
  • Ground: fill over lagoon sediment
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: moderate
  • Access: urban avenues next to Puerto Cancún
  • Development regime: high-rise residential and mixed use on the lagoon

A lagoon-front district whose 2016 mangrove dispute made environmental compliance closely watched here.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a restaurant in a tower podium in Malecón Tajamar?

Usually yes, if the condo regime allows it and the building has a route for extraction and a grease trap. We confirm both before you sign.

Can I put tables on the malecón in front of my unit?

Only with municipal permission for the use of the public way. We design the terrace to the conditions it sets.
