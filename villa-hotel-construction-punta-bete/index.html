Villa and hotel construction in Punta Bete

Off-grid boutique hotels and beach rental villas north of Playa del Carmen: infrastructure planning, federal filings, front-row construction and an operable handover.

Punta Bete has a handful of small hotels and beach houses on a wide, quiet beach with no sewer and no nearby power line. A hospitality project here is defined by its infrastructure as much as by its architecture.

Infrastructure is the first chapter

Before rooms are drawn, we design the power system (solar with storage, generator, or a costed line extension), water storage and treatment with zero discharge. Their size, space and cost shape the number of keys the project can support.

Small keys, big beach

Guests come for space and quiet. A boutique hotel of eight to twenty keys, or a pair of large rental villas, fits the low density and the beach. We place every room for sea views and keep service areas towards the road.

Dirt road, coastal climate

Access off the highway is unpaved, and the beach front carries severe salt and a very high water table. We stage materials on site, pour in the dry months, raise floors, anchor pools and specify for marine exposure throughout.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Punta Bete

For an off-grid boutique hotel or rental villa in Punta Bete we work in a range of MXN 21,000 to 37,000 per built m² (roughly USD 1,145 – 2,015/m²). The power and treatment systems, the access road and the marine specification drive the figure.

An eight to twenty key hotel takes fourteen to nineteen months, including the federal environmental file and the beach concession.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Punta Bete: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Solidaridad
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession)
  • Ground: sand over limestone
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: dirt road off the federal highway
  • Development regime: low density, off-grid

No sewer and no CFE nearby: a treatment plant and a line extension belong in the first budget.

Frequently asked questions

How many rooms can an off-grid hotel in Punta Bete support?

It depends on the power and treatment capacity you can install on the lot as much as on density. We size both first and fit the keys to them.

Is a line extension better than solar in Punta Bete?

Sometimes. We price both in the first budget: a line extension has a high upfront cost and wait time; solar with storage and a generator has lower running costs but needs space.
