Gym & Fitness Centre Construction in Puerto Morelos

For chains, franchisees and boutique studios opening in Puerto Morelos. We build gyms that stay cool, quiet for the neighbours and easy to clean: impact-absorbing floors and slabs, acoustic isolation from shops and homes, A/C and fresh air for full classes, changing rooms that survive hundreds of showers a day — in a new building or a plaza unit.

Heat, noise and water

Gyms here fail in three places: A/C that cannot cope with a full class at 6 pm, music and dropped weights that bring complaints from neighbours, and changing rooms that leak after a year. We size cooling and fresh air for peak occupancy, isolate slabs and walls, and waterproof wet areas to the slab.

New building or plaza unit

Most gyms open in plazas: we check the structure for live loads and equipment, the plaza’s power and A/C capacity and its works rules before signing the lease. Larger clubs with pools are built as their own buildings.

Building in Puerto Morelos

Puerto Morelos is low-rise by design: strict height and density, a protected reef and a careful environmental review. Buyers want quiet, small, well-built projects; costs run about 9% above Cancún.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Puerto Morelos; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
