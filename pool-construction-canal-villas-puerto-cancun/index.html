Pool Construction in the Puerto Cancún Canal Villas

Canal villa pools sit close to the water table and the seawall; we design the structure and waterproofing for that and often build the pool deck down to the dock.

Ultra-luxury villas built in the last decade and lots still waiting for construction. Typical projects are new villas from scratch, docks, pools and complete repositioning of the first villas built here.

The setting: Navigable canals, private docks

Water on one side means seawall and dock detailing, deep foundations on fill, waterproofing below the water table and marine-grade metalwork everywhere outdoors.

Groundwater and soil

The water table in Cancún is shallow and the ground is sand over limestone, so shells are designed against uplift and drained correctly during construction.

Equipment that survives salt

Pumps, heaters and fittings are placed in ventilated rooms and specified for coastal air; salt chlorination is set up correctly to avoid corrosion.

Rules in this sector

The association's architectural review, the municipal licence with DRO and the master plan's marine guidelines for docks and seawalls; crews and trucks are scheduled with security. New pools and changes to the exterior need the community's approval and a municipal licence; rooftop pools also need the tower administration and structural calculations.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
