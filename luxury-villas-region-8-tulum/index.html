Luxury villas in Región 8, Tulum

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Región 8 is the part of Tulum closest to the centre: smaller lots, mixed street surfaces and more density pressure than the newer regions. A luxury villa here wins on location — walk to town — and has to create privacy on a tighter plot.

Privacy on a smaller lot

Neighbours are close and some will build higher. We design inward-looking houses: courtyards, a pool screened by walls and planting, roof terraces with parapets and windows placed to avoid overlooking, so the villa feels secluded a few blocks from the main avenue.

Density pressure

Municipal density allows more on neighbouring lots than you may want. We check the land-use coefficients of the surrounding parcels and plan the villa for the likely future skyline, not just today’s empty lots.

Town-centre logistics

Streets are narrow and busy, and some are unpaved. We plan deliveries, crane positions and concrete pumping with the neighbours and the municipality, and keep the street frontage clean and safe throughout the works.

Región 8 sits in the urban band: karst, low salt and no committee premium. Tight lots that need basement-free compact layouts, rooftop pools and imported finishes push towards the top.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Región 8, Tulum: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: close to the centre, mixed street surfaces
  • Development regime: municipal density

Frequently asked questions

Why build a luxury villa in Región 8?

Because it is walking distance to central Tulum, which matters for both living and rental. The design has to create privacy on a smaller lot.

Can my neighbours build higher than me?

Yes, within municipal density. We check the surrounding land-use coefficients before design and plan views and privacy for that future.
