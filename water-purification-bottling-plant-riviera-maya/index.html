Water Purification & Bottling Plant Construction in the Riviera Maya

Nobody drinks tap water in the Riviera Maya, so purified water is a steady business, from the neighbourhood refill station to the bottling plant that supplies hotels and stores. We build purification stations and bottling plants with sanitary finishes, wells and cisterns, reverse-osmosis and UV lines and a layout ready for the COFEPRIS sanitary inspection.

Quick answer: Water Purification & Bottling Plant Construction in the Riviera Maya — 2026 prices: Bottling plant building with sanitary finishes, per m²: $14,000–$24,000 MXN; Treatment, filling and capping line: $3M–$15M MXN; Well, cistern and pre-treatment: $0.5M–$3M MXN; Bottling plant 800–1,500 m² with line: $20M–$45M MXN.
Riviera Maya 2026: neighbourhood purification station $0.6M–$2.5M MXN; bottling building $14,000–$24,000 per m²; treatment and filling line $3M–$15M. A bottling plant of 800–1,500 m² runs about $20M–$45M.

Loading, crate storage and delivery vehicle yard.

Karst water needs real treatment

Groundwater here is hard and carries limestone and, near the coast, salt. Pre-treatment, softening and reverse osmosis sized for the real raw water decide quality and membrane life. We test the source before sizing the line.

A building inspectors approve

A bottling plant is a food plant: washable walls and floors, sanitary drainage, separated clean and dirty areas, staff filters and controlled flows from bottle washing to dispatch. We design it that way from the start so the COFEPRIS licence is a formality.
