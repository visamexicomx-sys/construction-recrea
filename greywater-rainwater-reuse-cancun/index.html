Rainwater Harvesting and Greywater Reuse in Cancún

Cancún residents know the pattern: in the dry months and peak season, pressure drops and some neighbourhoods go days with little water, while from June to November tropical downpours flood the streets. Capturing that rain and reusing greywater turns the problem around. For villas in Isla Dorada, Lagos del Sol and Cumbres, towers in Puerto Cancún and Tajamar and hotels with large gardens, it means fewer tanker trucks, lower bills and less dependence on the network.

Cancún 2026: rainwater system for a villa (10–40 m³ cistern, filtration, pump) $80,000–$450,000 MXN; greywater recycling for a villa $90,000–$400,000 MXN; combined system for a condo tower or hotel with dual plumbing $1.5M–$6M MXN.

Sized for Cancún's rain

Most of Cancún’s rain falls between June and November, with heavy single events during tropical storms, while March to May is dry. A cistern sized only on annual totals runs empty exactly when the network is weakest. We model rainfall and demand month by month, add first-flush diverters so roof dirt and salt stay out, and size overflow for storm intensity so the system also protects the house from flooding.

For towers and hotels

In multi-storey buildings, greywater recycling for toilets is usually the biggest win: a large, steady volume reused every day. It needs a separate riser and treatment room, so it is easiest in new towers and major renovations. For existing hotels, irrigation and laundry reuse can be added with less disruption.
