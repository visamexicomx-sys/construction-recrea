Villa and hotel construction in Chetumal

City hotels and villas in the state capital: design, licence, construction and a handover ready to operate.

Chetumal’s hotel demand is not beach tourism: it is government business, Belize border trade, medical visitors and travellers on the way to Chetumal. That calls for a different hotel — urban, efficient and open all year.

A year-round market, not a season

Civil servants, suppliers, conference visitors and families crossing to Belize keep occupancy steadier than on the coast. The product that works is a mid-size city hotel or serviced apartments with parking, meeting space and reliable air conditioning, rather than a resort; we size the brief to that demand.

The bayfront and the city centre

Lots on the Boulevard Bahía have the view and the manatee-sanctuary review; lots in the centre near Avenida de los Héroes have the walk-in trade and simpler filings. Parking requirements under the municipal regulations often decide how many keys fit, so we test the parking layout before the room layout.

Hurricane-rated, then comfortable

Janet and Dean set the design load here: reinforced concrete frame, tied roof slabs, protected openings and back-up power for the lifts and the cold room. Once that is in place, we put the money into acoustics and air conditioning, which are what business guests actually rate.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Chetumal

For a city hotel in Chetumal we work in a range of MXN 14,000 to 26,000 per built m² (roughly USD 765 – 1,420/m²), among the lowest in the state, because materials and crews are local. The hurricane specification and the lifts are where we do not cut.

A twenty to forty key hotel takes eleven to fifteen months of construction. The municipal licence with Othón P. Blanco takes two to three months; on the bayfront, add the environmental review to the critical path.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Chetumal: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Othón P. Blanco
  • Environmental regime: municipal + environmental review on the bay front (manatee sanctuary)
  • Ground: limestone with sascab and pockets of clay
  • Water table: moderate, higher near the bay
  • Salt exposure: moderate (brackish bay)

A state capital on a protected bay: city procedures, local supply and hurricane wind as the governing load.

  • Pockets of clay appear inland: a soil study before design avoids oversizing or, worse, undersizing the foundation.
  • On the bay front, include the environmental review in the programme from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Is there demand for a new hotel in Chetumal?

For the right product, yes: business and government travel, border trade and visitors heading to Chetumal keep occupancy steadier than at the beach. A feasibility check on location and parking comes before design.

Can I build on the bayfront in Chetumal?

Yes, but the bay is a manatee sanctuary, so setbacks, runoff and any work on the shore go through an environmental review as well as the municipal licence. We include it in the programme from the start.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
