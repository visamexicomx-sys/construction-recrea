Pool Construction in the Puerto Cancún Golf District

Golf villas usually have the pool facing the fairway; we design infinity or flush edges towards the course, with equipment rooms and drainage that respect the course boundary.

New-build villas on golf lots, villas from the first phases that owners now reposition, and golf-view condos. Clients are families relocating from the US, Canada and Mexico City who want a house rather than a tower.

The setting: Centre of the master plan, 18-hole course

Protected from the sea, so the main issues are sun, rain and irrigation: roof waterproofing, drainage of gardens towards the course, shading of terraces and pool equipment rooms.

Groundwater and soil

The water table in Cancún is shallow and the ground is sand over limestone, so shells are designed against uplift and drained correctly during construction.

Equipment that survives salt

Pumps, heaters and fittings are placed in ventilated rooms and specified for coastal air; salt chlorination is set up correctly to avoid corrosion.

Rules in this sector

New villas and exterior changes go through the master plan's architectural review (setbacks, heights, façades, colours), plus a Benito Juárez licence with DRO; fairway edges cannot be used for storage or access. New pools and changes to the exterior need the community's approval and a municipal licence; rooftop pools also need the tower administration and structural calculations.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
