Self-Storage (Mini Bodegas) Construction in the Riviera Maya

Condos without storage rooms, owners who live abroad half the year, businesses with stock and nowhere to keep it: demand for self-storage in Cancún, Playa del Carmen and Tulum keeps growing. We design and build mini-bodega facilities that rent well and are cheap to run: efficient unit mixes, steel partitions, security, climate-controlled units and drive-up access.

2026 costs: single-storey drive-up self-storage $7,500–$11,500 MXN/m² built; multi-storey facility with lift and corridors $11,000–$16,000 MXN/m²; climate-controlled units add $1,500–$3,000 MXN/m². Steel unit partitions and roll-up doors included in these ranges.

What a Self-Storage Facility Needs

Unit Mix

A mix from 2 to 30 m² planned against local demand, so the building fills evenly.

Partitions & Roll-Up Doors

Galvanized steel partition systems and roll-up doors that resist humidity and salt.

Security

Perimeter fence, access control, CCTV and lighting designed in from the start.

Climate-Controlled Units

Dehumidified units for furniture, documents and wine, a premium product on the coast.

Drive-Up Access

Wide aisles and loading areas so customers can unload a pick-up or van at the door.

Land Use & Permits

Commercial or warehouse land use, construction licence with DRO and Protección Civil.

Design that rents

Self-storage profitability depends on rentable area per m² built and on getting the unit mix right. Small units (2–6 m²) rent at the highest price per m² and suit condo residents; medium units (9–15 m²) suit families moving or renovating; large drive-up units (20–30 m²) suit businesses and contractors. We plan the layout to maximise rentable area, keep corridors efficient and leave room for a second phase.

Humidity is the enemy

On the coast, a closed metal box full of furniture and cardboard grows mould in a rainy season. We specify ventilated or dehumidified buildings, reflective insulated roofs, galvanized partitions and drainage that keeps water out of ground-floor units. Climate-controlled units, priced as a premium, are often the first to fill in Cancún and Playa del Carmen.
