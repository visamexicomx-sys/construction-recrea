Craft Brewery, Brewpub & Taproom Construction in Cozumel

Tourists and locals in Cozumel want beer brewed here, and a brewpub is both a factory and a destination. We build craft breweries, brewpubs and taprooms for brewers and investors: slabs and trench drains for the brewhouse, glycol cooling and cold rooms, steam or electric heating, wastewater pretreatment that the utility accepts and a taproom that sells the beer.

Cozumel 2026: building $13,000–$22,000 MXN per m²; 10–20 hl brewhouse installed $4.3M–$11M; fermentation and glycol $2.4M–$7.3M; taproom $15,000–$27,000 per m². A complete brewpub of 400–600 m² runs about $17.1M–$36.6M.

What We Deliver

Floors & Drains

Sloped slabs, trench drains and acid-resistant finishes.

Glycol & Cold

Glycol chillers, cold rooms and insulated piping.

Heat & Steam

Boiler or electric heating, ventilation and gas.

Wastewater

pH and solids pretreatment to meet discharge limits.

Taproom

Bar, draft system, kitchen and terrace.

Permits

Land use, health licence, alcohol licence and Civil Protection.

Floors, drains and water

Breweries fail on floors and drains before they fail on beer. We design slopes, trench drains, acid-resistant coatings and wastewater pretreatment from the brewhouse layout, then install equipment with the manufacturer.

Heat and cold in the tropics

Fermentation needs steady cold in a hot climate. We size glycol and insulation for local temperatures and design ventilation so the brewhouse stays workable.

Building in Cozumel

In Cozumel every truck of materials crosses by ferry, so costs run about 22% above Cancún and the schedule must plan barges, weather and spare parts. Demand comes from second-home buyers, divers and long-stay rentals.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Cozumel; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
