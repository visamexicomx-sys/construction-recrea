Clinic and Hospital Construction Cost Calculator

Healthcare buildings are among the most demanding: medical gases, emergency power, air quality, shielding and COFEPRIS rules. Choose the facility type, area, operating rooms and imaging rooms and get a 2026 range for your zone.

How it works: built area × the 2026 rate for the facility type, plus operating rooms and imaging rooms as packages (air handling, medical gases, shielding), adjusted to the zone.

What Moves the Price

Facility Type

Offices, clinics and hospitals differ in MEP intensity.

Operating Rooms

Each OR adds HVAC, medical gases, power and finishes.

Imaging

X-ray and CT rooms need shielding and power.

Emergency Power

Generators and UPS are mandatory for critical areas.

COFEPRIS

Health licensing shapes the layout from day one.

Zone

Islands and remote towns add logistics.
