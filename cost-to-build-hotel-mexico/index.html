How Much Does It Cost to Build a Hotel in Mexico? The Full Budget per Key (2026)

Construction is only part of a hotel budget. This page puts the whole development cost per key together — construction by class, FF&E, design and permits, pre-opening and contingency — with 2026 prices from our own quotes in Quintana Roo and the factor for each town.

Quick answer: In the Mexican Caribbean in 2026, construction alone runs from about $252,000 MXN per key for an economy hotel to $2.6M–$4.4M for a luxury resort. With FF&E, design, permits, pre-opening and contingency the total is usually 35–55% above construction, before land.
2026, Cancún base, construction per key: economy $252,000–$364,000 MXN; 3★ $480,000–$680,000; boutique / 4★ $1,020,000–$1,440,000; luxury / 5★ $2,640,000–$4,400,000. Add FF&E, 8–12% design and permits, 3–6% pre-opening and 5–10% contingency.

What Goes Into a Hotel Budget

Construction

Structure, envelope, MEP, finishes and site works — 60–70% of the total.

FF&E and OS&E

Furniture, fixtures, equipment, linens and operating supplies — 10–20%.

Design & Permits

Architecture, engineering, environmental review, licences — 8–12% of construction.

Pre-Opening

Hiring, training, systems and marketing before the first guest — 3–6%.

Contingency

5–10% for what the ground and the authorities reveal.

Land

Priced separately: it varies more than everything else combined.

Construction per key, by class

Gross area per key is what separates the classes: a hostel needs about 28 m² per key including circulation and services, a 3★ city hotel about 40, a boutique about 60, a 5★ resort 110 or more with its restaurants, spa and back of house. Multiply by the cost per m² and the class sets the budget long before the finishes do.

Town factors

The same hotel costs more on the islands and in remote towns because of ferries, barges, travel and crews: Holbox about 30% above Cancún, Isla Mujeres 26%, Cozumel 22%, Costa Mujeres 20%, Tulum 13%; Chetumal and Bacalar sit slightly below. The town table is at the end of this page.

A worked example

A 20-key boutique hotel in Playa del Carmen (≈1,200 m²): construction about $21M–$30M MXN, FF&E $5M–$12M, design and permits $2M–$3.5M, pre-opening and contingency $2.5M–$5M — roughly $31M–$50M MXN before land and VAT, or $1.5M–$2.5M per key.
