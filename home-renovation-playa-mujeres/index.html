Home renovation in Playa Mujeres

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Playa Mujeres is young and high-end: villas and condos delivered over the last fifteen years inside golf and resort developments north of Cancún. Renovation here is about bringing developer-built homes up to the owner’s standard and protecting them against a beachfront that is severe on materials.

Upgrading inside a development

Owners upgrade kitchens, bathrooms, outdoor living and pool areas, add shade and automation and replace finishes that aged faster than expected. The development’s committee reviews anything visible from outside, so we submit drawings and samples before works start.

Sand, mangrove and the water table

Homes sit on sand with mangrove behind and the water close to the surface. Leaks into ground floors, pool cracks and drainage that backs up in heavy rain are common; we trace the cause and rebuild waterproofing and drainage as one system.

Isla Mujeres municipality

Although on the mainland next to Cancún, Playa Mujeres belongs to Isla Mujeres. Works that change structure, façade or footprint need its licence, filed in parallel with the development’s approval.

Realistic budget and programme in Playa Mujeres

A full renovation in Playa Mujeres runs at MXN 9,500 to 19,000 per m² renovated (roughly USD 520 – 1,035/m²). Premium finishes, coastal glazing and waterproofing against the water table sit at the top.

A typical villa or condo takes two to five months. The development’s committee approval comes first; structural or façade changes also need the Isla Mujeres licence.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Playa Mujeres: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Isla Mujeres (continental)
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession)
  • Ground: sand and mangrove
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: single road via Punta Sam
  • Development regime: developments with committee and golf course

It belongs to the Isla Mujeres municipality even though it sits on the mainland — the authority is not Cancún.

Frequently asked questions

Which municipality issues renovation permits in Playa Mujeres?

Isla Mujeres, on its continental side, not Benito Juárez (Cancún). The development’s committee approval is also needed for exterior changes.

Why does water get into ground floors in Playa Mujeres?

Because the water table is close to the surface on sand next to mangrove. The fix is waterproofing from the negative side and proper drainage, not just paint or sealant.
