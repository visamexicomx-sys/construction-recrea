Hotel MEP & Energy in Chan Chemuyil: Chillers, VRF, Hot Water and Controls

Cooling, hot water and power are the biggest running cost of a hotel in Chan Chemuyil and its most visible failure; we replace them with the hotel open and cut energy bills on the way. Municipal water and power are limited, so new buildings usually add storage tanks, solar water heating and their own wastewater treatment.

Chan Chemuyil, 2026 references: Chiller plant: $4.6M–$28.7M; VRF: $105k–$205k; Heat-pump hot water: $1.7M–$6.9M.

How the climate in Chan Chemuyil wears out plant rooms

Inland jungle conditions: less salt than the beach but high humidity, heavy rain and karst soil that must be checked for cavities before foundations. We specify coated coils, stainless fixings and ventilated plant rooms so new equipment lasts in these conditions.

Replacement windows in Chan Chemuyil

Support facilities here work for resorts that never close, so projects are planned to hand over in phases without interrupting hotel operations. Chillers and boilers are swapped unit by unit in these windows, with rental plant when needed.

Permits and authorities in Chan Chemuyil

Within Tulum municipality, urban lots here have simpler zoning than the beach, which makes the town practical for staff housing, laundries and warehouses serving nearby hotels.
