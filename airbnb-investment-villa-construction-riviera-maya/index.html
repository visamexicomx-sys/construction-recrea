Investment Villa in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Riviera Maya is one of Latin America’s strongest vacation-rental markets: Tulum, Playa del Carmen and Cancun combine year-round tourist demand with strong land appreciation. A villa built to rent (not just to live in) is designed differently: more en-suite bedrooms, a pool, a rooftop, and the details that earn 5-star reviews and lift the nightly rate. Well run, an Airbnb villa can gross $80,000–$180,000 USD/year at nightly rates of $200–$2,000+ depending on tier. We build turnkey — design, permits, construction, pool and furnishing — and hand you a property ready to list on Airbnb from day one, with a fixed-price contract and warranty.
