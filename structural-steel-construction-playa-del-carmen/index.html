Structural Steel Fabrication and Erection in Playa del Carmen

Our metal workshop is in Playa del Carmen, so here steel goes from the shop to your site in minutes, not hours. We build warehouses and workshops on Avenida Juárez, the 307 and the west side, commercial buildings and showrooms on Constituyentes, CTM and Avenida 30, rooftop structures and additions in the centre, and steel frames for villas in Playacar, Corasol and the north communities.

Building in the centre

In central Playa, streets are narrow, many are pedestrian and cranes need permits and early hours. Steel helps: pieces are fabricated to size in our workshop, lifted in small sections and bolted on site, so rooftop structures and additions go up with minimal disruption to neighbours and businesses on Quinta Avenida and the surrounding streets.

Salt and storms

Playa is close to the sea everywhere, so we specify galvanizing or duplex coatings for anything exposed, and design wind loads with the CFE 2020 manual for the site. Rooftop structures, being high and exposed, get uplift-rated connections and anchorage into slabs checked by our structural engineer.
