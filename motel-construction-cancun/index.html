Motel & Auto-hotel Construction in Cancún

Motels with private garages are one of the highest-occupancy hospitality formats in Mexican cities, and Cancún is no exception. We build motels and auto-hotels for investors and operators: rooms with private garages and discreet access, suites with jacuzzi, acoustic separation, a laundry and service building that turns rooms fast, and the licences to open.

Quick answer: Motel & Auto-hotel Construction in Cancún — 2026 prices: Per room with private garage: $650,000–$1,200,000 MXN; Construction, per m²: $13,000–$22,000 MXN; Jacuzzi suite upgrade, per room: $120,000–$300,000 MXN; Laundry and service building: $2M–$5M MXN.
Cancún 2026: per room with garage $650,000–$1,200,000 MXN; construction $13,000–$22,000 per m²; jacuzzi suite upgrade $120,000–$300,000 per room. A 20-room motel runs about $16M–$30M; a 40-room motel $32M–$58M.

Turnover is the business

A motel earns on room turnover. We plan service corridors, laundry and linen flow so rooms are ready in minutes, and garages and circulation so guests never cross.

Durable, easy-clean finishes

High occupancy wears rooms out fast. We specify finishes, sanitaryware and A/C that survive heavy use and clean quickly, and design suites that justify a higher rate.

Building in Cancún

Cancún is the largest market in the state: vertical condos along Bonampak, Malecón Tajamar, Puerto Cancún and the Hotel Zone, mid-rise buildings in Cumbres, Arbolada and Huayacán, and the most complete supply chain and labour pool in the region. Heights and densities are set by the urban development programme, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Benito Juárez; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
