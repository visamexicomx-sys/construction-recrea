Hotel Construction and Modernisation Services, Under One Roof

Owners, operators and asset managers of 5-star resorts, branded hotels and premium boutiques need more than a builder: they need one partner for the new wing and the kitchen upgrade, the chiller plant and the brand conversion, the facade after a hurricane and the staff housing behind the resort. This is the full list of what we deliver for hotels in Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Costa Mujeres, Cozumel and the rest of Quintana Roo.

For hotels of every class: from hostels and 3-star city hotels to 5-star, luxury and all-inclusive resorts. New builds, expansions and modernisation, with the hotel open or closed.

What We Do for Hotels

New Hotels & Expansions

Hotels of every class, new wings, villas and amenities.

Renovation & Brand Conversion

Guestroom programmes, public areas, PIPs and repositioning.

MEP & Energy

Chillers, VRF, hot water, BMS, solar and efficiency retrofits.

Back-of-House

Kitchens, laundries, stores, staff areas and staff housing.

Safety & Resilience

Fire and life safety, accessibility, facades and hurricane hardening.

Maintenance & Recovery

Preventive maintenance contracts and post-hurricane recovery.

How we work with hotel owners

We start from the operator’s standards and the owner’s numbers: cost per key, downtime and return. Each project gets one project director, a phasing plan agreed with operations, and weekly reports that owners, asset managers and lenders can use. We build in our own steel and carpentry workshops in Playa del Carmen, so mock-up rooms, millwork and structures are ready when the programme needs them.
