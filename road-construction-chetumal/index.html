Road Construction in Chetumal: private roads, streets and access roads

We build private roads, subdivision streets, access roads and yards in Chetumal as one package, from survey and earthworks to pavement, drainage and signage. Chetumal, the state capital, needs street paving and resurfacing in colonias, car parks for government buildings, commerce and clinics, and yards for logistics linked to the border and the free zone.

Chetumal, 2026 references: Unpaved access road: $260–$480; Asphalt road, light traffic: $690–$1,200; Asphalt road, heavy traffic: $1,000–$1,700.

What We Build

Survey & Pavement Design

Topography, geotechnical study and pavement design for the real traffic, from cars to 40-tonne trucks.

Signage & Safety

Line marking, traffic signs, speed humps, reflective studs and street lighting conduits.

Subdivision Streets

Streets for gated communities and lot developments with kerbs, sidewalks, storm drainage and conduits for services.

The ground under a road in Chetumal

Chetumal sits on limestone with clay and low-lying areas near the bay, where the water table is high and bases must be thicker and better drained. The pavement design and the base thickness are set for these conditions after the soil study, not copied from another site.

Permits for a road in Chetumal

The municipality of Othón P. Blanco and state agencies handle licences and public works; connections to federal highways need SCT approval, and many street projects follow municipal standards for kerbs and sidewalks. We map these approvals during design so that construction starts with every connection and clearing authorised.

Rain and drainage in Chetumal

The city floods in heavy storms in low areas near the bay; storm design combines pipes, open channels and infiltration where the soil allows it.

Plants, materials and access in Chetumal

Chetumal has its own suppliers of hot mix, concrete and aggregates, so prices are among the lowest in the state, though our crews travel about four hours from Playa del Carmen.
