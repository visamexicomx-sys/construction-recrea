Offices in the Riviera Maya: Build, Fit Out, Renovate

One builder for every stage of an office: new office buildings, turnkey fit-outs from shell to move-in, renovations of offices in use and of whole older buildings, coworking spaces and medical and dental offices. In Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Chetumal, with weekly reports for head offices abroad.

Quick answer: In 2026, about $6,000–$12,000 MXN/m² for a basic open-plan fit-out and $12,000–$25,000 for a corporate office; Playa del Carmen about 4% more, Tulum 13% more, Chetumal 8% less.
2026 budget guide (Cancún base): office building core and shell $11,000–$26,000 MXN/m²; fit-out $6,000–$12,000 basic and $12,000–$25,000 corporate; medical or dental $20,000–$40,000; renovation $1,800–$20,000/m². Playa +4%, Tulum +13%, Chetumal −8%.

Who we work for

  • Companies and head offices opening or moving an office in the Riviera Maya, including foreign companies with no one on site.
  • Developers and owners building office buildings or repositioning older ones.
  • Professionals — lawyers, notaries, accountants, real-estate agencies, doctors and dentists — fitting out their own office or consultorio.
  • Coworking operators serving the region’s remote workers.

What makes an office work here

Parking, backup power, internet redundancy and A/C that copes with real occupancy matter more to tenants than finishes. We design those first, then the layout, acoustics, lighting and image.
