Commercial, Hotel & Dark Kitchen Construction in Cancún

Every restaurant, hotel and delivery brand in Cancún lives or dies in the kitchen. We build commercial kitchens, dark kitchens and central production kitchens: extraction hoods and fire suppression, gas and power, cold rooms, grease traps, washable finishes and the workflow that lets a team cook hundreds of covers without crossing paths — ready for the health inspection.

Quick answer: Commercial, Hotel & Dark Kitchen Construction in Cancún — 2026 prices: Commercial kitchen fit-out, per m²: $18,000–$35,000 MXN; Extraction hood and fire suppression, per linear metre: $45,000–$90,000 MXN; Walk-in cold room: $0.3M–$0.9M MXN; Grease trap and drainage: $0.1M–$0.4M MXN.
Cancún 2026: kitchen fit-out $18,000–$35,000 MXN per m²; hood and fire suppression $45,000–$90,000 per linear metre; walk-in cold room $0.3M–$0.9M. A dark kitchen for 4–6 brands runs about $6M–$12M; a 600 m² hotel or central kitchen $18M–$35M.

What We Deliver

Extraction

Hoods, ductwork, make-up air and fans sized for the cooking line.

Fire Suppression

Wet-chemical systems over the line, gas shut-off and detection.

Cold Rooms

Walk-in coolers and freezers with remote condensers.

Drainage & Grease

Floor drains, slopes and grease traps that pass inspection.

Finishes

Epoxy or quarry-tile floors, washable walls, stainless.

Workflow

Receiving, prep, cook, pass and wash planned with your chef.

Workflow before equipment

A kitchen is a production line: receiving, cold storage, prep, cooking, pass and wash. We lay out the flow with your chef or operator first, then place equipment, utilities and drains — so nothing is moved after the slab is poured.

Dark kitchens

Delivery-only kitchens put several brands in one space. We design separate stations sharing cold rooms, wash and extraction, rider pick-up away from the cooks and enough power and gas for all brands at peak.

Building in Cancún

Cancún is the largest market in the state: vertical condos along Bonampak, Malecón Tajamar, Puerto Cancún and the Hotel Zone, mid-rise buildings in Cumbres, Arbolada and Huayacán, and the most complete supply chain and labour pool in the region. Heights and densities are set by the urban development programme, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Benito Juárez; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
