Clinic and medical facility construction in Bacalar

Design, construction and services with the health file resolved before opening.

Bacalar is growing faster than its medical infrastructure. The referral hospital is in Chetumal, around forty minutes away, so a local clinic resolves the everyday and prepares the transfer when needed.

Real demand: residents, tourism and hospitality staff

The resident population is growing, tourism arrives all year and the hotel sector employs hundreds of people. That sustains general consultation, dentistry, a basic laboratory and wound care, with seasonal peaks of minor emergencies. The brief that works is small and well resolved, with stabilisation and transfer capacity, rather than an attempt at a low-volume hospital.

Water and waste: scrutinised closely here

Any health facility near the lagoon concentrates two risks the municipality watches: sanitary discharge and biological-infectious hazardous waste. A sized treatment plant, no discharge to the ground, a temporary waste store under NOM-087 and a contract with an authorised carrier. It is resolved in the file, not with operational goodwill.

Reliable power and a supply line that comes from far

Outages happen and a clinic cannot depend on them not happening: an emergency generator with automatic transfer and UPS on critical equipment are part of the scope. On supply, Bacalar is far from the corridor’s centres, so medical equipment, gases and consumables are planned well ahead and slack is left on items with a single supplier.

Budget and programme for a clinic in Bacalar

Clinic fit-out: MXN 13,000 to 24,000 per m². Without marine exposure the specification drops a step against the coast; what rises is freight from the corridor and the availability of specialist installers, who do not live in the municipality.

A 120–250 m² clinic takes four to seven months. The municipal file runs six to ten weeks and the sanitary licence is resolved at the end, on completed works with equipment installed. The treatment plant and the waste store are budgeted from concept stage, because near the lagoon they are a condition rather than an adjustment.

Scope: from concept to the sanitary licence

We cover concept and executive design with full engineering, municipal filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical with back-up, plumbing, air conditioning and extraction, medical gases where applicable, sanitary finishes, fixed furniture and signage. We accompany the health file through to inspection, coordinating the facility’s health officer.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets and equipment manuals.

Why the order of filings decides the opening date

The municipal licence authorises building; the sanitary licence or notice authorises operating, and it is resolved on completed works with equipment installed and running. So the opening date is set when the health file closes, not when the construction contract is signed. We plan both in parallel so the second does not begin when the first ends.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Many of our clients are doctors or investors who do not live in the area and need to follow the works remotely.

The ground in Bacalar: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Bacalar
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT (lagoon and stromatolites) + municipal
  • Ground: limestone with lake sediment
  • Water table: very high next to the lagoon
  • Salt exposure: low

A freshwater lagoon with living stromatolites: zero discharge, a setback from the shore and restricted chemicals on the lakeside strip.

  • Federal SEMARNAT filing: the environmental resolution is the project’s critical path. Budget 4–8 months before the first excavation.
  • Very high water table: forget the basement. Compacted fill platform, raft foundation and negative-side waterproofing.

Frequently asked questions

Can we discharge to the municipal sewer or a septic tank?

Near the lagoon, no. A health facility needs its own properly sized treatment and no discharge to the ground. It is the item most often underestimated in quick budgets and the one that causes most problems at inspection, because it cannot be corrected without redoing services.

How much does the distance to Chetumal affect the project?

Far more in the brief than in the cost. A forty-minute transfer means the clinic has to stabilise well — oxygen, observation, clear ambulance access — rather than expand areas that patient volume here does not sustain.

Do you accompany the COFEPRIS process?

We handle the technical file: drawings, calculation records, specifications for finishes and services, medical gases and biological waste handling, and we respond to inspection observations. The facility’s health officer remains the signatory, and we work coordinated with that role from concept stage.

Can a clinic be fitted out in a unit that is already leased?

Yes, and it is the most common case. What has to be verified before signing the lease is clear height, electrical capacity, the possibility of extraction, the position of drainage stacks and space for the waste store. A two-hour review before signing avoids months of trouble afterwards.
