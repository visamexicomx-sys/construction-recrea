Building in Puerto Morelos: What to Know

Puerto Morelos has been its own municipality since 2016, a quiet fishing town between Cancun and Playa del Carmen, facing the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park. It’s a residential, eco zone more affordable than Cancun or Playa, in high demand from expats and for vacation rental. Beachfront work requires ZOFEMAT authorization (Federal Maritime Zone) and compliance with reef and dune rules; the rest follows the municipal license and the DRO. As a construction company in Puerto Morelos, we know the local authorities and handle everything — ideal for owners who don’t live here.
