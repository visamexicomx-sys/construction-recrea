Beach Club and Spa Construction in Cancún

In Cancún, beach clubs live inside a tight frame: most of the Hotel Zone beach is fronted by hotels, so new clubs appear on the few public-access lots, inside resorts, and along Playa Mujeres and Costa Mujeres, where the coast is still being developed. Spas follow the same pattern: hotel spas being upgraded to compete with the new resorts, and urban day spas in Puerto Cancún and downtown. We build both, with the permits that the Cancún coast requires.

Cancún 2026: beach club or day club $25,000–$50,000 MXN/m² built; hotel spa renovation $18,000–$35,000 MXN/m²; new spa or wellness centre $22,000–$45,000 MXN/m²; commercial pools $18,000–$35,000 MXN/m² of water. Erosion protection and beach works are priced per site.

Cancún's beach is a moving target

Cancún's Hotel Zone has lost and regained sand several times through hurricanes and beach nourishment projects. A beach club here needs finished floor levels set against storm surge, structures that can be partly dismantled before a hurricane, and pools and plant rooms that survive flooding. We design with the erosion history of the specific stretch of beach in mind, not just the current shoreline.

Permits on the Cancún coast

  • ZOFEMAT concession for any structure in the federal maritime zone.
  • Environmental rules for dunes and, from May to October, turtle-nesting lighting.
  • Municipal licence from Benito Juárez (Hotel Zone) or Isla Mujeres (Playa and Costa Mujeres).
  • Protección Civil occupancy, especially for clubs with events and large crowds.
