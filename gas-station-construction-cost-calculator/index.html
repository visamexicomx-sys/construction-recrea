Gas Station Construction Cost Calculator

A gas station is a project of tanks, pipes, canopy, store and permits more than of square metres. Choose dispensers, tanks, store and car wash and get a 2026 range for your zone, including the civil works, canopy and installations.

How it works: base civil works and permits engineering, plus dispensers with islands, canopy sized to the dispensers, tanks with excavation and containment, store and car wash, adjusted to the zone.

What Moves the Price

Dispensers

Each dispenser adds island, piping, canopy area and paving.

Tanks

Double-wall tanks, excavation in rock and containment are a major cost item.

Store

The convenience store is often what makes the station profitable.

Car Wash

Adds revenue and water treatment requirements.

ASEA & CRE

Federal energy-sector permits shape the schedule.

Zone

Island and remote locations add freight and logistics.
