Park & Fly Parking Lot Construction Near Cancún and Tulum Airports

Travellers search every day for long-stay parking at Cancún airport: price per day, Terminal 2, 3 and 4, covered parking, and private lots with a shuttle. For a landowner near the airport road this is one of the simplest airport businesses: a well-drained, lit and fenced lot with shade, gates and a shuttle. We design and build it turnkey, at Cancún or at the new Tulum airport.

Quick answer: Paving costs $800–$1,600 MXN/m² depending on asphalt, concrete or pavers, which is about $20,000–$48,000 MXN per stall including aisles. Shade structures, gates, lighting, the office and the shuttle area are added per project.
Parking lot 2026: asphalt paving with base, drainage and markings $800–$1,400 MXN/m²; hydraulic concrete $1,000–$1,600 MXN/m²; permeable pavers $900–$1,600 MXN/m². With aisles, one stall uses about 25–30 m², so paving alone is roughly $20,000–$48,000 MXN per stall; shade, gates, lighting and the office are priced per project.

What a Park & Fly Lot Includes

Earthworks & Paving

Clearing, sascab base, compaction and asphalt or concrete, built for daily shuttle traffic.

Drainage

Slopes, gutters and absorption wells sized for tropical storms, so the lot never floods.

Shade Structures

Steel and membrane or sheet shades, rated for hurricane wind, that customers pay extra for.

Gates & Control

Barrier gates, plate reading, ticket or app payment, perimeter fence.

Lighting & CCTV

LED poles, cameras and a guard booth, the two things reviews mention most.

Shuttle & Office

Covered drop-off, waiting area, restrooms and a small office.

How the business works

Customers leave the car for days or weeks and take a shuttle to the terminal. The lot competes with the official airport parking on price per day, covered spaces and trust: lighting, cameras, a fence and reviews. Size the lot by stalls, not by land: a 1 ha lot holds roughly 300–350 stalls after aisles, the office and the shuttle loop.

Built for rain and heat

In Cancún and Tulum the lot fails on water, not on load: one tropical storm on a flat lot without wells means flooded cars and bad reviews. We grade every bay to a drain, use absorption wells in the karst, and choose asphalt, concrete or permeable pavers by budget and by the municipality’s permeable-area rules.

Permits

Land inside the airport fence belongs to the airport operator and is built under its contracts and rules; most private projects sit outside the fence, along the access roads and Highway 307. There, three things are checked before buying or designing: the municipal land use and density (PDU), the height allowed under the airport’s obstacle limitation surfaces (AFAC), and access from the federal highway, which needs its own permit for a new entrance.
