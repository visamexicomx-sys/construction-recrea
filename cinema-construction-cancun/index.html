Sound is the product

A cinema fails when the action film next door bleeds into the drama, or when the A/C hums through a quiet scene. We isolate each auditorium as a box within the building, specify A/C with low noise criteria and test before handover. In a plaza, the same isolation protects the shops and homes around it.

Inside a plaza

Most new cinemas open as anchors of plazas: heavy long-span roofs, deep risers and high occupancy loads must be designed with the plaza structure. We coordinate with the plaza’s engineers early, or build the cinema as part of a new plaza.

Building in Cancún

Cancún is the largest market in the state: vertical condos along Bonampak, Malecón Tajamar, Puerto Cancún and the Hotel Zone, mid-rise buildings in Cumbres, Arbolada and Huayacán, and the most complete supply chain and labour pool in the region. Heights and densities are set by the urban development programme, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Benito Juárez; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
