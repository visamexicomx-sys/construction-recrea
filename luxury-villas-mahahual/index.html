Luxury villas in Mahahual

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

A luxury villa in Mahahual is a beach house at the end of the road: reef in front, mangrove behind, no high-rises and no crowds once the cruise ships leave. Luxury here means comfort that does not depend on the grid.

Off-grid luxury

Beyond the town centre there is little or no network, so the villa produces its own comfort: solar with a battery bank sized for air conditioning, a cistern for days of use, a treatment plant and a backup generator. Done properly, guests never notice that the house is independent.

Built for the reef coast

Front-row lots face open sea with salt in every breeze. We raise the ground floor, use a reinforced concrete structure, 316 stainless, sealed coastal window systems and decks of durable timber or composite. Everything that can be shuttered before a storm is designed to be shuttered quickly.

A house that can be managed from abroad

Mahahual is two hours from Chetumal and four from Cancún, so most owners are not nearby. We add remote monitoring of power, water and security, and hand over a maintenance plan with local staff, so the villa is ready when you arrive and safe when you leave.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Mahahual: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Othón P. Blanco
  • Environmental regime: ZOFEMAT + NOM-022 (mangrove) + municipal
  • Ground: coastal sand over limestone, peat near the mangrove
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A narrow strip between reef and mangrove: federal beach zone in front, protected wetland behind and limited services in between.

  • Locate the ZOFEMAT line and the mangrove edge on a topographic survey before buying; they define the real buildable area.
  • Budget water, power and treatment as part of the house, not as optional extras.

Frequently asked questions

Can a luxury villa in Mahahual run fully off-grid?

Yes. With a properly sized solar and battery system, a cistern and a treatment plant, a villa can run independently, with a generator as backup during long cloudy spells.

Is it safe to build front row after Hurricane Dean?

Yes, with the right design: raised ground floor, reinforced concrete structure, protected openings and a storm protocol. Houses built that way since 2007 have come through later storms well.

Will you work with the architect I already have?

Yes. We can come in purely as builder — reviewing the design, closing the engineering, pricing by line item and executing. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred. We do not make the works conditional on also being the authors of the design.

What warranty do you give?

One year on services and finishes and five years on structure and waterproofing, with as-built drawings, datasheets and equipment manuals. We also offer a six-monthly maintenance plan, which in a coastal climate is what decides how the house reaches its fifth year.
