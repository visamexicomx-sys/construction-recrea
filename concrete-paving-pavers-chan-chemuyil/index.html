Concrete & Pavers in Chan Chemuyil: concrete, stamped concrete and pavers

Where trucks turn, water sits or the look matters in Chan Chemuyil, concrete and pavers outlast asphalt and can let rain soak into the ground. Village streets drain by gravity to low points where water ponds for days; infiltration wells at those points and proper crossfall are the most effective improvements.

Permeable paving and rules in Chan Chemuyil

The area belongs to Tulum municipality; Xcacel-Xcacelito is a protected sanctuary, and works near the coast face strict environmental conditions, while village streets are handled with the municipality and local authorities. Permeable pavers, pervious concrete or grass pavers are chosen where these rules and the site make them the right answer.

Bases for concrete and pavers in Chan Chemuyil

The ground is shallow limestone with sascab pockets and frequent rock outcrops, which makes trenching for drainage and utilities slow and expensive; cavities are possible near the coast. Base thickness, joints and edge restraints are designed for this ground so slabs and pavers stay level.

Plants, materials and access in Chan Chemuyil

About 40 minutes from our yard; hot mix comes from distant plants, so small streets are often better built in hydraulic concrete or pavers using local sascab bases.

Traffic and work windows in Chan Chemuyil

Highway traffic and tour buses peak in high season; works on access points to the highway are scheduled with traffic control and outside peak hours.
