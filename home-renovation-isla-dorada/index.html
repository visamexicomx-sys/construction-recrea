Home renovation in Isla Dorada, Cancún

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Many homes on Isla Dorada were built in the 1990s and 2000s and now need more than a facelift. Renovation here means dealing with lagoon damp, tired docks and dated layouts, while respecting the island association’s rules.

Damp from below

With the lagoon just under the ground floor, older houses show rising damp, salt stains and swollen floors. We open up, find where water enters, add negative-side waterproofing and proper drainage, then finish with materials that tolerate moisture.

Docks, seawalls and lifts

Seawalls crack and docks rot after decades in brackish water. Repairing them within the existing footprint is usually simpler than building new; we check what the original permits cover before work starts.

Opening up older plans

Typical upgrades open closed 1990s layouts towards the lagoon, add large sliding glazing, refresh pools and bring electrics and air conditioning up to current standards.

Realistic budget and programme in Isla Dorada, Cancún

A full renovation on Isla Dorada runs at MXN 10,000 to 20,000 per m² renovated (roughly USD 545 – 1,090/m²). Damp treatment and dock or seawall repair are priced separately.

A typical house takes three to six months. The association’s approval comes first; structural or façade changes need the Benito Juárez licence.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Isla Dorada, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT on the lagoon edge and mangrove (NOM-022) + municipal
  • Ground: fill over sand and lagoon sediment
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: moderate to severe
  • Access: gated access from the Hotel Zone boulevard
  • Development regime: exclusive residential island with association rules

A gated residential island in the Nichupté lagoon: canal frontage, docks and mangrove edges each carry their own rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can damp in an Isla Dorada house be fixed for good?

Usually yes, with negative-side waterproofing, drainage and moisture-tolerant finishes. Paint and sealant alone only hide it.

Can I repair my old dock on Isla Dorada without new permits?

Often, if the repair stays within the existing footprint and original permits. We check them first.
