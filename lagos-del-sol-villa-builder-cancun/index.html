Building in Lagos del Sol, Cancún: villas, condos and renovation, one contract

Lagos del Sol is the gated residential community in southern Cancún, in the Benito Juárez municipality: single-family houses and villas around artificial lakes and gardens, with its own association rules and controlled access. Owners here build new villas, finish condos delivered by developers and renovate homes for rental, usually from abroad. We handle the design review, the licence and the works under one fixed-price contract.

Quick answer: A new premium villa costs about $35,000–$45,000 MXN per m² of construction and an ultra-luxury villa $45,000–$55,000+ MXN per m². A 350 m² villa is roughly $12.2–19.2 million MXN before land and fees.

Lagos del Sol rules we handle for you

  • Design review first. Lagos del Sol reviews every project against its own architectural rules (heights, setbacks, materials, colours, roofs) before the municipality sees it; we draw to those rules from day one.
  • Benito Juárez licence. Land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings, plus Protección Civil and utility feasibility, filed and followed by us.
  • Works rules. Access is controlled: crews, trucks and deliveries are registered, works hours are fixed, and deposits and insurance certificates are required; our crews are registered and insured (IMSS).
  • Lakefront lots. Setbacks from the lakes, fences, pools and terraces facing the water follow the association’s rules; we confirm them before drawing.
  • Environment. Vegetation removal, protected areas and turtle-safe lighting near the beach follow municipal and environmental rules; we file the papers before works start.

Rules differ by lot and change over time; we confirm them with the Lagos del Sol administration before quoting.
