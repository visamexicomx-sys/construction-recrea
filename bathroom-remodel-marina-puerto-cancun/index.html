Bathroom Remodel in the Puerto Cancún Marina

We renovate marina bathrooms with full waterproofing, porcelain or stone and glass enclosures, coordinating every plumbing change with the unit below.

Towers of different ages with large units and penthouses, many owned by boat owners and part-time residents. Projects range from new penthouse fit-outs to full renovations of the first units delivered.

The setting: Marina basin, boat slips

Marine air from the basin and the bay: stainless or aluminium marine-grade fittings, sealed glazing and corrosion protection for railings and outdoor kitchens.

Leaks in towers are expensive

A failed shower in a tower damages the unit below; we waterproof, flood-test and document every bathroom so the administration and your insurer have proof.

Water, scale and salt

Cancún water is hard, so we choose fittings and glass treatments that tolerate scale and add filtration where needed.

Rules in this sector

Tower fit-out manuals plus marina rules for anything near the slips; deliveries and debris use the service entrances only, and noisy work follows the administration's hours. Bathroom works in towers need the administration's approval and coordination with the unit below for any plumbing change.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
