Luxury Villa Renovation in the Riviera Maya

Many of the best addresses on this coast — Playacar, Puerto Aventuras, Akumal, Puerto Cancún, Isla Dorada, Mayakoba — are already built. Buying a 10–20-year-old villa on a great lot and renovating it is often faster and better value than building new. We take tired or salt-damaged houses back to the structure and deliver a villa that competes with new construction, while you are abroad.

Quick answer: In 2026 a cosmetic upgrade runs about $5,000–$9,000 MXN/m², a full interior renovation $12,000–$22,000 MXN/m² and a complete repositioning with structural repair, new layout, impact glazing and systems $22,000–$38,000 MXN/m².

Why renovate instead of building new

Prime lots in established communities are scarce, and the houses on them were often built before today’s standards for glazing, insulation and corrosion protection. A well-planned renovation keeps the lot, the permits already in place and often the structure, and delivers a modern villa in 6–12 months instead of 2–3 years. The key is knowing what is behind the finishes before you commit. That is why we start with a survey, ideally before you buy.

What we usually find in 10–20-year-old coastal villas

  • Rebar corrosion on balconies, roof parapets and slab edges, the result of thin concrete cover and salt air.
  • Waterproofing past its life on flat roofs and terraces, causing leaks, stains and mould inside.
  • Old aluminium windows without impact rating, which leak in storms and need full shutters every hurricane alert.
  • Undersized electrical and A/C for current use, and galvanised plumbing that is corroding from the inside.

Each of these has a price, and we put that price in your hands before you negotiate the purchase or sign a renovation contract.
