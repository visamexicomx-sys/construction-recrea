Villa Renovation in Puerto Cancún: from refresh to full repositioning

We renovate villas on the golf course and the canals of Puerto Cancún: from a cosmetic refresh before the season to a complete repositioning with new envelope, MEP, kitchens, baths, glazing and pool. Puerto Cancún is a gated marina and golf community in the heart of Cancún, with towers on the beach and the marina, villas along the TPC golf course and the canals, 24/7 security and its own rules for works.

Quick answer: Reference ranges in Puerto Cancún: Cosmetic refresh (Paint, floors, fixtures, lighting): $5,000–$9,000; Full interior (Kitchens, baths, floors, A/C, electrical): $12,000–$22,000; Complete repositioning (Layout, facade, glazing, MEP, pool): $22,000–$38,000.
2026 references: Cosmetic refresh: $5,000–$9,000; Full interior: $12,000–$22,000; Complete repositioning: $22,000–$38,000.

What we do

Cosmetic refresh

Paint, floors, lighting, fixtures and carpentry updated without touching the structure.

Full interior

Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, doors, A/C and electrical rebuilt room by room.

Complete repositioning

Layout changes, new facades, impact glazing and terraces for resale or premium rental.

Waterproofing and envelope

Roof membranes, sealants and facade repairs against salt and storms.

Pool and terraces

Pool resurfacing, equipment, decks, pergolas and outdoor kitchens.

MEP and smart home

New wiring, panels, efficient A/C, water treatment and home automation.

What a Puerto Cancún villa usually needs

Many villas here were built in the 2010s with good structure but finishes, glazing and A/C that now look dated next to new towers. Salt air attacks aluminium, metal and sealants, so a renovation usually combines a design update with envelope work that protects the investment for the next decade.

Renovating without losing the season

We phase the works around rentals and your visits, keep the site clean and secured inside the community, and close noisy stages within the allowed working hours.

Puerto Cancún rules we handle

Exterior changes (facade, colours, terraces, pool) normally need the community's architectural approval and a municipal licence with DRO; interior works need notice to security and registered crews.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
