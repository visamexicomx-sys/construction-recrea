Commercial construction in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Works, services and licensing for retail, restaurants and offices with a single contractor.

Felipe Carrillo Puerto is the market town of the Maya zone: families from dozens of surrounding communities come here to shop, bank, see a doctor and catch a bus or the Tren Maya. Commercial demand is steady, local and year-round.

A regional market town

Retail, food, pharmacies, hardware, banking and services serve the town and the communities around it, plus traffic on the 307 between Tulum and Felipe Carrillo Puerto and the roads to Mérida and Valladolid. Units near the centre, the market and the highway junction do best; we check access, loading and parking for the use.

Title, SEMA and the municipality

Commercial lots need full ownership and a registered title first, because much of the surrounding land is ejido. Then the state SEMA authorisation and the municipal licence, followed by Protección Civil and COFEPRIS where food is involved.

Heat, shade and running costs

Shopfronts and car parks bake in the inland afternoon. Deep canopies, shaded parking with trees kept from the lot, insulated roofs and efficient air conditioning reduce running costs, which matter more here than in a tourist zone.

Budget and programme for a commercial unit in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Commercial shell: MXN 7,500 to 12,500 per m², the lowest range we quote, because crews and materials are local and salt exposure is low. Fit-out by use: MXN 6,500 to 16,000 per m².

A 120–400 m² unit takes three to five months. Title check, SEMA and the municipal licence come first; Protección Civil, COFEPRIS for food and the CFE contract run in parallel so the unit opens when works finish.

Scope: from land use to handover with the unit ready to open

We cover the full cycle: land-use and feasibility checks, executive and services design, licence filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical, plumbing, air conditioning, extraction, voice and data, finishes, fixed furniture and signage. Handover comes with as-built drawings, calculation records and the files Protección Civil asks for.

Where the use handles food, we resolve the grease trap, extraction and washing at design stage rather than as a late adjustment: it is the item that causes the most retrofits and the most expensive to correct once the unit is finished.

How we control cost, programme and opening

A line-item budget with quantities and unit rates, changes signed before execution and payments against verified progress. The programme is organised by work fronts so services, finishes and equipment do not obstruct each other, and the opening date is set once the critical filings are resolved, not when the contract is signed.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Most of our clients live outside Mexico and run the business remotely: the system is built for that.

The ground in Felipe Carrillo Puerto: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Felipe Carrillo Puerto
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (state) + municipal; CONANP inside Sian Ka’an
  • Ground: karst limestone with thin red soil
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low

Much of the land is ejido: before buying, confirm full ownership (dominio pleno) and a registered title. The eastern part of the municipality lies in the Sian Ka’an biosphere reserve.

  • Ejido land: confirm dominio pleno and a title registered in the Public Property Registry before paying anything; a transfer of ejido rights is not a deed.
  • Inland heat without sea breeze: orientation, deep eaves, cross-ventilation and roof insulation do more than extra air-conditioning tonnage.

Frequently asked questions

Is Felipe Carrillo Puerto a good market for a new store?

For everyday trade, yes: it is the service centre for the Maya zone, with steady demand from the town and the surrounding communities, and highway and Tren Maya traffic on top.

What should I check before buying a commercial lot here?

Full ownership and a registered title, because much of the land is ejido; then land use, access from the street or highway and parking for the intended use.

Can you open in phases so trading starts earlier?

Yes, where the use and Protección Civil allow it. We define an operable core — access, washrooms, emergency exits and the services on that front — and execute the rest afterwards at hours that do not interfere. It has to be planned from the design stage; improvising it mid-build is expensive.

What filings go with the construction licence?

Depending on the use: land-use certificate, municipal construction licence, Protección Civil sign-off for occupancy and exits, the COFEPRIS operating notice where food is handled, and contracts or load increases with CFE and the water operator. We handle them inside the same contract.
