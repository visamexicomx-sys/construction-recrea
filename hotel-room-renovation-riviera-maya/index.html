What a Room Renovation Includes

Mock-Up Room

One finished room to test finishes, lighting, A/C noise and maintenance before production.

Bathrooms

Tubs to walk-in showers, full waterproofing, new supply and drain lines, fixtures.

Joinery & FF&E

Headboards, wardrobes, desks and minibars made in our workshop, plus loose furniture.

Flooring

LVT, porcelain or engineered wood chosen for salt, sand and housekeeping.

Lighting & Controls

Layered lighting, bedside USB, key-card energy saving and A/C controls.

Quiet Works

Barriers, separate service routes and noisy work outside guest hours.

Mock-up room first

A mock-up room is the cheapest insurance in a renovation: every finish, fixture, light and piece of joinery is installed once, reviewed by the owner, the operator and housekeeping, and corrected before 50 or 200 rooms repeat it. It also fixes the real cost and time per room, so the budget per key is a measured number.

Bathrooms decide the reviews

Most complaints start in the bathroom: low water pressure, mould, cracked grout, tubs guests no longer want. In coastal hotels we replace corroded supply lines, rebuild waterproofing to the slab and convert tubs to walk-in showers with linear drains. Done well, it is the change guests notice first.
