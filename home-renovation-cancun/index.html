Renovating in Cancun: What to Know

Cancun combines beachfront condos in the Hotel Zone with residential areas in the Supermanzanas and Puerto Cancun. Renovating a Hotel Zone condo requires coordinating access and hours with the building administration, and marine salt air demands anti-corrosion finishes and ironwork. Beachfront work may involve ZOFEMAT. We give a fixed line-item budget and, as a builder, coordinate plumbing, electrical and finishes under one roof — ideal for owners who rent or don’t live in Cancun.
