Hotel Construction in Playa del Carmen: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Playa del Carmen is a boutique-hotel town. Hundreds of 10–60 room hotels surround Quinta Avenida, while Playacar hosts the all-inclusives and the west side is filling with economy and 3-star hotels for long-stay workers and budget travellers. We build all three, on tight urban lots and in master-planned communities.

Playa del Carmen 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $9,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $12,000–$18,000; boutique and 4-star $18,000–$25,000; 5-star and luxury $25,000–$42,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

Hotel Classes We Build

Boutique & 4★

Design-led small hotels with rooftop pools, restaurants and a strong sense of place.

Hostels & Economy

Efficient layouts, durable finishes and low running costs for budget travellers and long-stay workers.

All-Inclusive Resorts

Multi-building resorts with pools, restaurants, theatres and back-of-house logistics.

Boutique hotels on tight lots

Lots near Quinta Avenida are narrow and expensive, so every square metre counts: stacked room modules, rooftop pools and bars, and structures that allow an extra floor where zoning permits. Deliveries in pedestrian streets are planned for early mornings.

Economy and long-stay on the west side

Along Avenida 30, Constituyentes and the colonias towards the 307, demand is for clean, efficient hotels and aparthotels at lower rates. Durable finishes and low maintenance matter more than design flourishes.

Solidaridad and the centre's limits

Height and density in the tourist centre are strict, and Quinta Avenida regulates facades and terraces. Playacar adds its association approvals. We confirm what the lot allows before the architect starts.
