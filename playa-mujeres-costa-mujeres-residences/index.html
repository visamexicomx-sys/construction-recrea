Golf and canal lots are sheltered from the surf but humid and close to the water table; foundations, pool shells and docks are designed for fill soils and salt.

What is built here

New villas on golf and canal lots, lots still waiting for construction, and condo buildings with golf or sea views. Clients are mostly North American and Mexico City owners building a second home or a rental villa.
