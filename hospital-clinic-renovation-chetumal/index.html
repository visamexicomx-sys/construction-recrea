Hospital & Clinic Renovation in Chetumal, While Patients Are Treated

Hospitals and clinics in Chetumal cannot stop treating patients to renovate. We work in phases behind infection-control barriers, with negative pressure, dust control and separate routes, and renovate operating theatres, ICUs, patient rooms, imaging and consulting areas, medical gases and power — to COFEPRIS standards.

What We Deliver

Infection Control

Sealed barriers, negative pressure, HEPA and dust monitoring.

Operating Theatres & ICU

Clean-room finishes, laminar flow, gases and isolated power.

Patient Rooms

Rooms, bathrooms and nurse call renewed floor by floor.

Medical Gases

Critical Power

Generators, UPS and isolated circuits for clinical areas.

COFEPRIS

Compliance with sanitary regulations and documentation.

Working next to patients

Every phase starts with a plan agreed with the medical team: which rooms close, how patients and staff move, where the barriers go and when noisy work happens. Barriers are sealed to the slab, work areas held under negative pressure and dust measured daily. Shut-downs of gases and power are planned and tested.

Operating theatres and ICUs

These are the most demanding rooms: seamless washable finishes, laminar airflow, pressure cascades, medical gases, isolated power and testing before use. We build them to the same standard as a new hospital.

Building in Chetumal

Chetumal is the state capital and a border city with year-round local demand from government, education, health and trade. Costs run about 8% below Cancún, land is cheaper, and projects are financed more locally.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Othón P. Blanco; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
