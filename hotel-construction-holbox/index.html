Hotel Construction in Holbox: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Holbox is a car-free island inside the Yum Balam protected area. Hotels are eco-boutique and small beach hotels, with sand streets, limited power and water, and every material arriving by boat.

Quick answer: In 2026, construction alone ranges from roughly $328,000–$473,000 MXN per key for a hostel or economy hotel to $3,432,000–$5,720,000 MXN per key for a 5-star property in Holbox, depending on room size, class and amenities. FF&E adds 15–25% for most hotels.
Holbox 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $12,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $16,000–$22,000; boutique and 4-star $22,000–$31,000; 5-star and luxury $31,000–$52,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

Hotel Classes We Build

Eco-Lodges

Low-impact cabins and treehouse hotels with off-grid water, power and wastewater.

Boutique & 4★

Design-led small hotels with rooftop pools, restaurants and a strong sense of place.

Eco-boutique by necessity

Small hotels with palapa roofs, local timber and natural ventilation fit both the rules and the guests.

Limited utilities

Power and water are constrained; solar, water treatment and sealed wastewater systems are standard.

Boat logistics

Everything crosses from Chiquilá; we plan loads, light equipment and crews that live on the island during works.
