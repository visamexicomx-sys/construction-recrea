Condo Renovation in Punta Cancún

Punta Cancún condos are often in rental programmes; we renovate them in phases between bookings so the unit keeps earning.

Mid-rise and high-rise condos from several decades, many units in rental programmes; owners renovate to compete on listings, often in short windows between bookings.

The setting: The elbow: bay and open Caribbean

Exposed to wind from two sides and to heavy foot and vehicle traffic; noise control, durable finishes and corrosion-resistant fittings matter more than anywhere else in the zone.

Towers, administrations and work hours

Every tower has its own regulations: approval of the work plan, set hours for noisy work, booking of the service lift, protection of common areas, deposits and insurance certificates. We prepare that package before starting, so the administration has no reason to stop the job.

Older towers, salt and two waterfronts

Many Hotel Zone towers are 30 to 40 years old and face the sea on one side and the lagoon on the other. We check risers, the electrical feed and balcony concrete before closing walls, and use stainless, marine-grade hardware and sealed frames that do not rust or stain.

Rules in this sector

Buildings here have tight schedules for noisy work and deliveries because of guests and traffic; we plan demolition and debris removal in the early hours allowed by each administration. Works inside towers need the administration's written approval, registered and insured crews, booked service-lift slots and protection of corridors and lifts.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
