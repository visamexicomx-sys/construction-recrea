Retail Store Remodel in Tulum: Reopen Fast, or Never Close

Your shop in Tulum is trading but looks tired, the A/C cannot cope, the electrics trip or the concept has changed. We remodel existing stores, showrooms and offices in tight windows — nights, weekends or a short closure — with everything prefabricated in our workshop so the time on site is as short as possible.

Quick answer: Retail Store Remodel in Tulum: Reopen Fast, or Never Close — 2026 prices: Refresh: paint, lighting, minor finishes: $2,000–$5,100 MXN; Retail refit: finishes, joinery, storefront, A/C: $9,000–$20,300 MXN; Office refit: partitions, ceilings, cabling, A/C: $10,200–$22,600 MXN; Restaurant refit including kitchen and extraction: $22,600–$50,800 MXN.

Refresh, refit or change of use

  • Refresh — paint, lighting, minor finishes and repairs: days, usually without closing.
  • Refit — new finishes, joinery, storefront and A/C: four to eight weeks for a retail unit, or phased at night.
  • Change of use — a new activity needs a new operating licence and often new services: drainage, grease, extraction to the roof, more power, accessibility and Civil Protection. Check feasibility before you commit to the concept.

Keeping the store open

Most of the time on a remodel is lost on site. We measure, design and prefabricate joinery, counters and storefront elements in our workshop, then install in short windows. Dust screens, floor protection and a clean, safe shop each morning let you keep selling. Shutdowns of power and water are planned with you and the landlord in advance.

Remodelling in Tulum

  • Av. Tulum through town: the main retail street, with small plazas and ground-floor shops
  • Aldea Zama and La Veleta: mixed-use buildings with shops and cafés under apartments
  • Highway 307 toward the new airport and Region 15: land for plazas, services and car-related retail
  • The beach road: boutique retail and restaurants under strict environmental and height rules

In Tulum the infrastructure decides the plaza: power and water capacity are short in many areas, so a transformer, a cistern and an own wastewater treatment plant are often part of the budget. Height limits are low, and lots in the jungle need an environmental review before clearing. Costs run about 13% above Cancún because of logistics and labour.
