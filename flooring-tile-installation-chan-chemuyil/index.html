Flooring & Tile in Chan Chemuyil: porcelain, stone, chukum and pool decks

We install floors and tile in Chan Chemuyil on levelled, properly bonded bases: porcelain and large format, marble, chukum, microcement, terraces and pool decks. Chan Chemuyil is a small residential community near Xcacel, with modest family homes, a growing number of rental houses and lots being built by local families and newcomers.

Chan Chemuyil, 2026 references: Porcelain up to 60×60: $320–$480; Large format 120×60 / 120×120: $480–$750; Marble / natural stone: $580–$1,000.

Floors and Surfaces We Install

Marble & Natural Stone

Marble, travertine and local stone, sealed and polished, with the right adhesive to avoid staining.

Terraces & Pool Decks

Anti-slip, cool-touch stone and porcelain for outdoor areas, with falls and joints for sun and rain.

Levelling & Prep

Removal of old floors, self-levelling screeds and waterproofing before any finish is laid.

Floors for the homes in Chan Chemuyil

The village is a short distance from the sea, so salt is moderate but humidity is high, and mould prevention matters more than marine-grade metal. Formats and materials are matched to how the property is used, from family homes to high-turnover rentals.

Heat, humidity and floors in Chan Chemuyil

Chan Chemuyil is about 40 minutes from our workshops and right on Highway 307, so deliveries are simple and costs stay close to the Riviera average. Adhesives, movement joints and outdoor finishes are chosen for these conditions so floors stay flat and bonded.

Rules, licences and administration in Chan Chemuyil

It falls under Tulum municipality; licences are municipal, and lots near the coast and Xcacel turtle sanctuary have environmental restrictions for exterior works and lighting.

Water supply in Chan Chemuyil

Supply is municipal but irregular, so most homes have cisterns and rooftop tanks, and new bathrooms are designed for pumps and filters.

Rentals and scheduling in Chan Chemuyil

Rentals are mostly long-term, which makes it easier to schedule work with the occupants.
