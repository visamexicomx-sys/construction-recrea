Reverse Osmosis and Water Purification in Playa del Carmen

Ask anyone who owns property in Playa del Carmen about water and you hear the same things: very hard water that scales showerheads, heaters and A/C coils; pressure drops and cuts in parts of town at peak times; and, outside the network, wells that turn brackish the closer you are to the sea. We build the systems that fix it, from a villa softener and RO kitchen tap to plants for condos and resorts in the north corridor.

Playa del Carmen 2026: whole-house filtration, softening and drinking-water RO for a villa $90,000–$350,000 MXN; system with cistern, pressure and UV for a large villa or small condo $250,000–$900,000 MXN; brackish-well RO for a hotel or condo $900,000–$4.5M MXN; seawater desalination for the north corridor $3.5M–$22M MXN.

Hard water costs money

Playa’s water comes from a limestone aquifer and is rich in calcium carbonate. Scale shortens the life of water heaters, A/C condensate systems, pool equipment and shower fittings, and leaves white marks on glass and stone. A properly sized softener protects the whole house; a small RO unit gives drinking water without buying bottles. For hotels and condos, the savings in maintenance usually pay the system back.

Storage and pressure

In parts of Playa the network pressure drops at peak times. A cistern with a pressure system and automatic switch-over keeps the house or building supplied, and is the base for any treatment. We size storage for at least a day of use and place filters and UV after the cistern so the stored water stays clean.
