Restaurant and Bar Fit-Out in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is a restaurant town: Quinta Avenida from Calle 1 to Calle 40 and beyond, the Calle 38 dining strip, the bars of Calle 12, the local favourites on Avenida 10 and 30, and beach and golf restaurants in Playacar. Units are small, rents are high and neighbours are close, so the kitchen, the extraction and the acoustics decide whether a concept works. We build them so they open on time and stay open.

Playa del Carmen 2026: premium dining room and bar $18,000–$32,000 MXN/m²; rooftop and signature venues $30,000–$50,000 MXN/m²; commercial kitchen package $1.2M–$5M MXN. Quinta Avenida units often add costs for night works and restricted deliveries.

Kitchens in Small Units

Compact, efficient kitchens with extraction routed through tight urban buildings.

Small units, big kitchens

A typical Playa restaurant unit is narrow and deep, often with apartments above. The challenge is getting extraction to the roof without crossing other owners’ units, fitting a grease trap that the water utility accepts, and finding enough power for modern equipment. We survey before you sign the lease and design the kitchen first, so the dining room is built around a kitchen that works.

Noise and licences in Solidaridad

In Playa, noise complaints are the fastest way to lose a licence. We design acoustic ceilings, sealed facades and sound-isolated equipment for bars on Calle 12 and rooftops near residential buildings. On the construction side we prepare the Solidaridad licence or notice with DRO, Protección Civil requirements and as-built plans, and coordinate with your gestor for operating and alcohol licences.
