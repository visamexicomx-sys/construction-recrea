Steel Stairs, Railings, Pergolas and Gates in Playa del Carmen

Our metal workshop is in Playa del Carmen, and architectural metalwork is where that matters most: you can see the pieces before they are finished, changes take days, and installers are minutes away. We make floating stairs for Playacar and Corasol villas, glass and stainless railings for centre penthouses and rooftops, pergolas and louvres for terraces and gates for gated communities across Playa.

Made down the road

Architectural metalwork is full of decisions: stair rise and tread, railing profile, finish, how a gate meets the wall. Because we make it in our own Playa del Carmen workshop, architects and owners can see prototypes, approve finishes on real pieces and change details without waiting for freight. Installation and later service come from the same team.

Community and coast

Playacar, Corasol and Mayakoba regulate gates, railings and pergolas visible from the street; we prepare submissions with drawings and samples. Near the beach we use 316 stainless and marine aluminium; on rooftops pergolas are anchored for uplift and drain without harming waterproofing.
