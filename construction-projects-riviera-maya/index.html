How to read these durations

The durations shown are time on site and exclude the permit phase, which in Solidaridad typically runs 6 to 12 weeks, and considerably longer in the municipality of Tulum once an environmental procedure is involved. In our contracts the construction calendar starts with the licence in hand, and payments follow accepted stages rather than dates.

What we document on every build

  • A weekly report with dated photographs and progress by line item.
  • Sign-off on concealed services before plastering — the point after which everything is closed up.
  • Testing: concrete cylinders per pour, and a 72-hour water test on bathrooms and the roof.
  • Handover against a signed snag list, with the warranty folder.
