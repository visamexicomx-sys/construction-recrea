Road Construction on Holbox: private roads, streets and access roads

We build private roads, subdivision streets, access roads and yards on Holbox as one package, from survey and earthworks to pavement, drainage and signage. Holbox has sand streets used by golf carts and bicycles, a growing number of hotels and villas, and demand for durable, permeable street surfaces that fit the island’s character.

Holbox, 2026 references: Unpaved access road: $360–$680; Asphalt road, light traffic: $980–$1,600; Asphalt road, heavy traffic: $1,400–$2,300.

What We Build

Drainage & Infiltration

Crossfall, gutters, kerb inlets and infiltration wells, since most of the region has no storm sewer.

Private & Access Roads

Access roads to villas, hotels, ranches and resorts, including long runs through jungle with passing places and turning areas.

The ground under a road on Holbox

The island is sand over shell and limestone with a very high water table, so conventional asphalt structures are rarely suitable; pavers, stabilised sand and concrete are the realistic options. The pavement design and the base thickness are set for these conditions after the soil study, not copied from another site.

Permits for a road on Holbox

Holbox is inside the Yum Balam protected area; the island limits cars, and any street or site work faces strict environmental rules and coordination with the municipality of Lázaro Cárdenas. We map these approvals during design so that construction starts with every connection and clearing authorised.

Rain and drainage on Holbox

Streets flood after rain because the water table is near the surface; grades, permeable paving and avoiding hard surfaces are the main tools.

Plants, materials and access on Holbox

All materials cross by ferry from Chiquilá and move on the island with small vehicles, which makes Holbox the most expensive zone for paving and earthworks.
