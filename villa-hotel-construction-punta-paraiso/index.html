Villa and hotel construction in Punta Paraíso

Boutique hotels and rental villas on a private seafront between Playa del Carmen and Puerto Aventuras: boundary survey, SEMARNAT filing, coastal construction and an operable handover.

Punta Paraíso offers what larger resorts cannot: a private seafront with protected coastal vegetation and few neighbours, a short drive from Playa del Carmen. Its hospitality product is the boutique hotel or rental villa that sells privacy and nature.

Protected vegetation shapes the plan

The coastal strip carries protected vegetation that the SEMARNAT file must document before any clearing. We survey and mark it, then arrange rooms and pool behind it, using the vegetation as a natural screen between guests and the beach.

Privacy as the brand

With low density and the highway far back, every key can feel secluded. We design separate pavilions or a low, long building with private terraces, keep arrival discreet from the road and avoid views between rooms.

Rock, sand and a high water table

The ground is limestone and sand with a high water table near the sea. Foundations, pools and plant rooms are designed for it, with 316 stainless and sealed glazing for the severe salt.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Punta Paraíso

For a boutique hotel or rental villa in Punta Paraíso we work in a range of MXN 20,000 to 35,000 per built m² (roughly USD 1,090 – 1,910/m²). Severe salt, a high water table and the environmental survey place it with the front-row coast.

An eight to twenty key hotel takes thirteen to eighteen months, starting with the boundary and vegetation survey and the SEMARNAT file.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Punta Paraíso: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Solidaridad
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT on the coastal strip
  • Ground: limestone and sand
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: off the federal highway
  • Development regime: low density

Frequently asked questions

Can I clear the coastal vegetation in Punta Paraíso for more rooms?

Protected coastal vegetation must be documented and respected under the environmental file. Rooms go behind it, and it becomes part of the guest experience.

What kind of hotel works in Punta Paraíso?

A small boutique hotel or rental villas that sell privacy and nature, close to Playa del Carmen but away from the crowds.
