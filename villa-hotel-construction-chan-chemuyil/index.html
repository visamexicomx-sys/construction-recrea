Villa and hotel construction in Chan Chemuyil

Guest-houses, small hotels and rental villas in an established community between Akumal and Tulum: design, SEMA and municipal filings, construction and an operable handover.

Chan Chemuyil is an established residential community on the inland side of the highway, minutes from Xel-Há, Akumal and the beaches of Tulum. Its large wooded lots suit guest-houses, small hotels and long-stay rentals that sell quiet and jungle rather than a beachfront.

The trees decide where the building sits

Lots here carry mature native vegetation, and the state SEMA authorisation starts from a vegetation inventory. We map the trees worth keeping before the architect places a single room, then arrange the keys around them; guests pay for that canopy, and the authority asks for it anyway.

A product for long stays and nearby attractions

Guests come for Xel-Há, the cenotes, Akumal’s turtles and Tulum’s beaches, often for a week or more. Kitchenettes, shaded terraces, a pool in a clearing and reliable air conditioning matter more than a lobby. We size the brief to that demand instead of copying a beach hotel.

Karst and unpaved access

The ground is karst with voids, and some internal streets are unpaved. A soil study with void verification comes before the foundation price, and concrete pours and deliveries are planned for the dry months, because a rainy week can stop trucks on the access road.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Chan Chemuyil

For a small hotel or guest-house in Chan Chemuyil we work in a range of MXN 16,000 to 30,000 per built m² (roughly USD 870 – 1,635/m²). Moderate salt exposure keeps the specification below the front-row coast; access surcharges for freight and concrete pumping are part of the budget.

A six to sixteen key property takes eleven to sixteen months of construction. The SEMA authorisation comes first, then the Tulum municipal licence; sanitary filings and Protección Civil close at the end, on completed works.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Chan Chemuyil: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst with native vegetation
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: moderate

Large lots with mature trees: the vegetation inventory drives where the house can sit.

  • State SEMA authorisation comes before the municipal licence: without it the town hall will not open a file.
  • Moderate salt exposure: 316 stainless outdoors, 304 acceptable indoors.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chan Chemuyil a good place for a small hotel?

For guests who want quiet and nature close to Xel-Há, Akumal and Tulum, yes. Lots are large and cheaper than on the beach, and the product that works is a guest-house or small hotel with long-stay comforts.

Can I clear the lot to fit more rooms?

Only within what the SEMA authorisation allows, which starts from a vegetation inventory. Designing around the best trees is usually both faster to approve and better for the guest.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
