Wine rooms and cinemas for homes on Holbox

Holbox sits between the Gulf and a shallow lagoon: very humid, salty and exposed to northers in winter, so wood, metal and paint need extra protection and frequent maintenance. Most projects here are villas and penthouses where the cellar or cinema is a selling point, so design and finish quality come first.

Condensation and heat on Holbox

Everything arrives by ferry from Chiquilá and moves on sand streets by golf cart or small truck, about three hours plus the crossing from our workshops, so jobs are planned in tight batches. Tropical humidity is the real enemy: vapour barriers, insulated glass and dew-point control stop condensation and mould behind racks and panels.

Rentals and scheduling on Holbox

High season runs from winter to summer holidays, so most hotels schedule renovation and maintenance in the quieter autumn months.

Water and utilities on Holbox

Fresh water is scarce and supplied irregularly, so bathrooms and gardens are designed with low-flow fittings, cisterns and treated greywater wherever possible.
