Car Wash Construction in Cancún: Express Tunnels, Self-Serve & Detailing

Salt, sand and rain keep cars in Cancún dirty, and membership car washes have become one of the steadiest small-format investments. We build express tunnel car washes, self-serve bays and detailing centres for investors and franchisees: site, building, tunnel equipment installed with the supplier, water recycling, vacuums, queuing lanes and the permits for discharge.

Site and stacking first

A tunnel's revenue depends on how many cars can queue off the street and how fast they get through. We test the site layout for stacking, turning and exit before design, then size the tunnel length and equipment to the volume you plan.

Water is the permit and the margin

Water and discharge decide both the permits and the operating cost. Reclaim tanks, separators and filtration let a wash reuse most of its water, meet discharge limits and keep running when the network is short.

Building in Cancún

Cancún is the largest market in the state: vertical condos along Bonampak, Malecón Tajamar, Puerto Cancún and the Hotel Zone, mid-rise buildings in Cumbres, Arbolada and Huayacán, and the most complete supply chain and labour pool in the region. Heights and densities are set by the urban development programme, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Benito Juárez; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
