Earthworks and Site Preparation on Limestone and Sascab

Every project here starts on the same ground: thin topsoil, hard limestone, pockets of sascab and, sometimes, caverns. Getting the platform right decides whether floors crack, pools leak and roads sink. We handle clearing, stripping, rock excavation, compacted fills, building platforms and levels, and investigate and treat cavities before anything is built on top.

Quick answer: In 2026 clearing vegetation and stripping organic soil costs about $18–$45 MXN/m² depending on density and access. On forest land a SEMARNAT land-use change authorisation is needed before any clearing.
2026 prices: clearing and stripping $18–$45 MXN/m²; excavation in soil or sascab $120–$250 MXN/m³; excavation in limestone rock with breaker $280–$650 MXN/m³; compacted sascab fill in 20 cm layers to 95% Proctor $320–$520 MXN/m³ supplied; spoil haulage $90–$180 MXN/m³. Forestry permits and blasting, where allowed, are separate.

Haulage & Disposal

Spoil removed to authorised sites and reuse of excavated rock as fill where it meets specification.

Karst: why site preparation matters more here

The Yucatán Peninsula is a slab of limestone dissolved by water into fissures, caverns and cenotes. Most sites are solid, but a hidden void under a footing or a road can cause settlement years later. Where the geology or the neighbours' experience suggest it, we probe with the drill, use ground-penetrating radar or geophysics, and treat what we find with concrete, rock fill or a bridging slab designed by the structural engineer. On every site the platform is built in compacted layers and tested, not just dumped and levelled.

Permits and environmental rules

  • Clearing jungle on forest land requires a land-use change authorisation (CUSTF) from SEMARNAT and often an environmental impact statement.
  • Municipal licences for earthworks and the construction itself, plus local rules on protected trees.
  • Spoil and debris must go to authorised disposal sites, with receipts.
  • Cenotes and caves on or near the site carry extra protection and setbacks, which we flag early.
