Renovating in Puerto Morelos: What to Know

Puerto Morelos is a quiet eco town (its own municipality since 2016) facing the Reef National Park, more affordable than Cancun or Playa del Carmen and with growing vacation-rental demand. Renovating here aims at a natural finish and maximizing Airbnb ROI. Salt air and humidity demand anti-corrosion finishes and reinforced waterproofing. Interior finishes don’t require a permit; beachfront work may require ZOFEMAT. We give a fixed line-item budget and coordinate everything under one roof — ideal for owners who rent or don’t live in Puerto Morelos.
