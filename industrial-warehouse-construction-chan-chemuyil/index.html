Industrial warehouse construction in Chan Chemuyil

Steel frame, industrial slab and the full permit file with a single contractor.

Chan Chemuyil sits on highway 307 between Akumal and Tulum, with Xel-Há and several resorts nearby. Warehouses here serve contractors, distributors and the parks and hotels along this stretch, on karst ground with moderate salt.

A logistics point between Akumal and Tulum

Building-materials depots, distributors and service companies for the parks and hotels save hours by being here rather than in Playa or Tulum. We check truck access from the highway, internal roads and the yard needed for the vehicles each use requires.

SEMA and the vegetation

Even an industrial lot needs the state SEMA authorisation before the Tulum municipal licence, starting from the vegetation on the lot. We plan the footprint and yard around what must be kept and keep a planted buffer towards neighbouring homes.

Karst under heavy racking

Karst with voids and heavy slab loads is a risky combination. We run a soil study with void verification, design the slab against the racking and forklift loads, and plan pours for the dry months because unpaved access can stop concrete trucks.

Budget and programme for a warehouse in Chan Chemuyil

Steel-frame warehouse with an industrial slab: MXN 7,500 to 12,500 per m². Moderate salt allows standard galvanised steel; void treatment, if the study finds it, and access surcharges are priced separately.

A 500–1,500 m² warehouse takes six to nine months. SEMA and the Tulum licence take three to five months together, including Protección Civil; the highway access and the CFE load increase run in parallel.

Scope: from the soil study to a warehouse in operation

We cover the soil mechanics study, architectural and structural design, an industrial slab calculated against the real racking layout, roof and envelope, electrical installation with back-up, plumbing, fire protection, offices, manoeuvring yard and site works. We file land use, licence and Protección Civil sign-off with the responsible engineer, and manage the load increase with CFE.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, structural and slab calculation records, and equipment manuals.

What actually decides the cost of a warehouse

Not the square metres: the slab loading, the clear structural span, the usable height and the yard. A larger span saves columns and improves operations but makes the structure more expensive; a badly sized slab forces demolition with racking installed. So we start from the operating layout — racking, forklifts, truck flow — and size from there, not the other way round.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. We work with operators and investors who do not live in the area and need to follow the works remotely.

  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst with native vegetation
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: moderate

Large lots with mature trees: the vegetation inventory drives where the house can sit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chan Chemuyil a good place for a warehouse?

For businesses serving the stretch between Akumal and Tulum, yes: highway access, lower land cost than the coast and less salt. A soil study comes first because of the karst.

Do I need SEMA authorisation for an industrial lot?

Yes. In the Tulum municipality the state SEMA authorisation comes before the municipal licence for any construction, starting from the vegetation inventory.

What structural span is best?

The one that allows the operation with the fewest columns within budget. In a warehouse with selective racking, spans of twenty to thirty metres are usually the balance point; beyond that, steel grows faster than the operational benefit. It is decided with the layout in hand, not from a catalogue.

Do you include the manoeuvring yard and site works?

Yes, and we budget them as their own line items because they weigh. Pavement with a thickness for tractor-trailers, storm drainage, lighting, a gatehouse, fencing and access are real costs that sometimes approach thirty per cent of the total and are forgotten in quick quotations.
