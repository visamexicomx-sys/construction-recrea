A floor every 7 to 10 days

The schedule of a tower is the cycle of its typical floor. We plan formwork, post-tensioning, MEP sleeves and inspections so each floor repeats in 7–10 days, with facade and interiors following a few floors below. That predictability is what lets a developer sell with a delivery date.

Foundations and the water table

Cancún sits on karst limestone with water a metre or two below the surface. Basements need dewatering, waterproofing and uplift design; often a podium above ground is cheaper per parking space. We compare both before the design is fixed.

Building in Cancún

Cancún is the largest market in the state: vertical condos along Bonampak, Malecón Tajamar, Puerto Cancún and the Hotel Zone, mid-rise buildings in Cumbres, Arbolada and Huayacán, and the most complete supply chain and labour pool in the region. Heights and densities are set by the urban development programme, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Benito Juárez; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
