Keeping a car park dry in Tulum

There is almost no storm sewer, so water must be infiltrated on site; permeable paving, swales and infiltration wells are the usual design, always located away from cenotes and septic or treatment systems. Grades, inlets and infiltration are designed for these conditions so storms drain in minutes.

Building around traffic in Tulum

Rainy-season downpours from June to October stop earthworks for days; the busiest tourist months from December to April slow deliveries on the federal highway, so heavy works are best planned for May and November. We phase the works around this pattern so the business keeps operating.

Permits and authorities in Tulum

Tulum’s urban development plan sets minimum absorption areas on each lot, so hard paving is limited and permeable solutions are often required; clearing jungle needs SEMARNAT authorisation, and near the National Park extra environmental review applies.

Ground conditions in Tulum

Plots sit on thin soil over porous limestone with caves and cenotes nearby, so cavity checks matter more here than anywhere else on the coast; the beach road zone is sand over rock with a high water table.
