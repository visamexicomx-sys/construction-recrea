Corrosion Protection and Repair of Steel in Cancún

Cancún’s Hotel Zone is one of the most corrosive environments in Mexico: a strip of land with the open Caribbean on one side and a lagoon on the other, constant wind and salt spray. Resorts there fight rust on canopies, railings, stairs, rooftop equipment frames and pool structures every season, and the problem reaches the condo towers of Puerto Cancún and the warehouses inland. We inspect, repair and protect steel with marine systems and reinforce what has lost section.

C5 conditions on Kukulcán

On the seaward side of the Hotel Zone, steel lives in what coating standards classify as very high corrosivity. Single-coat enamels last a season; we specify blasting, a zinc-rich primer, an epoxy build coat and a UV-resistant polyurethane, with film thickness measured and recorded. Where parts can be removed, hot-dip galvanizing plus paint (duplex) gives the longest life.

Working in operating resorts and towers

We phase work by area, contain blasting dust, use low-odour coatings near guest areas and schedule noisy work at agreed hours. For HOAs we provide inspection reports with priorities and budgets that the administration can present to owners and insurers, then execute in stages.
