Hotel Maintenance Contracts and Hurricane Recovery

After a hurricane, the hotels that reopen first are the ones that had a contractor already on call, materials reserved and damage documented for the insurer. Between storms, the same team keeps roofs, facades, pools and plant rooms in shape so small issues never become closures.

2026 costs: preventive maintenance contracts $60,000–$400,000 MXN per month depending on size and scope; hurricane response retainer from $40,000 MXN per month; repairs priced per event with pre-agreed rates.

What the Contract Covers

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspections of roofs, facades, waterproofing, pools and plant rooms.

Capital Repairs

Planned repairs and replacements budgeted year by year.

Rapid Response

Crews on call for leaks, failures and urgent repairs.

Hurricane Preparedness

Pre-storm checks, securing rooftop equipment, shutters and drainage.

Post-Storm Recovery

Damage assessment, temporary protection and fast reconstruction.

Insurer Documentation

Photo records, damage reports and cost breakdowns for claims.

Being first in line after a storm

When a hurricane hits the coast, every contractor is overbooked within hours. Hotels with a retainer get priority crews, pre-agreed rates and reserved materials, and a damage report the insurer accepts. We plan the response with your engineering team before the season starts.
