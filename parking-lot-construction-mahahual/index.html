Keeping a car park dry in Mahahual

A high water table and mangroves behind the town limit infiltration, so streets rely on grades and swales; storm surge can flood the whole strip during hurricanes. Grades, inlets and infiltration are designed for these conditions so storms drain in minutes.

Building around traffic in Mahahual

Cruise ship days fill the streets near the port, so works there are scheduled on non-cruise days; the hurricane season is the main risk for open works. We phase the works around this pattern so the business keeps operating.

Ground conditions in Mahahual

The town is on sand and coastal limestone between sea and mangrove; bases need geotextiles or thicker granular layers, and salt and storms attack pavement edges.

Plants, materials and access in Mahahual

About four hours from our yard; hot mix travels a long way from the south, so concrete, pavers and stabilised bases are often more practical and more durable in salt and sand.
