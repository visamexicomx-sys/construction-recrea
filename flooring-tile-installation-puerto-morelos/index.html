Flooring & Tile in Puerto Morelos: porcelain, stone, chukum and pool decks

We install floors and tile in Puerto Morelos on levelled, properly bonded bases: porcelain and large format, marble, chukum, microcement, terraces and pool decks. Puerto Morelos mixes the quiet fishing town by the beach, new gated communities and condos on both sides of the highway and jungle lots along the Ruta de los Cenotes, with many owners renovating older beach houses.

Puerto Morelos, 2026 references: Porcelain up to 60×60: $310–$460; Large format 120×60 / 120×120: $460–$710; Marble / natural stone: $540–$980.

Floors and Surfaces We Install

Terraces & Pool Decks

Anti-slip, cool-touch stone and porcelain for outdoor areas, with falls and joints for sun and rain.

Chukum & Microcement

Seamless Mayan chukum and microcement floors, walls and showers, sealed for wet areas.

Levelling & Prep

Removal of old floors, self-levelling screeds and waterproofing before any finish is laid.

Floors for the homes in Puerto Morelos

Houses by the sea get salt and wind, while the jungle side is humid and shaded, so the same house type needs different paint, metal and garden specifications depending on the side of the highway. Formats and materials are matched to how the property is used, from family homes to high-turnover rentals.

Heat, humidity and floors in Puerto Morelos

Puerto Morelos is about 30 minutes from our workshops and close to Cancún suppliers, so materials arrive quickly and crews can return daily. Adhesives, movement joints and outdoor finishes are chosen for these conditions so floors stay flat and bonded.

Rules, licences and administration in Puerto Morelos

Puerto Morelos has been its own municipality since 2016 and issues its own licences; the beach strip is under ZOFEMAT and the reef national park, and gated communities add HOA rules.

Rentals and scheduling in Puerto Morelos

Rental demand is growing but calmer than in Cancún or Tulum, which makes it easier to schedule work in occupied homes.

Water supply in Puerto Morelos

Town water reaches most of the coast, but jungle lots often depend on wells and cisterns, so filtration and storage are part of most bathroom and garden projects.
