Water and fittings for a bathroom in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Water comes from the municipal network and wells, often hard, so filters and simple softeners are common recommendations. That is why we size softeners, filters and pumps with the bathroom, and choose fittings that tolerate scale.

Humidity, salt and finishes in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Being inland, there is no salt, but heat and rainy-season humidity are high, so ventilation, anti-mould paint and good roof waterproofing matter most. Grout, paint, extraction and metal fittings are specified for these conditions so the bathroom still looks new after several seasons.

Rentals and scheduling in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Most homes are owner-occupied, so work is scheduled with the family living in the house.

Materials, crews and access in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

The town is about two hours from our workshops; we combine local materials with planned deliveries, and labour costs are lower than on the coast.

Rules, licences and administration in Felipe Carrillo Puerto

Licences come from Felipe Carrillo Puerto municipality, with simpler procedures than the coastal towns and few community rules.
