Home renovation in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Malecón Tajamar is mostly condo towers and new residences, so renovation here usually means apartments and penthouses: personalising developer finishes, upgrading kitchens and bathrooms and fixing what the first years of use revealed.

Condo rules and the building

Every job runs under the condo’s regulations: works hours, lift bookings, debris removal and insurance. We submit the scope to the administration, protect common areas and keep neighbours informed.

Personalising developer units

Owners replace standard kitchens and bathrooms, add built-in joinery, upgrade lighting and automation and change floors. We check slab loads and plumbing routes before moving wet areas.

Terraces and leaks

Lagoon-facing terraces and rooftop pools are where leaks start. We lift tiles, renew waterproofing and drainage and set finishes with the right falls, so water stays outside.

Realistic budget and programme in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

Renovating an apartment or penthouse in Malecón Tajamar runs at MXN 8,500 to 17,000 per m² renovated (roughly USD 460 – 925/m²). Terrace waterproofing and rooftop pool repairs are priced separately.

A typical unit takes six weeks to four months. The administration’s approval comes first; interior works rarely need a municipal licence unless structure or façade changes.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: municipal + SEMARNAT where mangrove remains
  • Ground: fill over lagoon sediment
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: moderate
  • Access: urban avenues next to Puerto Cancún
  • Development regime: high-rise residential and mixed use on the lagoon

A lagoon-front district whose 2016 mangrove dispute made environmental compliance closely watched here.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit to renovate my condo in Malecón Tajamar?

Usually only the administration’s approval for interior works. Structural or façade changes also need a Benito Juárez licence.

Why do lagoon-facing terraces leak in Malecón Tajamar?

Usually because of failed waterproofing under the tiles or poor falls to the drains. The fix is to lift, re-waterproof and re-lay, not to seal the joints.
