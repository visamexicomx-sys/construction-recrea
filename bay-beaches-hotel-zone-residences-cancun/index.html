Which side of the boulevard: Caribbean bay facing Isla Mujeres

The bay has calm water and little surf, but buildings still take salt spray and hurricane winds; concrete balconies and steel railings of this age often show chloride corrosion.

What is built here

Most residential buildings on this stretch date from the 1980s and 1990s: generous units, original kitchens and bathrooms, single glazing and old risers. Many owners are long-time residents or families renting monthly rather than nightly.
