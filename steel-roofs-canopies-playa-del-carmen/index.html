Steel Canopies, Arcotechos and Roofs in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen’s roofs are as varied as the town: arcotechos over schools and courts in the colonias, parking canopies for condos in Playacar and the north side, roofs over terraces and rooftop bars in the centre, and workshops and storage yards on the west side. We fabricate in our Playa workshop and design every roof for hurricane uplift.

Playa del Carmen 2026, installed: arcotecho $1,050–$1,900 MXN/m²; steel-frame roof with sheeting $1,700–$3,300 MXN/m²; condo parking canopy (solar-ready) $2,900–$5,200 MXN/m²; terrace and rooftop roofs with premium finishes $4,500–$9,000 MXN/m².

Rooftops in the centre

Many Playa buildings sell their rooftop: bars, pools, lounges. Covering part of it adds usable space year-round, but rooftops are the most wind-exposed place in town. We check the existing slab, design anchorage into the structure without damaging waterproofing, and choose sheeting, louvres or panels rated for uplift, with drainage that keeps water off the terrace.

Fast, local, low disruption

Because we fabricate in Playa, roofs are made to measure and delivered quickly. For schools we work in holidays; for condos we coordinate with the administration; for terraces on busy streets we lift early in the morning in small sections.
