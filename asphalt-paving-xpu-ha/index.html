Asphalt Paving in Xpu-Há: hot-mix paving, overlays and driveways

Hot-mix asphalt in Xpu-Há only lasts when the mix arrives hot, the base is sound and the thickness is what the contract says. About 30 minutes from our yard; hot-mix trucks reach the highway easily but narrow private roads limit the size of pavers and rollers, so long roads are paved in shorter daily sections.

Xpu-Ha, 2026 references: Hot mix on existing base: $380–$610; Overlay on old asphalt: $330–$530; Milling: $80–$170.

When to pave in Xpu-Há

Hotels and residents depend on a single access road, so works keep one lane open and are timed around check-in and deliveries; the rainy season makes sascab work difficult from June to October. Laying is scheduled with the plant and around these windows, so the mix is compacted at the right temperature.

Preparing the base in Xpu-Há

Between the highway and the beach the ground is limestone with cenotes and thin soil, changing to sand near the coast; sascab roads here rut and wash out quickly without a proper base and crossfall. We check and correct the base for these conditions before any asphalt is laid.

Rain and drainage in Xpu-Há

Unpaved roads lose material with every storm and turn into channels; crossfall, side drains and permeable or sealed surfaces stop the yearly cycle of regrading.

What gets built in Xpu-Há

Xpu-Há has long private access roads through the jungle to beach hotels, villas and condos, many still in sascab, plus car parks and service yards for resorts along the highway.
