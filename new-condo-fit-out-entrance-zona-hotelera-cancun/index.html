New Condo Fit-Out in the Hotel Zone Entrance

New towers at the entrance of the Hotel Zone are delivered in shell or with basic developer finishes; we finish them turnkey within the tower's fit-out manual, ready to live in or rent.

A mix of new towers delivered in shell or with developer finishes and 1980s–1990s low-rise condos. Buyers here are often first-time Hotel Zone owners and investors looking for a lower entry price than the beachfront.

The setting: Bay and Bojórquez lagoon

Calm bay water and the Bojórquez lagoon mean less wave energy than the south, but salt air and humidity still attack balconies, railings and A/C units.

Before or after delivery

The best moment to call us is before the developer delivers: we review the plans, the delivery specification and the building's fit-out manual, so changes are planned and nothing has to be demolished twice.

Snag list and developer warranty

At delivery we inspect the unit with you or for you, record defects for the developer's warranty and only then start the fit-out, so responsibilities stay clear.

Rules in this sector

New towers apply a fit-out manual with approved drawings, contractor registration and set hours; older condos need administration approval and service-lift booking. New towers apply a fit-out manual: approved drawings, protected common areas, working hours, contractor registration and sometimes a deposit; we follow it from the first day.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
