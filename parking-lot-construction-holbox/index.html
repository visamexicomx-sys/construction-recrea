Parking Lots on Holbox: paved, drained and marked

A parking lot on Holbox is the first thing guests and customers see; we design and build it with the right pavement per zone, drainage, accessible bays and clear markings. Holbox has sand streets used by golf carts and bicycles, a growing number of hotels and villas, and demand for durable, permeable street surfaces that fit the island’s character.

Holbox, 2026 references: Asphalt parking lot, complete: $1,000–$1,800 / m²; Concrete parking lot: $1,400–$2,100 / m²; Permeable / grass pavers: $1,200–$2,100 / m².

Keeping a car park dry on Holbox

Streets flood after rain because the water table is near the surface; grades, permeable paving and avoiding hard surfaces are the main tools. Grades, inlets and infiltration are designed for these conditions so storms drain in minutes.

Building around traffic on Holbox

High season from December to April and summer holidays crowd the streets; works are planned in short phases with minimal noise and machinery. We phase the works around this pattern so the business keeps operating.

Ground conditions on Holbox

The island is sand over shell and limestone with a very high water table, so conventional asphalt structures are rarely suitable; pavers, stabilised sand and concrete are the realistic options.

Plants, materials and access on Holbox

All materials cross by ferry from Chiquilá and move on the island with small vehicles, which makes Holbox the most expensive zone for paving and earthworks.
