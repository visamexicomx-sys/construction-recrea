Office Fit-Out in Tulum: From Bare Floor to Move-In Day

You have leased or bought an office in Tulum and it comes as a shell or a tired space. We turn it into a working office: layout, partitions, meeting rooms, data, A/C, lighting, acoustics and furniture, with the building’s approval and Civil Protection, on a fixed price and a date your team can plan the move around.

Quick answer: In 2026, about $6,800–$13,600 MXN/m² for a basic open-plan fit-out and $13,600–$28,200 for a corporate office with partitions, meeting rooms, cabling and joinery.
Tulum 2026 per m²: basic fit-out (open plan, ceilings, lighting, A/C distribution) $6,800–$13,600 MXN; corporate (partitions, meeting rooms, cabling, joinery) $13,600–$28,200; medical or dental $22,600–$45,200. A 150 m² corporate office: $2,030,000–$4,240,000.

What the Fit-Out Includes

Layout & Design

Workstations, meeting rooms, phone booths and break areas planned around how your team works.

Data & Power

Structured cabling, Wi-Fi design, UPS and power at every desk.

A/C Sized to Occupancy

Cooling calculated for people and equipment, zoned so one failure does not close the office.

Acoustics

Partitions to the slab, absorbent ceilings and sealed doors for meeting rooms and calls.

Lighting for Screens

Glare-free lighting designed for screen work, with controls.

Furniture & Move-In

Workstations, joinery, signage and a clean, ready office on the agreed date.

Installations matter more than finishes

An office that looks good and sounds bad gets remodelled again within two years. We size the electrical and data networks for the real density of workstations, calculate A/C with the heat of people and equipment, light for screens, and separate meeting rooms acoustically from the open area — partitions taken to the slab, not stopped at the ceiling. Then the finishes.

Working in a building

Office towers and plazas have their own works rules: drawings approved by the administration, hours for noisy work, booked lifts, protection of common areas, deposits and contractor insurance. Electrical and A/C connect to the building’s risers and meters, so we coordinate with its engineers. We handle all of it, and Civil Protection before you move in.

Offices in Tulum

  • Av. Tulum in town: notaries, banks, real-estate agencies and professional offices
  • Aldea Zama and La Veleta: coworking, studios and developers’ sales offices
  • Region 15 and Highway 307: administration and site offices for construction and hospitality

In Tulum offices are small and mixed-use, and the infrastructure decides the project: power and internet are less reliable than in Cancún, so backup power and redundant connections are not optional. Height limits are low and costs run about 13% above Cancún because of logistics and labour.
