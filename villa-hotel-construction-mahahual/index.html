Villa and hotel construction in Mahahual

Small beach hotels and rental villas on the Costa Maya: design, federal and municipal filings, off-grid systems and an operable handover.

Mahahual lives on two clocks: cruise days, when thousands of passengers walk the malecón for a few hours, and the overnight guests who come for the reef and the quiet. A small hotel here is built for the second group and survives the first.

Between the beach zone and the mangrove

Most hotel lots sit on the strip between the federal beach zone and the protected mangrove, so the real footprint is what lies between those two lines. We survey both before design, keep the building back from the dune and use the ZOFEMAT strip only for what a concession allows: loungers, not rooms.

Off-grid is part of the product

Away from the centre, a hotel needs its own cistern, treatment plant, solar with storage and a generator. We size them for full occupancy in August, not for an average day, because a guest without water or air conditioning writes the review that decides the next season.

Designed after Dean

Hurricane Dean came ashore here as a category 5 in 2007. Rooms on a raised ground floor, a reinforced concrete structure, shutters or impact glazing and 316 stainless on every exposed fitting are the baseline, together with an operations plan for securing the property when a storm is forecast.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Mahahual

For a small hotel in Mahahual we work in a range of MXN 18,000 to 32,000 per built m² (roughly USD 980 – 1,745/m²). Freight from Chetumal is reasonable; what pushes the figure up is the off-grid infrastructure and the marine-exposure specification.

An eight to twenty key hotel takes thirteen to seventeen months of construction, including the federal beach and mangrove reviews where they apply. We schedule pours and roofing outside the peak of the hurricane season.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Mahahual: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Othón P. Blanco
  • Environmental regime: ZOFEMAT + NOM-022 (mangrove) + municipal
  • Ground: coastal sand over limestone, peat near the mangrove
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A narrow strip between reef and mangrove: federal beach zone in front, protected wetland behind and limited services in between.

  • Locate the ZOFEMAT line and the mangrove edge on a topographic survey before buying; they define the real buildable area.
  • Budget water, power and treatment as part of the house, not as optional extras.

Frequently asked questions

Does the cruise traffic help a hotel in Mahahual?

Indirectly. Cruise passengers rarely stay overnight, but they sustain the restaurants, dive shops and services that make the town attractive to overnight guests. The hotel’s market is divers, Costa Maya travellers and long-stay visitors.

Can I put rooms on the beach?

No. The first 20 metres from the high-tide line are federal maritime zone; with a concession you can use them for loungers or a deck, not for rooms. Rooms go behind that line and in front of the protected mangrove.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
