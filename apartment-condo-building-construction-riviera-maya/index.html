Condo Development in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Riviera Maya is in a real-estate boom: Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Cancun absorb thousands of new units a year, driven by vacation rental and migration. A well-executed apartment building is one of the most profitable projects — it can be sold in pre-sale (financing part of construction with deposits) or held for Airbnb rental. The keys are feasibility (land use and allowed density), the right unit mix, amenities that sell (rooftop, pool) and a well-founded concrete structure on the karst soil. We build turnkey — feasibility, design, permits, DRO and construction — with a fixed-price contract, milestone payments and warranty, in any city in the region.
