Demolition & Debris Removal on Cozumel: strip-outs, concrete cutting and debris removal

We demolish selectively on Cozumel: interior strip-outs, concrete cutting and total demolitions, with structural review first and legal debris disposal. Works are licensed by Cozumel municipality; the coast is under ZOFEMAT and parts of the island are protected, and condo buildings have their own administration rules.

Cozumel, 2026 references: Interior strip-out: $220–$460/m²; Concrete slab demolition: $550–$1,100/m²; Total demolition, 1-storey house: $790–$1,300/m².

Permits and rules for demolition on Cozumel

Crews and materials cross on the car ferry from Playa del Carmen; we load full trucks, plan stays of several days and keep the most-used materials in stock on site. We process these approvals before any hammer starts, so neighbours and administrations have no reason to stop the job.

Getting debris out on Cozumel

Cozumel has family homes in San Miguel, waterfront condos and villas along the west coast and many dive-tourism rentals, with steady demand for bathrooms, floors, paint and storm repairs. Trucks, loading and disposal sites are planned around this access, and debris leaves the site daily.

Rentals and scheduling on Cozumel

Dive and cruise tourism keeps rentals busy all year, so we usually renovate one unit or room at a time.

Climate and exposure on Cozumel

The island takes salt and wind from every side and has been hit by strong hurricanes, so metal, windows, paint and garden structures need marine-grade materials and hurricane fixing.

Water supply on Cozumel

Water on the island is hard and supply can be irregular, so cisterns, softeners and filters are standard and fittings must tolerate scale.
