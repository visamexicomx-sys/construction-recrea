How Much Does It Cost to Build a Shopping Plaza in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: in 2026 a neighbourhood plaza comercial in the Riviera Maya costs about $9,000–$16,000 MXN per leasable m² for the shell, plus $25,000–$70,000 per parking space, plus services such as a treatment plant and a generator. Below: the breakdown, example budgets by town and what moves the number.

Quick answer: Structure, roof, walls, storefront openings and slab: $9,000–$16,000/m² leasable.
Example: an 800 m² leasable plaza in Cancún with 30 parking spaces — shell $7.2M–$12.8M, parking $0.75M–$2.1M, services and signage $0.85M–$3.0M — totals about $8.8M–$17.9M MXN before land, VAT, permits and tenant fit-outs.

What Goes Into the Budget

Shell

Structure, roof, walls, storefront openings and slab: $9,000–$16,000/m² leasable.

Parking

Paving, drainage and lighting: $25,000–$70,000 per surface space.

Food-Ready Units

Extraction, grease and services: $150,000–$500,000 per unit.

Treatment Plant

Where there is no sewer: $400,000–$1,500,000.

Generator

Common areas and plant: $300,000–$900,000.

Signage

Totem and wayfinding: $150,000–$600,000.

What moves the number

  • Parking ratio — it sets how much leasable area fits on the lot, so it moves the cost per leasable m² more than finishes do.
  • Services available — a lot without sewer, enough power or water adds a treatment plant, a transformer and a cistern.
  • Number of food units — each adds extraction, grease and heavier services, but earns the highest rent.
  • Town — Playa del Carmen about 4% above Cancún, Puerto Morelos 9%, Puerto Aventuras 11%, Tulum 13%.
  • Two storeys — more leasable area on the same lot, but stairs, a lift for accessibility and lower rents upstairs.

What is not included

Land, VAT, design and permit fees, the developer’s financing and leasing costs, and tenant fit-outs (usually paid by the tenant, from $5,500 MXN/m²). Budget design and permits at roughly 6–10% of construction.
