Hotel Construction in Tulum: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Tulum’s hotel market runs from eco-lodges and boutique hotels to 5-star resorts and a growing number of urban hotels in Aldea Zama, La Veleta and the centre serving the new international airport and the Tren Maya. It is also the most regulated: national park limits on the beach, SEMA authorisation, and wastewater rules on every site.

Tulum 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $10,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $14,000–$19,000; boutique and 4-star $19,000–$27,000; 5-star and luxury $27,000–$45,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

Beach, jungle or town

The beach strip lies largely within or next to the national park, with strict limits on height and density; jungle lots allow low-rise boutique and eco hotels; town districts allow denser urban hotels. The class of hotel follows the zoning, not the other way round.

SEMA and the environment

In the Tulum municipality the state SEMA authorisation precedes the municipal licence, starting from the vegetation inventory. Larger projects need a federal MIA. We plan the footprint around trees and cenotes and schedule the files from day one.

Water and wastewater first

Much of Tulum has no sewer, and the aquifer below feeds the cenotes. Every hotel needs its own treatment plant, sized for full occupancy, and often its own water treatment. We build both as part of the hotel.
