Warehouse and Light-Industrial Construction in Tulum

Storage, logistics and hospitality supply — clear span, industrial floor, delivered operational

Most warehouses in Tulum are not built for industry. They are built to supply hospitality: cold storage, dry goods, laundry, furniture and equipment for the hotels and restaurants along the corridor. That changes what the building has to do, and Tulum changes how long it takes to get permission to do it.

The environmental file comes first, and it is the schedule

In the Tulum municipality the state SEMA is involved in practically every project, and its resolution precedes the municipal licence. For a warehouse that means three to six months before ground can be broken, against four to ten weeks for the same building inland in Playa del Carmen or Cancún.

Clearing is part of it. A warehouse footprint on a jungle lot requires authorised clearing and, where it applies, flora rescue, both documented in the file. Planning the steel delivery before the resolution lands is how a build ends up paying for storage on material it cannot erect.

Karst under a slab is a different problem from karst under a house

On a house you find a void and redesign a footing. Under a 1,500 m² slab carrying racking you find one and the job stops. The soil study with void verification comes before the structure is priced, and in Tulum that study also has to account for jungle topsoil that has to be stripped and replaced with compacted fill.

The water table here is mid-depth, which is the one thing working in your favour: the slab sits on a predictable platform and drainage can go to an absorption well where the study supports it. On the coastal strip neither of those is true.

Power and water are project line items, not connections

In the Tulum regions the CFE supply is resolved lot by lot. A unit with cold storage, dock equipment and lighting will not run on what is at the kerb, so a capacity increase, a transformer and often a line extension paid by the owner belong in the first budget. Outside the urban area there is no sewer network either: treatment plant and water storage, not a connection fee.

We request the feasibility rulings alongside the SEMA filing, so the three run in parallel rather than one after the other. That sequencing is most of the difference between a nine-month project and a fourteen-month one.

What the building has to survive

Salt air reaches inland. Galvanising class and paint system are specified for the actual distance to the coast, and roof fixings and gutters — where an unprotected frame fails first — get the same attention as the portal frames. The envelope is calculated for the cyclone wind zone: sheet gauge, fixing pattern and purlin spacing, not a catalogue detail.

Inside, what gates occupancy is the fire and safety side: STPS workplace requirements, a protection system sized to what is stored, marked exits and the Civil Protection sign-off. On a warehouse the insurer asks for the same documents the authority does.

The ground in Tulum: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA; SEMARNAT on the coastal strip
  • Ground: karst under low jungle
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: depends on the sub-zone

The state SEMA sets the calendar; the environmental file is the rule, not the exception.

  • Federal SEMARNAT filing: the environmental resolution is the project’s critical path. Budget 4–8 months before the first excavation.
  • State SEMA authorisation comes before the municipal licence: without it the town hall will not open a file.
  • Karst ground: a soil study with void verification before you price the foundation. A cavity found after the pour costs more than the entire study.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a warehouse take in Tulum?

Three to six months for the SEMA resolution before work starts, plus the build. Structure and envelope move fast once steel is on site; the binding constraints are the environmental file and, where capacity has to increase, the CFE ruling. We run both in parallel from day one.

Can you build on a jungle lot?

Yes. Authorised clearing, flora rescue where it applies, topsoil stripped and replaced with compacted fill, and void verification before the structure is priced. All of it goes into the environmental file rather than being discovered later.

What if the lot has no drainage or nearby CFE?

That is the normal case outside the urban area. Treatment plant, water storage and either a capacity increase with a transformer or own generation. It is viable; what is not viable is pricing the building before knowing which applies.

Do you handle Civil Protection and the fire system?

Yes. The fire design is sized to the stored material and carried through Civil Protection as part of the file. Getting it wrong is the most common reason a finished warehouse cannot open.

How much does it cost per square metre?

The frame is the smaller half. Floor flatness class, dock count, electrical capacity and any refrigerated area move the figure far more. We price against a defined operation, after the feasibility rulings.
