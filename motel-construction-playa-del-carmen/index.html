Motel & Auto-hotel Construction in Playa del Carmen

Motels with private garages are one of the highest-occupancy hospitality formats in Mexican cities, and Playa del Carmen is no exception. We build motels and auto-hotels for investors and operators: rooms with private garages and discreet access, suites with jacuzzi, acoustic separation, a laundry and service building that turns rooms fast, and the licences to open.

Quick answer: Motel & Auto-hotel Construction in Playa del Carmen — 2026 prices: Per room with private garage: $676,000–$1,248,000 MXN; Construction, per m²: $14,000–$23,000 MXN; Jacuzzi suite upgrade, per room: $125,000–$312,000 MXN; Laundry and service building: $2.1M–$5.2M MXN.
Playa del Carmen 2026: per room with garage $676,000–$1,248,000 MXN; construction $14,000–$23,000 per m²; jacuzzi suite upgrade $125,000–$312,000 per room. A 20-room motel runs about $16.6M–$31.2M; a 40-room motel $33.3M–$60.3M.

Turnover is the business

A motel earns on room turnover. We plan service corridors, laundry and linen flow so rooms are ready in minutes, and garages and circulation so guests never cross.

Durable, easy-clean finishes

High occupancy wears rooms out fast. We specify finishes, sanitaryware and A/C that survive heavy use and clean quickly, and design suites that justify a higher rate.

Building in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the second market and the busiest for small and mid-size condo buildings: downtown near Quinta Avenida, Playacar, Centro Maya, the north and the Corasol corridor. Lots are narrow, municipal height limits are strict, and buyers are largely foreign investors who expect rooftop amenities and rental-ready units.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Solidaridad; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
