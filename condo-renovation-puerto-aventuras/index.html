Renovating a Condo in Puerto Aventuras: What to Know

Puerto Aventuras is a gated marina and golf community with controlled access, dominated by marina-front condos in high vacation-rental demand. Renovating here requires coordination with the master-condominium administration: work hours, deposit, controlled entry of materials and crew, and building rules. Marine salt air demands anti-corrosion finishes and ironwork. We know the regulations and work friction-free — ideal for owners who rent their condo or don’t live in Mexico. A good renovation raises rental value immediately.
