Building & Renovating in Akumal: What to Know

Akumal is a Caribbean-front ecological area, in the municipality of Tulum, famous for its turtles and reefs. Building near the coast demands maximum environmental responsibility: SEMA environmental authorization, respect for the dune and the Federal Maritime Zone (ZOFEMAT), turtle-friendly lighting and eco-friendly water and drainage solutions (cistern, treatment). An eco-chic style dominates (chukum, wood, integration with the surroundings). We know the coastal rules and SEMA, and coordinate everything with a 100% approval record — ideal for owners who don’t live here. High appreciation and beachfront rental demand.
