Private wellness for homes and hotels in Cancún

Hotel Zone buildings face open sea on one side and the lagoon on the other, while inland neighbourhoods are hot and humid; salt protection matters on the coast, mould prevention everywhere. Wellness rooms add value to villas and rentals here, so we plan them with the pool, terrace and guest flow.

Steam, heat and humidity in Cancún

Aguakan supplies the city, but the water is hard and pressure varies by sector, so softeners, filters and pressure pumps are common upgrades in any bathroom or kitchen job. Vapour barriers, extraction and drainage keep steam inside the spa and out of the rest of the house.

Permits and rules in Cancún

Licences come from Benito Juárez municipality; condo towers require building-administration approval, insurance certificates and set work hours, and Hotel Zone buildings often restrict noisy work to weekdays.

Rentals and scheduling in Cancún

Many Hotel Zone and downtown units are short-term rentals, so we phase work between guests and coordinate lifts and loading bays with building management.
