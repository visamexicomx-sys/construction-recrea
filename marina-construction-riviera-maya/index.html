Marina & Yacht Club Construction in the Riviera Maya

Boat owners on this coast wait for slips, and a well-run marina lifts the value of every unit around it. We build marinas and yacht clubs for developers, hotels and residential communities in the Riviera Maya: floating and fixed docks, breakwaters, fuel and pump-out, power and water to every slip and the club building — with the federal concession and environmental permits handled from the start.

Concession & MIA

Permits decide the schedule

A marina sits on federal land and water: it needs a ZOFEMAT concession, an environmental impact statement (MIA) approved by SEMARNAT and port authority sign-off before a single pile goes in. These take longer than the works, so we build the permit file and the marine engineering together and keep the docks ready to fabricate while approvals run.

Built for hurricanes and salt

Docks, piles and pedestals live in salt water and must survive hurricane surge. We specify marine-grade concrete, hot-dip galvanised or stainless hardware, piles designed for scour and floating docks with guide piles tall enough for storm surge — and a hurricane plan for boats and docks.
