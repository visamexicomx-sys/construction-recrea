Home and Villa Maintenance in Playa del Carmen: One Team for Every Repair

Salt, sun and humidity attack a house every day: rust on railings, leaks on the roof, mould in closets, AC units that drip and pumps that fail the day guests arrive. Instead of calling a different handyman for each problem, you get one maintenance team with electricians, plumbers, painters and carpenters, scheduled preventive visits and a photo report after each one, ideal for owners who live abroad or rent their property.

2026 prices: handyman visit up to 2 hours $650–$1,200 MXN; preventive maintenance plan for a house or condo $3,500–$7,500 MXN/month; villa with pool and garden $7,500–$15,000 MXN/month; annual roof waterproofing inspection $1,500–$3,000 MXN; hurricane preparation visit $2,500–$6,000 MXN. Preventive maintenance costs less than repairs

Most expensive repairs on the coast start small: a cracked roof membrane that becomes a ceiling stain, a rust spot that becomes a broken railing, a clogged AC drain that becomes a mould problem. A monthly or quarterly visit catches these early. We follow a checklist for each property, record the condition with photos and send you a report with what we fixed and what needs a decision.

For owners abroad and rental properties

  • Scheduled visits with a written checklist and photo report.
  • Turnovers between guests: quick fixes, bulbs, filters, touch-ups.
  • Emergency response for leaks, power failures and storm damage.
  • Annual plan for repainting, waterproofing and equipment replacement, so costs are predictable.
