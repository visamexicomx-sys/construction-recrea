Property Maintenance in the Hotel Zone Entrance

Absentee owners at the entrance need a simple condo plan: monthly visit, A/C and water checks, repairs between tenants and a photo report.

A mix of new towers delivered in shell or with developer finishes and 1980s–1990s low-rise condos. Buyers here are often first-time Hotel Zone owners and investors looking for a lower entry price than the beachfront.

The setting: Bay and Bojórquez lagoon

Calm bay water and the Bojórquez lagoon mean less wave energy than the south, but salt air and humidity still attack balconies, railings and A/C units.

What fails first near the sea

Rust, sealants, A/C drains, roof membranes and pool equipment are the first problems in the Cancún Hotel Zone; our checklist targets them before they become damage.

Hurricane season

The Hotel Zone is the first area evacuated before a hurricane, so from June to November we prepare properties as soon as a warning is issued and inspect them as soon as access reopens, with photos for you and your insurer.

Rules in this sector

New towers apply a fit-out manual with approved drawings, contractor registration and set hours; older condos need administration approval and service-lift booking. We are registered with the building administrations, so our crews can enter on schedule and in emergencies.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
