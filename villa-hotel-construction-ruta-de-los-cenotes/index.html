Villa and hotel construction in Ruta de los Cenotes

Eco-lodges, cabins and nature retreats along the cenote route: ground radar, zero discharge, federal filings and a handover ready to operate.

The Ruta de los Cenotes draws visitors for swimming, diving and jungle, and the hospitality that fits is small and low: eco-lodges, cabins and glamping on large plots, often with a cenote as the centrepiece. The ground and the water decide how it is built.

Cabins placed by radar

Karst with voids and connected caverns lies under most plots. Before placing cabins, we survey with ground-penetrating radar and keep each unit on sound rock; spreading small buildings makes that easier than one large foundation.

The cenote is the product, protect it

Guests come for clean water. Treatment with zero discharge to the ground, careful storage of fuel and chemicals and runoff kept away from the cenote are both an environmental requirement and the way to protect the asset that sells the stay.

Off-grid comfort

Plots off the Leona Vicario road may be far from the grid. Solar with storage, a generator, rainwater and well water with treatment and efficient fans or split units give guests comfort without a municipal network.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Ruta de los Cenotes

For an eco-lodge or cabins on the Ruta de los Cenotes we work in a range of MXN 15,000 to 28,000 per built m² (roughly USD 815 – 1,525/m²). Radar surveys, treatment, off-grid systems and access works are part of the figure; low salt keeps the envelope lean.

A six to twenty unit lodge takes twelve to sixteen months, including the federal environmental review near cenotes. Deliveries are planned for the dry season.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Ruta de los Cenotes: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Puerto Morelos
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT (cenotes and jungle)
  • Ground: karst with voids
  • Water table: close to cenotes
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: Leona Vicario road and dirt tracks
  • Development regime: low density, eco-tourism use

Cenote system: ground-penetrating radar before foundations and zero tolerance on discharge.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build cabins around a cenote?

Yes, within environmental rules that set setbacks, zero discharge and controlled runoff. We map the cenote and the karst before placing any building.

Is an eco-lodge on the cenote route viable off-grid?

Yes: solar with storage, a generator, rainwater and treated well water can run a small lodge comfortably. We size them for full occupancy.
