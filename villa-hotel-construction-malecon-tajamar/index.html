Villa and hotel construction in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

Boutique hotels, aparthotels and rental buildings on Cancún’s lagoon front: piles, environmental due diligence and an operable handover.

Malecón Tajamar’s mixed-use zoning makes it one of the few places in central Cancún where a boutique hotel, an aparthotel or a building of rental suites can sit on the lagoon. The product is business and long-stay guests who want the city and the water in one address.

The right product for the lot

Between towers and avenues, small lots suit 20 to 60 keys: aparthotel suites with kitchenettes, a rooftop with pool and bar and parking below. We test the programme against density, height and parking rules before design, so the numbers hold.

Due diligence before the first drawing

Lenders and operators here ask about the environmental status first. We document the lot’s authorisations, mangrove boundaries and the municipal land use, and attach them to the project file from day one.

Piles, parking and the water table

Fill over lagoon sediment means piles and a basement only where waterproofing and uplift design justify it. Often a raised podium for parking is cheaper and safer than digging below the water table.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún

A boutique hotel or aparthotel in Malecón Tajamar runs at MXN 20,000 to 34,000 per built m² (roughly USD 1,090 – 1,850/m²), with piles and FF&E as separate lines.

A 30 to 60 key building takes eighteen to twenty-six months. Soil study, environmental review, land-use confirmation and the Benito Juárez licence come first.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Malecón Tajamar, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: municipal + SEMARNAT where mangrove remains
  • Ground: fill over lagoon sediment
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: moderate
  • Access: urban avenues next to Puerto Cancún
  • Development regime: high-rise residential and mixed use on the lagoon

A lagoon-front district whose 2016 mangrove dispute made environmental compliance closely watched here.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build a boutique hotel in Malecón Tajamar?

Often yes, where the lot’s land use allows hotel or mixed use. We confirm it and the environmental status before design.

Is a basement car park a good idea in Malecón Tajamar?

Rarely. With a very high water table, a raised parking podium is usually cheaper and safer than a waterproofed basement.
