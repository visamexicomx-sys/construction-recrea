Steel Frame Homes and Rental Casitas in Playa del Carmen

In Playa del Carmen, time is income: a rental unit finished three months earlier earns three months more. Steel framing builds faster than block and concrete, which is why we use it for rental casitas on larger lots, rooftop studios on existing houses and buildings, and homes on jungle lots and in the north corridor where termites, humidity and access make light, dry construction attractive.

Playa del Carmen 2026: steel frame structure and envelope $6,000–$9,500 MXN/m²; turnkey rental casita or home with mid-range finishes $14,000–$22,000 MXN/m²; premium villa finishes $22,000–$35,000 MXN/m².

Playa Uses for Steel Framing

Rental Casitas

Studios and one-bedroom units for long-term or holiday rental, finished in months.

Rooftop Studios

Light additions on centre houses and buildings that cannot take more concrete.

Jungle Lots

Termite-proof, dry construction for eco-homes west of the 307.

North Corridor

Homes near Punta Bete and Xcalacoco with corrosion-protected frames.

Hurricane Engineering

Shear walls, hold-downs and roof straps for each site.

Insulated Comfort

Lower A/C bills than uninsulated block, a real advantage in Playa's summer.

Built for the rental market

Many owners in Playa add a casita or rooftop studio to rent. With steel framing the structure goes up in weeks and the unit can be on the market in a few months, with insulation that keeps guests comfortable and A/C bills lower. Interiors are finished with cement board and drywall, so they look and feel like any premium rental.

Jungle and termites

Termites are a constant on Playa’s jungle lots, and timber framing suffers. Galvanized steel does not feed them or rot with humidity. We raise the slab, add moisture barriers and choose heavier zinc coatings near the coast, and we anchor the frame firmly against hurricane uplift.
