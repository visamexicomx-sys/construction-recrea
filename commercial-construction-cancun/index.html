Commercial Construction in Cancún

Retail, plazas, restaurants and offices — turnkey, with an opening date

A commercial build is not measured in square metres, it is measured in days of lost trading. We build and fit out retail units, restaurants, offices and anchor stores in Cancún with the permit file settled from day one and an opening date that holds.

What we build in Cancún

Retail units and plazas

New build and fit-out in shopping plazas: shopfront, glazing systems, flooring, electrical installation and air conditioning sized for the trade. We agree working hours and material access with the plaza management before the first delivery.

Restaurants and kitchens

Hood and extraction, duct to roof, grease trap, gas line with a pressure test, slip-resistant flooring and hygienic finishes. This is the commercial build with the most Civil Protection requirements and the one that slips furthest when the services design arrives late.

Offices and corporate space

Ceilings, lighting, voice and data, access control and zoned climate. In an existing building the constraint is the available electrical capacity and the duct route, not the interior design.

Stores and franchises

We have built for 7-Eleven and GoMart. A franchise arrives with its own brand manual and specification; our job is to meet it without losing the opening date and without surprises at the municipal inspection.

Commercial land use is read by supermanzana in Cancún

Benito Juárez zones by supermanzana rather than by neighbourhood, and commercial use is not permitted in all of them. Two units on the same avenue can carry different authorised trades, and the trade decides everything else: occupancy, parking spaces, emergency exits and Civil Protection requirements.

The land-use certificate is requested with the cadastral key and is the first document in the file. If the trade you want is not permitted, you find out in week one rather than after signing the lease. That is the most expensive mistake we see on commercial jobs: lease first, verify later.

You can check the regime for your area in the zone checker, and the process itself in construction permits in Cancún.

What drives up the cost of a commercial build in Cancún

  • Electrical load. A kitchen, a cold room or the air conditioning for a 200 m² unit demands capacity the existing connection almost never has. The CFE feasibility request belongs at the start of the project; asking for it at the end costs four to eight weeks of stopped work.
  • Glazing and shopfront. Quintana Roo is a high cyclone-risk zone. A street-facing shopfront is resolved with tempered or laminated glass and calculated anchorage, not with the cheapest profile on the market.
  • Corrosion. Near the Hotel Zone and the lagoon, 304 stainless hardware and window systems pit within two years. We specify 316 outdoors and concrete cover above the code minimum.
  • Civil Protection. Emergency exits, signage, extinguishers, detection and, in restaurants, a hood suppression system. Their sign-off gates the opening, not the handover.
  • Hours and noise. In a plaza and in the Hotel Zone, work runs in restricted windows. Daily output drops and the real schedule is calculated on effective working days.

The opening date is the budget

On a commercial job the cost of delay usually exceeds the cost of the saving. A unit that opens a month late loses a month of trading while still paying rent, contracted payroll and advertising already bought. So we plan backwards: we start from the opening date and derive the deadline for every filing from it.

In practice that means three things. The CFE feasibility request and the land-use certificate are filed before the construction drawings. The special services — extraction, gas, grease trap, voice and data — are designed alongside the architecture rather than after it. And the Civil Protection sign-off is worked in parallel with the build, not once it is finished.

Where your unit sits: four different Cancúns

Central supermanzanas and avenues. Entirely municipal process, deep water table, compact limestone. This is the fastest scenario: four to ten weeks to the licence with a complete file.

Puerto Cancún. On top of the municipality, the development design committee reviews facade and materials before the town hall opens a file, and any work on the canal edge carries a separate federal concession.

Hotel Zone. Here the city does not decide. The strip between the sea and the Nichupté lagoon has federal frontage on both sides: SEMARNAT for environmental impact and ZOFEMAT for the beach concession, with a six-to-twelve-month calendar. Density is read against the FONATUR master plan.

Riviera Cancún. The corridor towards Puerto Morelos, with mangrove to the west and dune to the east. The real buildable strip is usually considerably smaller than the lot area, and the environmental study is what fixes it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build a commercial unit in Cancún?

It depends far more on the trade than on the floor area. A standard fit-out in a plaza sits in one range; a restaurant with a full kitchen, extraction, gas and a grease trap can cost double per square metre. We give a fixed figure after reviewing the trade, the condition of the unit and the available electrical capacity.

Do I need a land-use certificate if I am only remodelling?

If the trade changes, yes. If you keep the trade and the work does not touch structure, shopfront or main services, a remodelling-mode licence is usually enough. In both cases Civil Protection reviews again before you open.

How long does a restaurant permit take?

In a supermanzana, with a complete file, four to ten weeks for the municipal licence. The real bottleneck is usually not the licence but the CFE feasibility ruling when capacity has to be increased, and the Civil Protection sign-off for the hood suppression system.

Do you work with franchises and their brand manuals?

Yes. We have built for 7-Eleven and GoMart. We work to the brand specification and reconcile it with the municipal code; where the manual and the local rule conflict, we settle it before filing rather than at the inspection.

Can you guarantee an opening date?

We give a contractual date when the permit file is under our control from the start: land use, licence, CFE and Civil Protection. If a client arrives with filings half-done by a third party, we give a date after auditing where they actually stand.
