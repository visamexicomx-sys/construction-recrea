Waterproofing in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

The Riviera Maya combines intense rain, extreme sun and salt air — the worst mix for a poorly waterproofed roof. Most leaks come not from the slab but from poorly sealed parapets, skylights, rain drains and joints. Good work starts with diagnosis and prep (cleaning, crack repair, primer), continues with the right system for the rooftop use (walkable or not), and ends with well-sealed details. We offer a written warranty and reflective systems that also lower indoor temperature.
