Luxury villas in Puerto Morelos

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Puerto Morelos offers what Cancún and Playa no longer have: large lots, low height and quiet, half an hour from the airport. A villa here is designed for privacy and for the view, not for density.

Reef front row: a privilege and a file

The Puerto Morelos Reef National Park turns the view into protected heritage. On the seaward side SEMARNAT and the ZOFEMAT concession enter, and the dune is respected: boardwalk access, no continuous wall towards the beach, warm shielded lighting. That file sets the project calendar and is started before the interior design.

Jungle, private cenote and the house you cannot see from the road

Towards the Ruta de los Cenotes there are lots of thousands of square metres with mature vegetation and sometimes a cenote of their own. There the luxury is seclusion: a siting that keeps the large trees, treatment with zero discharge because karst goes straight to the aquifer, and a house organised around shade and water rather than around a façade.

Autonomy: the villa has to run on its own

Sized cistern, water treatment, wastewater plant, electrical back-up with automatic transfer and solar where consumption justifies it. A high-end villa in this area cannot depend on nothing external failing: outages happen and guests do not forgive them. It is designed to run as an energy island for days, with maintenance scheduled every six months.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Puerto Morelos: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Puerto Morelos
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT (reef national park) + ZOFEMAT
  • Ground: limestone, mangrove to the west
  • Water table: high near the coast
  • Salt exposure: severe on the front row

Independent municipality since 2016 — different authority, rulebook and timelines from Benito Juárez and Solidaridad.

  • Federal SEMARNAT filing: the environmental resolution is the project’s critical path. Budget 4–8 months before the first excavation.
  • ZOFEMAT frontage: the federal strip is not yours even if you buy the lot — it is concessioned separately and paid for annually.
  • High water table: the foundation sits higher and the pool needs anchoring against uplift.
  • Severe salt exposure: 316 stainless throughout the hardware, concrete cover above the code minimum and six-monthly maintenance. 304 pits within two years at this distance from the sea.
  • Mangrove on the parcel: protected under federal law. The real buildable footprint can be a fraction of the lot.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build directly on the beach?

Not in the way people usually imagine. The twenty-metre federal strip from the high tide line is ZOFEMAT: it is concessioned and paid for, not bought, and what is allowed there is limited. The house is set back, access is resolved as a boardwalk over the dune, and the seafront palapa or terrace goes through the federal process.

How long does a front-row villa take here?

With a sea frontage, six to twelve months of environmental and ZOFEMAT process before work can start, and fourteen to twenty months of construction for 400–700 m². Inland the process drops to weeks and construction to twelve to sixteen months. That is why the order of permits, not the design, decides the delivery date.

Will you work with the architect I already have?

Yes. We can come in purely as builder — reviewing the design, closing the engineering, pricing by line item and executing. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred. We do not make the works conditional on also being the authors of the design.

What warranty do you give?

One year on services and finishes and five years on structure and waterproofing, with as-built drawings, datasheets and equipment manuals. We also offer a six-monthly maintenance plan, which in a coastal climate is what decides how the house reaches its fifth year.
