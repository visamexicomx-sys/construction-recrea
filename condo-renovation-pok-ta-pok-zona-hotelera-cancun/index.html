Condo Renovation in Pok-Ta-Pok, Cancún

The small condo buildings of Pok-Ta-Pok have lagoon and golf views; we renovate their units fully, opening kitchens towards the water.

Houses from the 1970s–1990s on generous lots and small condo buildings with lagoon views; many have been updated piecemeal and now need a complete renovation of MEP, glazing and finishes.

The setting: Nichupté lagoon, golf island

The lagoon side is sheltered from waves but humid, with mangrove, mosquitoes and fluctuating water levels; docks, seawalls and gardens need lagoon-specific detailing.

Towers, administrations and work hours

Every tower has its own regulations: approval of the work plan, set hours for noisy work, booking of the service lift, protection of common areas, deposits and insurance certificates. We prepare that package before starting, so the administration has no reason to stop the job.

Older towers, salt and two waterfronts

Many Hotel Zone towers are 30 to 40 years old and face the sea on one side and the lagoon on the other. We check risers, the electrical feed and balcony concrete before closing walls, and use stainless, marine-grade hardware and sealed frames that do not rust or stain.

Rules in this sector

Houses follow the residential association's rules and a Benito Juárez licence with DRO for exterior or structural works; anything on the shore sits in federal zone and needs a ZOFEMAT concession and environmental review. Works inside towers need the administration's written approval, registered and insured crews, booked service-lift slots and protection of corridors and lifts.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
