3. Working next to patients

Every phase starts with a plan agreed with the medical team: which rooms close, how patients and staff move, where the barriers go and when noisy work happens. Barriers are sealed to the slab, work areas held under negative pressure and dust measured daily. Shut-downs of gases and power are planned and tested.

4. Operating theatres and ICUs

These are the most demanding rooms: seamless washable finishes, laminar airflow, pressure cascades, medical gases, isolated power and testing before use. We build them to the same standard as a new hospital.

5. Not included

  • Land, VAT and financing
  • Design and permits: 6–10% of construction
  • Contingency 5–10%
