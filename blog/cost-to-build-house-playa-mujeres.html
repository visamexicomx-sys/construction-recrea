How Much Does It Cost to Build in Playa Mujeres? (2026)

A 350 m² villa in Playa Mujeres costs roughly $9.8M–$14M MXN ($544k–$778k USD) turnkey in 2026, and more on a beachfront lot. It feels like part of Cancún, but the permits come from another municipality, and the open-sea exposure makes this a different building from a city villa.

Related service: House construction in Playa Mujeres

Another municipality, another rulebook

Playa Mujeres sits on the mainland north of Cancún, inside the municipality of Isla Mujeres, not Benito Juárez. Land use, construction licence and Protección Civil all go through Isla Mujeres, and its processes, fees and timelines differ from Cancún’s. Budget time for this; do not assume a Cancún licence timeline.

  • Gated master plan. Golf, marina and beach sections each come with design guidelines and a review committee, plus contractor registration and working-hour rules.
  • Beachfront lots face the federal maritime zone (ZOFEMAT): a concession for the 20-metre strip, coastal dune protection, and a sea-turtle nesting season from May to October that restricts lighting and beach-side work.
  • Distance. Crews, concrete and materials travel from Cancún. Mobilisation and transport are real lines in the estimate, as is a crew able to stay on schedule that far out.

Turnkey villa cost by size

Luxury-specification turnkey ranges for a non-beachfront lot (golf or marina section):

A beachfront lot adds:

  • Higher specification for direct sea exposure: count $40,000–$60,000 MXN/m² instead of the band above
  • ZOFEMAT concession, environmental study and dune management: $150,000–$450,000
  • Impact-rated glazing and storm protection for the sea facade: $1M–$3M
  • Pool with sea-facing structural design: $1.1M–$2.6M

Building for open-sea exposure

Playa Mujeres faces the open Caribbean with no reef or island in front of it, and it lies directly in the path of the storms that reach the northern Quintana Roo coast. That changes the specification:

  • Wind design to code for the region, with a structural engineer signing the calculations, connections and roof anchorage — not a generic plan.
  • Concrete cover and mix for a marine environment, and 316 stainless for everything exposed outdoors.
  • Raised floor level and drainage designed for surge and heavy rain on the beach side.

Plan 5–8 months for design, the committee and the Isla Mujeres permits, longer on beachfront with the ZOFEMAT file, and 12–18 months of construction. Fixed price against a line-item budget, milestone payments and weekly photo reports matter even more when the site is 30 minutes from the city.
