How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Playa del Carmen? (2026)

A well-built 150 m² family home in Playa del Carmen costs roughly $2.6M–$3.2M MXN ($145,000–$178,000 USD) turnkey in 2026. Here is the full phase-by-phase breakdown so you know exactly where the money goes.

Phase-by-phase budget (150 m² standard home)

Phase% of budgetApprox. cost (MXN)Notes
Permits, DRO & studies5%$130,000–$160,000Licencia, DRO, soil study, topography
Foundation & structure30%$780,000–$960,000Slab, columns, beams, slabs
Walls & roof18%$470,000–$580,000Block, castillos, roof slab
Installations15%$390,000–$480,000Electrical, plumbing, sanitary
Finishes25%$650,000–$800,000Floors, paint, chukum, carpentry
Cleanup & delivery7%$180,000–$220,000Final details, handover

Total turnkey ≈ $2.6M–$3.2M MXN. Excludes land, pool and furniture.

Optional add-ons

  • Swimming pool: $350,000–$600,000 MXN depending on size and finish.
  • Rooftop terrace / palapa: $180,000–$450,000 MXN.
  • Solar system: $90,000–$180,000 MXN (pays back in 4–6 years).
  • Landscaping & fencing: $120,000–$300,000 MXN.

How long does it take?

A 150 m² home takes about 7–10 months from permit approval to handover, depending on finish complexity and weather (the rainy/hurricane season can add weeks).

How to keep the budget under control

Insist on a fixed-price written contract with a detailed line-item budget, a payment schedule tied to milestones, and a written warranty. Vague per-m² handshakes are where budgets blow up.
