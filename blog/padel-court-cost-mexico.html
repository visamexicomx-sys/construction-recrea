How Much Does a Padel Court Cost in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: in 2026 a complete outdoor padel court costs about $660,000–$1,170,000 MXN: $480,000–$850,000 for the court installed and $180,000–$320,000 for the slab and drainage. A roof adds $900,000–$1,800,000; a resin tennis court costs $750,000–$1,400,000.

3. What moves the price

  • Roof: rain and sun make covered courts usable all year, at more than the court itself.
  • Glass and turf quality: panoramic glass and pro turf cost more.
  • Ground: a flat, well-drained slab is what keeps the court true.
  • Salt air: galvanised and coated steel near the coast.

Permits, fencing beyond the court, lighting upgrades and clubhouse are separate.
