How Much Does It Cost to Build a Beach Club in the Riviera Maya? 2026 Guide

A beach club is a restaurant, a bar, a pool complex and a hotel back-of-house built on the hardest ground there is: sand, salt, wind and a federal beach. This guide gives the 2026 cost per m² for beach bars, full day clubs and spas, two worked budgets, what makes the price move on the coast, and the permit path that decides the design.

1. Cost per m² by project (2026)

ProjectTypical sizeCost (MXN/m² built)Typical budget (MXN)
Boutique beach bar / club200–400 m² built$25,000–$35,000$5M–$14M
Full beach club / day club500–1,200 m² + pools$35,000–$50,000$18M–$60M
Spa / wellness centre250–800 m²$22,000–$45,000$6M–$36M

The built area includes bar, kitchen, palapas, decks, restrooms, changing rooms and back-of-house. Pools are priced apart: $18,000–$35,000 MXN per m² of water surface, more for infinity edges and swim-up bars. Land or the concession, furniture, kitchen equipment and VAT are extra.

Ranges, not quotes: the site, the soil under the sand, the services you must bring yourself and the brand level move the final number by ±20–30%.

3. What moves the price on the beach

  • Foundations in sand: piles or deep footings to rock, and a structure that tolerates a high, salty water table.
  • Hurricanes: roofs, palapas, shade sails and glass calculated for wind, with storm-ready storage for furniture.
  • Salt: stainless 316, hot-dip galvanised steel, marine-grade concrete, sealed electrical equipment.
  • Services: many beach sites have no sewer and a weak grid; a treatment plant, generators or solar can be 10–15% of the budget.
  • Pools: infinity edges, swim-up bars and filtration sized for day-club crowds.
  • Sound: DJ sets need acoustic design so neighbours and the municipality stay on your side.

4. Permits decide the design

The first 20 metres from the high-tide line are federal: anything there — decks, loungers, shade — needs a ZOFEMAT concession. Dunes, vegetation or mangroves bring an environmental authorisation, and sea-turtle nesting rules limit lighting facing the beach. Add the municipal land use, the construction licence, Protección Civil and the alcohol licence. Building is the easy part; we review the permit file with your lawyer before the design is frozen. Heavy works on the beach are planned outside turtle-nesting season where the authorisation requires it, and before the high season you want to open for.
