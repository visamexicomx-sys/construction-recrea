How Much Does It Cost to Build in Cancún’s Hotel Zone? (2026)

In Cancún’s Hotel Zone, a new 300 m² lagoon-side house costs roughly $9M–$12.6M MXN ($500k–$700k USD) turnkey in 2026, but new houses are rare here. Most of the work we price in the Hotel Zone is inside existing condominium towers, and that has its own economics.

Related service: House construction in Cancún’s Hotel Zone

What gets built in the Hotel Zone

The strip between the Caribbean and the Nichupté lagoon was planned by FONATUR for hotels and condominiums. Single-family lots exist mainly on the lagoon side and in small residential pockets; the sea side is almost entirely towers. That means two kinds of project:

  • New houses on lagoon lots — few, expensive, with the lagoon federal zone along the water and high ground-water.
  • Condo and penthouse renovations — the bulk of the market: owners modernising 1980s–2000s units for their own use or for short-term rental.

Licences come from the municipality of Benito Juárez. In a tower, the condominium administration’s approval comes first: any work on structure, facade, windows or shared installations needs its sign-off, and its rules decide working hours, elevator use and debris removal.

Condo and penthouse renovation, per m² of unit:

  • Cosmetic refresh (paint, lighting, fixtures, partial furniture): $5,000–$9,000 MXN/m²
  • Full renovation (kitchen, bathrooms, floors, carpentry, A/C): $12,000–$20,000 MXN/m²
  • Penthouse or luxury gut renovation with new installations: $20,000–$32,000 MXN/m²
  • Impact-rated window and sliding-door replacement: $18,000–$35,000 MXN per m² of glazing

A 120 m² ocean-view condo with a full renovation typically lands at $1.5M–$2.4M MXN; a 300 m² penthouse gut renovation at $6M–$9.6M.

What drives cost and time inside a tower

  • Elevator and hours. Materials go up a shared elevator with protection and booked slots; noisy work is limited to weekday hours. On a full renovation this can add 15–25% to labour time.
  • Debris. Everything comes down the same way and leaves in bagged loads; the administration often sets the route and charges deposits.
  • Hidden condition. Old risers, aluminium wiring, corroded window frames and spalling balcony slabs show up once walls open. Budget a 10–15% contingency on older towers.
  • Salt and storms. Sea-facing units need 316 stainless, marine-grade aluminium and impact-rated glazing that matches the facade rules.

A full condo renovation takes 3–5 months, a penthouse 5–8 months, a new lagoon house 12–16 months after approvals. If you are renting the unit, schedule the work in the low season and agree a fixed price with milestone payments and weekly photo reports.
