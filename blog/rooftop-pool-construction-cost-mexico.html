Rooftop Pools: Structure, Waterproofing and Real Cost

Rooftop plunge pools are the signature of Tulum and Playa del Carmen condos and villas. A metre of water weighs a tonne per m², and a leak runs straight into the bedrooms below. Done right, they last; done cheaply, they are the most expensive repair in the building.

Related: Natural Pools and Cenote-Style Pools in the Riviera Maya · Pool Safety for Homes and Rental Villas in Mexico

1. Structure

  • Slab and beams designed for water + finishes + people: typically 1.2–1.8 t/m² over the pool area.
  • Solid slab or specifically designed system; not standard vigueta y bovedilla (see slab types).
  • On existing buildings: structural assessment and often reinforcement before anything else.

2. Waterproofing and leaks

  • Pool shell in watertight concrete with crystalline admixture.
  • Second membrane below the shell, separate from the roof waterproofing.
  • Leak-detection drain or inspection point under the pool.
  • Overflow and emergency drain sized for tropical rain.

4. Practical details

  • Equipment room on the roof or below, with access for maintenance.
  • Wind: furniture, glass and railings designed for hurricane loads.
  • Condo approval and licence for structural works.
  • Salt chlorination needs 316 stainless and titanium heat exchangers.
