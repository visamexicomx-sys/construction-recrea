How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Bacalar? (2026)

Turnkey construction in Bacalar runs about 3% below Cancún for the same specification. Below are our 2026 ranges per m², example totals and the local factors that explain the difference. Municipality: Bacalar.

3. What makes Bacalar different

  • Lagoon setbacks. Lots on the Laguna de Bacalar face federal and municipal setbacks, stromatolite protection and strict wastewater rules; most need a treatment plant, not a septic tank. See lagoon rules.
  • Labour and land. Labour is cheaper than in the north, but specialised trades (pools, aluminium, A/C) travel from Chetumal or Tulum.
  • Ground. Shallow water table near the lagoon and soft sascab pockets; a soil study is a must.

4. Not included

  • Land and closing costs
  • Design, licences and DRO: 6–10% of construction
  • Pool, landscaping, furniture
  • Contingency 5–10% (see overruns)

5. Before you buy land here

Run the land due diligence checklist and price utility connections; in Bacalar they can change the total more than the finish level.
