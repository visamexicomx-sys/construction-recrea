How Much Does It Cost to Build on Isla Dorada? (2026)

A 400 m² lagoon-front residence on Isla Dorada costs roughly $12M–$17.6M MXN ($667k–$978k USD) turnkey in 2026. Vacant lots on the island are almost gone, so most projects start with an existing house — and the decision to keep it or take it down drives the whole budget.

Related service: House construction on Isla Dorada

A gated island in the Nichupté lagoon

Isla Dorada is a small private island inside the Hotel Zone, reached over a single guarded bridge, with large lots facing the Nichupté lagoon and most houses dating from the 1990s and 2000s. That history produces a specific set of costs.

  • Existing structures. Thirty years of lagoon humidity and salt air mean chloride-induced corrosion at slab edges, columns and balconies. A condition survey with opening-up costs $50,000–$160,000 MXN and tells you whether the shell is worth keeping.
  • Lagoon federal zone. The strip along the water is federal; docks, seawalls and boathouses need their own environmental and concession paperwork, separate from the house licence. Mangrove fringes are protected and cannot be cleared.
  • One way in. Every truck, concrete pump and crane crosses the same bridge under the association’s rules. Large pours need scheduling with security, and oversized deliveries may need off-hours windows.

The licence comes from the municipality of Benito Juárez, with the island association’s approval first. Its rules on height, setbacks, worker access and hours apply to remodels as much as to new builds.

Keep-or-rebuild figures for an existing house:

  • Demolition and debris removal across the bridge: $250,000–$700,000 MXN
  • Deep remodel keeping the structure: $16,000–$30,000 MXN/m², plus whatever structural repair the survey finds
  • New dock or seawall rebuild: $500,000–$1.8M, permits included
  • Impact-rated glazing for a full lagoon facade: $900,000–$2.5M

The rule of thumb we use: once structural repair passes about 25–30% of the rebuild cost, rebuilding gives a better house for similar money — higher ceilings, an open lagoon facade and a modern MEP installation instead of patched ones.

Lagoon-front specification and programme

The lagoon is calmer than the open sea, but it is not gentle on buildings. What we specify as standard here:

  • Finished floor level raised above the recorded storm-surge line, with drainage that can cope when the lagoon is high.
  • 316 stainless for exterior fixings and railings, marine-grade coatings and condensers with coastal protection.
  • Waterproofed foundations and a membrane under the slab: the water table here is the lagoon itself.

Expect 3–5 months for survey, design and approvals and 10–16 months for a full rebuild; deep remodels run 6–10 months. Many owners are abroad during the work, so the contract should have a fixed price against a line-item budget, milestone payments and weekly dated photo and video reports.
