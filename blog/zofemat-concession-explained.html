ZOFEMAT: What It Means for a Beachfront Lot

Every beachfront lot in Quintana Roo ends where the Zona Federal Marítimo Terrestre (ZOFEMAT) begins. It is federal land: you cannot own it, but you can hold a concession to use it. Getting this right decides what goes on the sand side of a villa, hotel or beach club.

Related: Ejido Land in Mexico · Karst Voids and Caverns · Utility Connection Costs in the Riviera Maya · Land Due Diligence Checklist Before Buying a Lot in Mexico

1. Where the line is

ZOFEMAT is the 20 m strip measured inland from the highest ordinary tide line (on beaches; it changes around lagoons and estuaries). Behind it, land reclaimed from the sea (terrenos ganados al mar) is also federal. The official line is set by a SEMARNAT delimitation, not by the survey on your deed, and it can differ by several metres from what the seller shows.

3. The concession process

  • Request to SEMARNAT (Dirección de ZOFEMAT) with the property deed, plan of the front strip and intended use.
  • Federal delimitation if none exists for the stretch.
  • Opinion of the municipality and, near reefs or turtle beaches, of CONANP / PROFEPA.
  • Title of concession, typically up to 15 years and renewable.
  • Annual fee (derechos) per m² under the Ley Federal de Derechos, by zone and type of use.

5. Mistakes that cost the most

  • Designing the pool or terrace on the seller's survey instead of the federal line.
  • Buying a lot whose previous owner never paid ZOFEMAT fees: the debt and penalties follow the use.
  • Building a concrete palapa or deck in the strip without concession: PROFEPA can order demolition.
  • Ignoring turtle nesting rules for lighting and work hours.

6. How we handle it

On every beachfront project we commission the federal-line plan first, design the setbacks from it and file the concession in parallel with the municipal licence so the beach side is ready when the house is.

FAQ
