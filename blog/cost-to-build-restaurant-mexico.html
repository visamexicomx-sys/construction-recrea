How Much Does It Cost to Build a Restaurant in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: a new restaurant building costs about $16,000–$28,000 MXN/m²; fitting out a dining room and bar in an existing shell $18,000–$35,000/m² (signature venues $35,000–$55,000), plus a commercial kitchen package of $1.5M–$6M MXN before equipment.

4. What moves the price

  • The kitchen: extraction route to the roof, gas, grease trap and refrigeration often cost as much as the dining room.
  • The shell: a unit without power, drainage or an extraction route adds months and money.
  • Location: Quinta Avenida and the Hotel Zone add access and logistics premiums.
  • Licences: food, alcohol and Civil Protection shape the layout; design for them from day one.

Kitchen equipment, furniture, land and VAT are extra. Our kitchen packages include the installations, not the cooking line.
