How Much Does It Cost to Build an Office Building in Chetumal? (2026)

Prices for an office building in Chetumal in 2026: core and shell per m², fit-out and parking, worked budgets for 600–2,000 m² and the permit path. Figures from our own quotes.

Related: Office building construction in Chetumal · Office fit-out in Chetumal · Office fit-out cost in Chetumal · Office building cost in the Riviera Maya · Offices

Chetumal prices run about 8% below Cancún. Land, VAT, design and permits (6–10%) are extra.

3. Where offices are built in Chetumal

  • Av. Héroes and downtown: government agencies, banks and professional offices
  • Av. Insurgentes and Av. Constituyentes: service, medical and regional corporate offices
  • Boulevard Bahía: representative offices and institutions facing the bay

Chetumal is the state capital: government agencies, institutions, banks and regional corporate offices create steady demand for office buildings and renovations of older ones. Costs run about 8% below Cancún, the climate is just as hot and humid, and public-sector tenants bring their own accessibility and security requirements.

4. What moves the number

  • Parking ratio sets lettable area.
  • Height: lifts and fire protection above 3–4 storeys.
  • Shell vs fitted suites: fitted suites cost more but lease faster to small tenants.
  • Backup power and data redundancy: what tenants pay rent for.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use, construction licence with DRO, Civil Protection, accessibility, CFE connection and municipal licences in Othón P. Blanco. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 6–14 months.
