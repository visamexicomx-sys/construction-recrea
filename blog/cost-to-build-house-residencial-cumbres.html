How Much Does It Cost to Build in Residencial Cumbres? (2026)

A new 200 m² house in Residencial Cumbres costs roughly $3.6M–$4.8M MXN ($200k–$267k USD) turnkey in 2026. But Cumbres is an established community, and many projects here start with an existing house: an addition, a second storey or a rebuild. Those follow different numbers.

Related service: House construction in Residencial Cumbres

An established community: buy, then build

Residencial Cumbres is one of Cancún’s long-established gated communities, with mature trees, larger lots than newer subdivisions and a mix of houses from the 1990s onward. Buyers often choose it for the location and the lot, then plan to change the house. Three common projects:

  • Second-storey addition on a single-storey house. The existing foundations and columns were rarely designed for it; a structural assessment decides between reinforcement and a separate structure.
  • Extension and modernisation — a bigger kitchen, a master suite, a pool and terrace, new installations throughout.
  • Rebuild when the existing house is too compromised or too small for the lot.

The community has its own rules on height, setbacks, facade and works, and licences come from Benito Juárez. Additions need a licence and DRO just like new builds; skipping it causes problems at sale.

For existing houses:

  • Structural assessment for an addition: $30,000–$80,000 MXN
  • Foundation and column reinforcement for a second storey: $250,000–$700,000
  • Second-storey addition, finished: $17,000–$24,000 MXN/m²
  • Full modernisation of an existing house (installations, kitchen, bathrooms, floors, windows): $9,000–$16,000 MXN/m²
  • Demolition for a rebuild: $150,000–$400,000

Living next to the works

In a mature community, the neighbours are already there, and the committee enforces it. What that means for the project:

  • Working hours and noise. Restricted hours lengthen the programme by a few weeks; plan noisy demolition in a single block.
  • Trees. Mature trees are valuable and often protected; roots near footings need an engineered solution, not a chainsaw.
  • Living in during the work is possible for modernisations done in phases, but not for a second storey: the roof comes off.

Expect 2–3 months for assessment, design and permits. A second-storey addition takes 4–7 months, a full modernisation 3–6, a new 200–250 m² house 8–11. Fixed price, line-item budget, milestone payments and weekly photo reports as standard.
