How Much Does It Cost to Build an Office Building in Tulum? (2026)

Prices for an office building in Tulum in 2026: core and shell per m², fit-out and parking, worked budgets for 600–2,000 m² and the permit path. Figures from our own quotes.

Tulum prices run about 13% above Cancún. Land, VAT, design and permits (6–10%) are extra.

3. Where offices are built in Tulum

  • Av. Tulum in town: notaries, banks, real-estate agencies and professional offices
  • Aldea Zama and La Veleta: coworking, studios and developers’ sales offices
  • Region 15 and Highway 307: administration and site offices for construction and hospitality

In Tulum offices are small and mixed-use, and the infrastructure decides the project: power and internet are less reliable than in Cancún, so backup power and redundant connections are not optional. Height limits are low and costs run about 13% above Cancún because of logistics and labour.

4. What moves the number

  • Parking ratio sets lettable area.
  • Height: lifts and fire protection above 3–4 storeys.
  • Shell vs fitted suites: fitted suites cost more but lease faster to small tenants.
  • Backup power and data redundancy: what tenants pay rent for.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use, construction licence with DRO, Civil Protection, accessibility, CFE connection and municipal licences in Tulum. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 6–14 months.
