3. Soft costs

  • Design and engineering 5–8%
  • Permits, DRO and impact studies 2–4%
  • Condominium regime and notary 0.5–1%
  • Contingency 5–8%
  • Utility connections priced per project

4. Building in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is Cancún’s newest coastal corridor: brand residences, condo towers next to the resorts and gated communities. Coastal setbacks, hurricane design and premium specs push costs about 20% above downtown Cancún.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and density, environmental review, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection in Isla Mujeres, then the condominium regime. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 10–18 months for 4–40 units.
