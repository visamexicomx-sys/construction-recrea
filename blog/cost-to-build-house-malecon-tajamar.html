How Much Does It Cost to Build in Malecón Tajamar? (2026)

Building in Malecón Tajamar costs roughly $24,000–$32,000 MXN/m² in 2026 for townhouses and boutique mid-rise; a 200 m² residence lands at $4.8M–$6.4M MXN ($267k–$356k USD). The price is not the first question here, though. Before anything is designed, check what the lot is legally allowed to build.

Related service: House construction in Malecón Tajamar

Check the lot before the design

Malecón Tajamar is a FONATUR urban development on the Nichupté lagoon shore next to downtown Cancún, planned for residential towers, commercial lots and a lagoon-front promenade. In 2016 mangrove clearing on part of the site led to widely reported legal challenges and court suspensions, and the environmental question has come back since. For a buyer or developer that means:

  • Lot-by-lot legal check. Confirm the land-use certificate, any environmental authorisation and the current status of court orders that affect that specific lot before you pay for design.
  • Protected vegetation. Mangrove is federally protected. Some lots have remnants or buffers that cannot be built on, which changes the usable footprint.
  • A municipal file in Benito Juárez that builds on the above, with a DRO and, for mid-rise, the structural and Protección Civil requirements of a multi-storey building.

We do this check as the first paid stage. It costs a fraction of a set of architectural drawings and stops projects that would never get a licence.

Typical additional lines:

  • Geotechnical study and foundation design for lagoon-edge soil: $60,000–$150,000 MXN
  • Piles or a reinforced mat for mid-rise: $1.2M–$4M depending on height
  • Elevator, per stop: $350,000–$600,000
  • Penthouse or apartment fit-out from shell to furnished: $14,000–$26,000 MXN/m²

Engineering on the lagoon edge

The soil and water here are the lagoon’s, and they set the structure.

  • Shallow ground-water means dewatering for basements, cisterns and lift pits, and waterproofed foundations as standard.
  • Variable fill and organic layers often push mid-rise buildings onto piles; a two-storey townhouse may be fine on a mat. The geotechnical report decides, not the architect’s preference.
  • Storm and salt. Lagoon-facing facades get impact-rated glazing and marine-grade metalwork, like the Hotel Zone across the water.

After the due-diligence stage, allow 4–8 months for design and licensing and 10–14 months for a townhouse; a 4–6 storey building runs 16–24 months. Use a fixed price against a line-item budget and milestone payments, and keep every legal document in the project file. Lenders and buyers will ask for them.
