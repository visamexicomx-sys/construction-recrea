How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Riviera Cancún? (2026)

3. What drives the price in Riviera Cancún

Riviera Cancún is the gated golf and beach community south of Cancún, on the way to Puerto Morelos: a Jack Nicklaus signature golf course, beachfront condos and residential villa lots, with its own design rules and controlled access.

  • Beach and golf lots. Beachfront lots carry the federal maritime zone (ZOFEMAT) and coastal setbacks; golf-front lots follow the course’s setbacks and fencing rules. We check both before design.
  • Ground and water. The ground here is limestone with a shallow water table: foundations, cisterns and pools are designed for it, and soil studies come first.
  • Hurricane standard. Impact-rated windows, anchored roofs and protected equipment cost more but protect the house and its insurance.
  • Climate systems. Inverter A/C, solar with batteries and good insulation lower the running cost in a hot, humid city.

4. Licences and timeline

The association reviews the project first; then the local municipality issues the land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings. Design and approvals take about 2–4 months.

StageTime
Design and association review1–3 months

Finishes, systems, pool and handover4–6 months
