3. Power and cooling are the budget

The building itself is a small part of a data centre budget; power and cooling are most of it. We size UPS, generators and cooling to the real IT load and growth plan, decide the redundancy level (N, N+1, 2N) with you and phase equipment so you do not pay for capacity years before it is used.

4. Designed for this climate

Heat, humidity, salt air and hurricanes are the enemies here. The room is a sealed concrete box above flood level, with humidity control, corrosion-resistant equipment, fuel for days of autonomy and a tested switchover before handover.

5. Not included

  • Land, VAT and financing
  • Design and permits: 6–10% of construction
  • Contingency 5–10%
