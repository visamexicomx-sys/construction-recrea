How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Puerto Morelos? (2026)

Building a solid 150 m² family home in Puerto Morelos costs roughly $2.60M–$3.20M MXN ($144k–$178k USD) turnkey in 2026. Below are real budgets by home size, the local factors that move the price, and how long it takes.

Turnkey cost by home size in Puerto Morelos

Standard-finish turnkey ranges (structure, installations and finishes) for Puerto Morelos — land, pool and furniture excluded:

Home sizeTurnkey (MXN)Turnkey (USD)
100 m²$1.70M–$2.10M$94k–$117k
150 m²$2.55M–$3.15M$142k–$175k
200 m²$3.40M–$4.20M$189k–$233k
250 m²$4.25M–$5.25M$236k–$292k

Reference: $17,000–$21,000 MXN/m² standard finish. USD/MXN ≈ 18. Excludes land, pool and furniture.

Local factors in Puerto Morelos

  • Puerto Morelos is its own municipality (since 2016) with its own permits, and the reef national park adds environmental care; logistics are calmer, keeping costs mid-range.
  • Soil study and foundation type set the base cost in Puerto Morelos — karst limestone and coastal lots need engineered foundations.
  • Finishes and add-ons (pool, rooftop, solar, landscaping) are quoted separately and can shift the final price 20–40%.

Timeline

A 150 m² home in Puerto Morelos takes about 7–10 months from permit approval to handover, depending on finish complexity and weather.

Keep your budget under control

Insist on a fixed-price written contract with a line-item budget, milestone payments and a written warranty. Vague per-m² handshakes are where budgets blow up.
