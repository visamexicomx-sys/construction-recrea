Water Filtration: Softener, Reverse Osmosis and UV for a Caribbean House

Peninsula water comes from limestone: very hard, sometimes slightly brackish near the coast, and not recommended for drinking straight from the tap. A filtration train designed with the house protects fixtures and gives drinkable water without buying jugs.

Related: Stormwater and Flooding on a Lot in the Riviera Maya · Foundations on Beach Sand in the Riviera Maya

1. The usual train

StageWhat it doesInstalled cost (MXN, 2026)
Sediment filterSand and particles from cistern$3,000–$8,000
Carbon filterChlorine, taste, odour$5,000–$15,000
Whole-house softenerRemoves hardness (scale)$18,000–$55,000
UV disinfectionBacteria and viruses, especially with wells or cistern$8,000–$25,000
Reverse osmosis under kitchen sinkDrinking water$6,000–$18,000
Whole-house reverse osmosisDrinkable water at every tap, brackish wells$60,000–$250,000

2. Choosing

  • Municipal water (Aguakan / CAPA): sediment + carbon + softener, RO at kitchen.
  • Well water: lab test first; add UV, and RO if salinity is high.
  • Rental villa: RO at kitchen and bar so guests don't need bottled water.
  • Off-grid: see desalination and off-grid homes.

4. Plan space

Put the equipment in the pump room after the cistern pump, with a drain for backwash and RO reject. See water pressure systems and water heaters.
