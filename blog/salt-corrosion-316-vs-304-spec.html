Salt-Air Corrosion: What Survives on the Coast (and 316 vs 304)

Within roughly 300 m of the sea in Cancún, Isla Mujeres, Cozumel or Tulum, salt spray destroys the wrong materials in two to five years. This is the specification we use on beachfront work, and what it adds to the cost.

2. Stainless: 316 vs 304

304 stainless rusts (tea staining, pitting) within months on a first-line façade. 316 adds molybdenum and holds up if it is washed periodically. For railings, balustrade fixings, pool fittings, hinges and exterior screws we specify 316 within 500 m of the sea. The cost difference is 20–35% on the item, typically under 1% of the build.

4. What it adds to the budget

On a first-line villa the marine specification adds roughly 3–6% to the construction cost. Replacing failed railings, windows and A/C after five years costs several times that. See aluminium windows and beachfront house construction.

5. Maintenance

  • Fresh-water rinse of stainless and aluminium every 1–3 months.
  • Re-seal exterior concrete and stone every 2–3 years.
  • A/C coil cleaning every 3 months on first line.
  • Inspect railing fixings yearly.
