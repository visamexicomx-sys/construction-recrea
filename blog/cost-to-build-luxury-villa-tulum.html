Cost to Build a Luxury Villa in Tulum (2026)

A luxury villa in Tulum — 300–450 m², pool, premium finishes — typically costs $11M–$27M MXN ($610,000–$1.5M USD) turnkey in 2026. Here is what drives the number and why Tulum commands a premium.

Luxury villa budget ranges (2026)

Villa profileBuilt areaTurnkey cost (MXN)Turnkey cost (USD)
Boutique villa250–300 m²$8.5M–$12M$470k–$670k
Luxury villa300–400 m²$13M–$20M$720k–$1.1M
Ultra-luxury / beachfront400–600 m²$22M–$40M+$1.2M–$2.2M+

Includes structure, premium finishes, pool and landscaping. Excludes land and furniture. USD/MXN ≈ 18.

What luxury buyers pay for

  • Signature finishes: chukum walls, tropical hardwoods, imported stone, seamless indoor-outdoor design.
  • Amenities: infinity pool, rooftop lounge, home cinema, wine cellar, gym, smart-home automation.
  • Sustainability: solar, rainwater harvesting, greywater treatment — increasingly expected in Tulum.
  • Architecture: custom design by a named architect, double heights, cantilevered volumes.
  • Jungle & cenote integration: building around protected vegetation under SEMA rules.

ROI: why investors build in Tulum

A well-run luxury Airbnb villa in Tulum can generate $80,000–$180,000 USD/year in gross rental income at strong occupancy, with luxury nightly rates of $600–$2,000+. Combined with land appreciation, many owners target a 6–9 year payback.

Timeline

Luxury villas take 10–16 months given custom detailing, imported materials and environmental permitting. We manage the full process — design, DRO, SEMA permits, construction and handover.
