How Much Does an Office Fit-Out Cost in Cancún? (2026)

Prices to fit out an office in Cancún in 2026: cost per m² by level, examples for 60 and 150 m², renovation prices and what obra gris leaves out.

Related: Office fit-out in Cancún · Office renovation in Cancún · Cost to build an office building in Cancún · Office fit-out cost in the Riviera Maya · Offices

1. Office fit-out cost per m² in Cancún (2026)

LevelPer m²60 m²150 m²

3. What obra gris usually leaves out

  • Floor finishes and levelling
  • Ceilings and partitions
  • Toilets inside the unit
  • A/C units and lighting
  • Data cabling
  • Often the panel and meter connection

4. Office areas in Cancún

  • Av. Bonampak and Malecón Tajamar: the newest corporate and mixed-use towers, many delivered as shell (obra gris)
  • Cumbres, Av. Huayacán and Av. Nichupté: new office towers and professional suites next to the residential growth
  • Downtown avenues — Tulum, Cobá, Yaxchilán — with older office buildings ready for renovation
  • Puerto Cancún mixed-use towers and the airport corridor for tour operators, airlines and logistics

5. Permits and timeline

Fit-out licence and Civil Protection in Benito Juárez, the building’s approval, and a DRO for structural or facade changes. 6–12 weeks for most offices of 80–300 m².
