3. Seats, sound and uptime

A call centre's KPIs depend on three things the building controls: how many seats fit, how quiet the floor is and whether power and network stay up. We plan density with your workforce team, treat acoustics from the ceiling down and design power and data with redundancy you can test before go-live.

4. Fast, phased go-live

Operations usually start with a first wave of seats. We fit out in phases, so training and the first campaign start while the rest of the floor is finished.

5. Not included

  • Land, VAT and financing
  • Design and permits: 6–10% of construction
  • Contingency 5–10%
