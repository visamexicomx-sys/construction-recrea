4. Building in Bacalar

Bacalar is small, lagoon-driven and protected: setbacks, wastewater treatment and limited footprint are not negotiable, and the market is growing fast. Costs run about 3% below Cancún.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and density, environmental review, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection in Bacalar, then the condominium regime. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 10–18 months for 4–40 units.
