2. The full budget, not just construction

  • Construction: 60–70% of the total.
  • FF&E and OS&E: furniture, equipment, linens, operating supplies — 10–20%.
  • Design, permits and supervision: 8–12% of construction.
  • Pre-opening: hiring, training, systems, launch — 3–6%.
  • Contingency: 5–10%.

4. Building a hotel in Bacalar

Bacalar has eco-hotels, cabins and boutique hotels on the lagoon of seven colours. The lagoon is protected: wastewater treatment, setbacks and limited footprint are not negotiable, and the best projects use palapa, wood and local stone. Costs run about 3% below Cancún.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use, environmental impact (state or federal MIA on the coast), construction licence with DRO, Civil Protection, water, wastewater and CFE connections, then operating licences. Design and permits 4–12 months; construction 10–16 months for a boutique, 18–30 for a resort.
