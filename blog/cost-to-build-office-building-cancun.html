How Much Does It Cost to Build an Office Building in Cancún? (2026)

Prices for an office building in Cancún in 2026: core and shell per m², fit-out and parking, worked budgets for 600–2,000 m² and the permit path. Figures from our own quotes.

3. Where offices are built in Cancún

  • Av. Bonampak and Malecón Tajamar: the newest corporate and mixed-use towers, many delivered as shell (obra gris)
  • Cumbres, Av. Huayacán and Av. Nichupté: new office towers and professional suites next to the residential growth
  • Downtown avenues — Tulum, Cobá, Yaxchilán — with older office buildings ready for renovation
  • Puerto Cancún mixed-use towers and the airport corridor for tour operators, airlines and logistics

Cancún’s office demand comes from tour operators and DMCs, airlines, logistics, professional services, medical practices and a growing number of back-office operations serving North America. Tenants ask first about parking, then backup power, internet redundancy and A/C that copes; the municipality of Benito Juárez checks parking, Civil Protection and accessibility closely, and the high water table makes basements expensive.

4. What moves the number

  • Parking ratio sets lettable area.
  • Height: lifts and fire protection above 3–4 storeys.
  • Shell vs fitted suites: fitted suites cost more but lease faster to small tenants.
  • Backup power and data redundancy: what tenants pay rent for.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use, construction licence with DRO, Civil Protection, accessibility, CFE connection and municipal licences in Benito Juárez. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 6–14 months.
