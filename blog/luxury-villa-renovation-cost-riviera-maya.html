Luxury Villa Renovation in the Riviera Maya: Cost per m², Renovate or Rebuild

Many of the best-located villas in Playacar, Puerto Cancún, Tulum and Akumal were built 10–20 years ago. The lot and the view cannot be replaced; the bathrooms, the windows and the systems can. This guide gives the 2026 cost per m² for three renovation scopes, a worked budget for a 400 m² villa, what we usually find behind the finishes, and how to decide between renovating and rebuilding.

Related: Luxury villa renovation · Villa renovation in Cancún · Villa renovation in Playa del Carmen · Cost to build a luxury villa in Tulum · Luxury villas in Playacar

1. Cost per m² by scope (2026)

ScopeIncludesMXN per m²400 m² villa (MXN)
Cosmetic upgradePaint, lighting, fixtures, soft furnishing$5,000–$9,000$2M–$3.6M
Full interiorKitchen, bathrooms, floors, carpentry, A/C$12,000–$22,000$4.8M–$8.8M
Complete repositioningStructure, layout, impact glazing, MEP, pool$22,000–$38,000$8.8M–$15.2M

Figures are for the house; landscaping, furniture and VAT are extra. A complete repositioning usually lands well below the cost of demolishing and building new, because the structure, the foundations and often the permits stay.

2. What 10–20-year-old coastal villas hide

  • Corroded reinforcement in balconies, beams and columns near the sea: rust stains and cracks in the concrete cover.
  • Failed waterproofing on roofs and terraces, and water coming in around old aluminium windows.
  • Undersized electrical service for today’s A/C, kitchens, pool heat pumps and EV chargers.
  • Tired pool systems: leaking shells, old pumps and filtration, no automation.
  • Non-impact windows that are both a hurricane risk and an insurance problem.
  • Missing paperwork: extensions built without a licence that must be regularised before a sale.

We survey before you buy or before you sign a budget: structure, corrosion, moisture, installations and permits, with a written scope you can use in the negotiation.

3. Renovate or rebuild?

Renovate when…Rebuild when…
The structure is sound or repairable locallyCorrosion is spread through the frame
The layout works with moderate changesThe layout fights the lot, the view or the light
Existing permits cover the built areaYou need far more m² than the permits allow
You want to use the villa within a yearYou can wait 18–24 months

A complete renovation usually takes 6–12 months; a new luxury villa 12–24 months plus permits. The survey decides, and sometimes we recommend rebuilding.

4. Where the money makes the biggest difference

  • Impact glazing and new waterproofing: comfort, energy and insurance at once.
  • Kitchen and primary bathroom: what buyers and rental guests judge first.
  • Outdoor living: terraces, pool, outdoor kitchen and shade, where most of the time in the Caribbean is spent.
  • Systems: inverter A/C, solar with battery backup, water treatment and smart controls.

5. Renovating from abroad

Most villa owners live abroad. We work to a fixed price on a line-item budget, with weekly dated photo and video reports, a cost-to-date report and every change approved in writing before it is built. In condominiums and master-planned communities we also handle the design committee and the works rules.
