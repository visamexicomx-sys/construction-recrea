How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Selvamar, Playa del Carmen? (2026)

Prices for Selvamar in 2026: cost per m² by project type, worked budgets for 180–350 m², what makes building here cheaper or dearer, and the licence path. Figures from our own quotes in Playa del Carmen.

3. What drives the price in Selvamar

Selvamar is a residential community in Playa del Carmen built around protected green areas, in the Solidaridad municipality: house and villa lots between preserved jungle strips, with a development rulebook and a management that reviews setbacks and trees.

  • Green strips and trees. The preserved jungle strips cannot be touched, and trees on the lot are reviewed by the management before clearing; we plan the footprint around them.
  • Ground and water. The ground here is limestone with a shallow water table: foundations, cisterns and pools are designed for it, and soil studies come first.
  • Hurricane standard. Impact-rated windows, anchored roofs and protected equipment cost more but protect the house and its insurance.
  • Climate systems. Inverter A/C, solar with batteries and good insulation lower the running cost in a hot, humid city.

4. Licences and timeline

The association reviews the project first; then the Solidaridad municipality issues the land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings. Design and approvals take about 2–4 months.

StageTime
Design and association review1–3 months

5. Building from abroad

Many owners live abroad. We work on a fixed price per item, with weekly dated photo and video reports, a running cost report and every change approved in writing before it is built.
