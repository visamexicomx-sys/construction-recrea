How Much Does It Cost to Renovate a Hotel in Costa Mujeres? (2026)

Prices for a hotel renovation in Costa Mujeres in 2026: the three levels per key, line items from bathrooms to pools, budgets for 40 and 120-key hotels and how long each takes.

3. What each level includes

  • Soft refresh: paint, soft goods, LVT or carpet, lighting, artwork, some loose furniture.
  • Full room and bathroom: case goods, flooring, bathroom to the waterproofing, A/C and controls.
  • Repositioning: layout, facade and balconies, MEP and public areas, often for a new brand.

4. Renovating hotels in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is the newest resort corridor of Cancún: large all-inclusives, brand residences and new projects every year. Brand standards, hurricane design and coastal setbacks drive the budget; costs run about 20% above downtown Cancún.

Open sample rooms before fixing the budget: corroded pipes, failed waterproofing and rusted rebar are what turn a refresh into a full renovation on this coast.

5. Timeline

A mock-up room first (3–6 weeks), then production floor by floor: 1–2 weeks per floor for a soft refresh, 4–8 weeks for full rooms; a repositioning takes 6–14 months in phases.
