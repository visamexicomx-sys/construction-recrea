3. Hidden costs in the landlord’s shell

  • Electrical capacity and a dedicated meter
  • Drainage position, and a grease trap for food
  • An extraction route to the roof for food
  • Real ceiling height after ducts and lighting
  • What you may change in the storefront
  • Structure and waterproofing above the unit

Check them before signing and negotiate the shell or rent-free months against the list.

4. Retail streets and plazas in Puerto Morelos

  • The Highway 307 crossing and the start of the Ruta de los Cenotes: convenience, fuel and services for passing traffic
  • The colonia (Zetina Gasca), where the town’s everyday retail grows with new housing
  • The low-rise centre by the square and the pier: cafés, restaurants and small shops for visitors

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and the operating licence for your activity, the fit-out licence (DRO for structural or facade changes), Civil Protection before opening, health requirements for food and signage approval in Puerto Morelos. A retail fit-out takes 6–10 weeks once permits are in place.
