How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Puerto Aventuras? (2026)

Building a solid 150 m² family home in Puerto Aventuras costs roughly $2.81M–$3.46M MXN ($156k–$192k USD) turnkey in 2026. Below are real budgets by home size, the local factors that move the price, and how long it takes.

Turnkey cost by home size in Puerto Aventuras

Standard-finish turnkey ranges (structure, installations and finishes) for Puerto Aventuras — land, pool and furniture excluded:

Home sizeTurnkey (MXN)Turnkey (USD)
100 m²$1.85M–$2.25M$103k–$125k
150 m²$2.77M–$3.38M$154k–$188k
200 m²$3.70M–$4.50M$206k–$250k
250 m²$4.62M–$5.62M$257k–$312k

Reference: $18,500–$22,500 MXN/m² standard finish. USD/MXN ≈ 18. Excludes land, pool and furniture.

Local factors in Puerto Aventuras

  • As a gated marina community, HOA architectural approval, access fees and restricted work hours add time and cost on top of the Solidaridad municipal permit.
  • Soil study and foundation type set the base cost in Puerto Aventuras — karst limestone and coastal lots need engineered foundations.
  • Finishes and add-ons (pool, rooftop, solar, landscaping) are quoted separately and can shift the final price 20–40%.

Timeline

A 150 m² home in Puerto Aventuras takes about 7–10 months from permit approval to handover, depending on finish complexity and weather.

Keep your budget under control

Insist on a fixed-price written contract with a line-item budget, milestone payments and a written warranty. Vague per-m² handshakes are where budgets blow up.
