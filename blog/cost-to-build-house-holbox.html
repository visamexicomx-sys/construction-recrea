How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Holbox? (2026)

Turnkey construction in Holbox runs about 30% above Cancún for the same specification. Below are our 2026 ranges per m², example totals and the local factors that explain the difference. Municipality: Lázaro Cárdenas.

3. What makes Holbox different

  • No cars, sand streets. Materials go by boat from Chiquilá and then by small truck or cart; productivity is lower and logistics add 20–30% to materials.
  • Inside Yum Balam. The island is in a protected area; density, height and land use are tightly limited and environmental approval is the critical path. See what you can build.
  • Services. Power and water capacity are limited; plan for cisterns, efficient A/C and often solar backup.

4. Not included

  • Land and closing costs
  • Design, licences and DRO: 6–10% of construction
  • Pool, landscaping, furniture
  • Contingency 5–10% (see overruns)

5. Before you buy land here

Run the land due diligence checklist and price utility connections; in Holbox they can change the total more than the finish level.
