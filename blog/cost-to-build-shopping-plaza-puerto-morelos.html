How Much Does It Cost to Build a Shopping Plaza in Puerto Morelos? (2026)

Prices for a plaza comercial in Puerto Morelos in 2026: cost per leasable m², parking and services, worked budgets for 400–2,000 m² and the permit path. Figures from our own quotes.

Puerto Morelos prices run about 9% above Cancún because of logistics and labour. Land, VAT, design and permits (about 6–10% of construction) and tenant fit-outs are extra.

3. Where plazas work in Puerto Morelos

  • The Highway 307 crossing and the start of the Ruta de los Cenotes: convenience, fuel and services for passing traffic
  • The colonia (Zetina Gasca), where the town’s everyday retail grows with new housing
  • The low-rise centre by the square and the pier: cafés, restaurants and small shops for visitors

Puerto Morelos is small and low-rise, and it sits next to a protected reef and mangroves, so plazas here are modest: one or two levels, a convenience or pharmacy anchor and local services. Most new demand is along Highway 307 and in the colonia, where the housing is growing.

4. What moves the number

  • Parking ratio sets the leasable area that fits on the lot, so it changes the cost per leasable m² more than finishes.
  • Services: no sewer, short power or water add a treatment plant, a transformer and a cistern.
  • Food units add extraction, grease and services but pay the highest rents; plan two or three from day one.
  • Two storeys give more area on the same lot, but need stairs, a lift and accept lower rents upstairs.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and density, the construction licence with a DRO, Civil Protection for the development and the municipal licences in Puerto Morelos; projects on Highway 307 also need a federal access permit. Design and permits take about 3–6 months, construction 8–14 months.
