How Much Does a Parking Lot Cost in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: a complete asphalt parking lot costs $800–$1,400 MXN/m², concrete $1,000–$1,600/m² and permeable pavers $900–$1,600/m²; with circulation that is about $25,000–$70,000 per space including drainage and lighting.

4. What moves the price

  • Drainage: on karst, absorption wells and slopes decide whether the lot floods.
  • Base: poor soil or fill needs a thicker base.
  • Lighting and shade: poles, photocells and trees add per space.
  • Surface: concrete lasts longer, asphalt is cheaper and faster, pavers help infiltration.

Land, permits and gates or barriers are extra.
