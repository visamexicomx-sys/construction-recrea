How Much Does Structural Steel Cost in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: in 2026 structural steel supplied, fabricated, protected and erected costs about $55–$95 MXN/kg, plus $12–$22/kg to galvanise. A rigid-frame building with sheet roofing costs $4,500–$8,500 MXN/m², or $7,000–$13,000/m² with insulated panels.

4. What moves the price

  • Corrosion: near the sea, galvanising or a marine paint system is not optional.
  • Span and wind: wider spans and hurricane loads add kilos.
  • Connections: bolted, shop-welded connections are faster and better on site.
  • Cladding: insulated panel costs more but cools the building.

Foundations, slabs and permits are separate.
