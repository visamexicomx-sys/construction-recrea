How Much Does It Cost to Build a Warehouse in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: in 2026 a basic steel bodega costs from $6,500 MXN/m², a standard warehouse with offices and docks $9,000–$12,000/m², a logistics or premium warehouse $12,000–$16,000+ and a refrigerated warehouse $16,000–$28,000/m².

4. What moves the price

  • Clear height and span: taller, wider column-free space needs heavier steel.
  • Floor slab: forklift and racking loads need an industrial slab with joints planned.
  • Docks and offices: each dock and the office block add fixed cost.
  • Refrigeration: chilled and frozen zones double the cost per m².

Land, VAT, racking and the refrigeration equipment beyond the plant are extra. Coastal sites need hurricane wind loads and corrosion protection.
