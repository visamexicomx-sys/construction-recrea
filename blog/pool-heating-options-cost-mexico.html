Pool Heating: Heat Pump, Solar or Gas in the Riviera Maya

Pool water here is 28–30 °C in summer but can drop to 23–25 °C from December to March, exactly when most foreign guests arrive. For rental villas a heated pool is now an expected amenity. This is what it costs.

1. Options (2026)

SystemInstalled cost (MXN)Running costNotes
Heat pump$60,000–$180,000Low–mediumBest all-round; very efficient in warm air
Solar collectors$40,000–$120,000Almost zeroNeeds 50–80% of pool area in roof collectors
LP gas heater$35,000–$90,000HighFast heat-up; for occasional use or jacuzzis
Thermal cover$8,000–$40,000—Cuts heat loss 50%+; makes any system cheaper

2. Sizing

A heat pump for a 40–60 m³ villa pool is typically 17–30 kW thermal. Wind, shade and an infinity edge increase losses; an infinity pool can need 30–50% more capacity.

3. Running cost

With a cover and a heat pump, holding 28 °C in winter in a 50 m³ pool costs roughly MXN 2,500–6,000 per month in electricity; without a cover, about double. Gas is typically 2–3 times more. Pair with solar panels to avoid the DAC tariff.

4. Plan it in the build

  • Space for equipment, ventilation for the heat pump.
  • Electrical capacity sized from the start (see utilities).
  • Stainless or titanium heat exchangers near the sea.
  • Piping prepared even if the heater is added later.
