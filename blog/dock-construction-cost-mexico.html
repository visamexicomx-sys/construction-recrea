How Much Does a Private Dock Cost in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: a fixed dock on piles with hardwood or composite deck costs $9,000–$16,000 MXN/m² — a 2 × 10 m dock about $180,000–$320,000 before permits. A concrete pier $18,000–$32,000/m², a floating pontoon $8,000–$15,000/m² and a boat lift $250,000–$650,000.

3. What moves the price

  • Water and seabed: depth, rock or sand and wave exposure decide piles or floats.
  • Federal zone: works over water need a federal concession and environmental permit.
  • Materials: tropical hardwood, composite or concrete trade price against maintenance.
  • Hurricanes: removable or floating elements reduce storm damage.

Permits (federal zone, environmental) are separate and can take months.
