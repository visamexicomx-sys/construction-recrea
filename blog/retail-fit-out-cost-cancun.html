How Much Does a Retail Fit-Out Cost in Cancún? (2026)

3. Hidden costs in the landlord’s shell

  • Electrical capacity and a dedicated meter
  • Drainage position, and a grease trap for food
  • An extraction route to the roof for food
  • Real ceiling height after ducts and lighting
  • What you may change in the storefront
  • Structure and waterproofing above the unit

Check them before signing and negotiate the shell or rent-free months against the list.

4. Retail streets and plazas in Cancún

  • Downtown avenues — Bonampak, Tulum, Kabah, López Portillo — around Plaza Las Américas and Malecón Américas
  • The north and west growth along Av. Huayacán, Arco Norte and the new subdivisions, where neighbourhood plazas follow the housing
  • The Hotel Zone on Boulevard Kukulcán, with La Isla and Kukulcán Plaza: tourist retail, restaurants and high rents
  • Puerto Cancún, with Marina Town Center and mixed-use towers: premium retail and services

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and the operating licence for your activity, the fit-out licence (DRO for structural or facade changes), Civil Protection before opening, health requirements for food and signage approval in Benito Juárez. A retail fit-out takes 6–10 weeks once permits are in place.
