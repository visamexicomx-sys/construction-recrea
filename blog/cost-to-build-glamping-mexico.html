3. Permits for a light build

Glamping is often approved faster than a hotel because it clears little and builds lightly, but the environmental review still decides the density, the setbacks from water and the treatment of wastewater. We prepare the site plan around the vegetation and file the land use, environmental and construction permits together.

4. Built for hurricanes

Tents and domes are engineered for wind and have a plan to strike or secure fabric before a storm; decks and cabins are anchored to point footings. After the season, the site reopens in days, not months.

5. Not included

  • Land, VAT and financing
  • Design and permits: 6–10% of construction
  • Operator equipment and furniture beyond what is listed
  • Contingency 5–10%
