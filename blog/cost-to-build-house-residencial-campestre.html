How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Residencial Campestre, Cancún? (2026)

Prices for Residencial Campestre in 2026: cost per m² by project type, worked budgets for 180–350 m², what makes building here cheaper or dearer, and the licence path. Figures from our own quotes in Cancún.

3. What drives the price in Residencial Campestre

Residencial Campestre is one of Cancún’s traditional gated communities, on Avenida Colosio in the south of the city, in the Benito Juárez municipality: large lots with mature trees, family houses and new apartment projects, its own roundabout entrance and 24/7 access control.

  • Large lots, old trees. Lots range from a few hundred to a few thousand m², with mature trees and association setbacks; we plan the footprint around both.
  • Ground and water. Cancún sits on limestone with a shallow water table: foundations, cisterns and pools are designed for it, and soil studies come first.
  • Hurricane standard. Impact-rated windows, anchored roofs and protected equipment cost more but protect the house and its insurance.
  • Climate systems. Inverter A/C, solar with batteries and good insulation lower the running cost in a hot, humid city.

4. Licences and timeline

The association reviews the project first; then the Benito Juárez municipality issues the land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings. Design and approvals take about 2–4 months.

StageTime
Design and association review1–3 months
Municipal licence1–2 months
Structure and envelope4–7 months
Finishes, systems, pool and handover4–6 months

5. Building from abroad

Many owners live abroad. We work on a fixed price per item, with weekly dated photo and video reports, a running cost report and every change approved in writing before it is built.
