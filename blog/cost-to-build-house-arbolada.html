How Much Does It Cost to Build a House in Arbolada, Cancún? (2026)

Prices for Arbolada in 2026: cost per m² by project type, worked budgets for 180–350 m², what makes building here cheaper or dearer, and the licence path. Figures from our own quotes in Cancún.

Prices are for the construction itself: land, design fees, licences, furniture and VAT are extra. The range inside each line depends on finishes, glazing, systems and how much is custom-made.

Add about 8–12% for architecture, structural design, licences and the DRO, and $400,000–$800,000 MXN for a pool.

3. What drives the price in Arbolada

Residencial Arbolada is the gated community on Avenida Huayacán, opposite Residencial Cumbres in southern Cancún, in the Benito Juárez municipality: houses and apartments around a central park, a bike path and green areas, with 24/7 access control.

  • Developer models and association rules. Extensions, second floors and roof terraces must respect the association’s setbacks and heights and the developer’s facade model; we check the rules before design.
  • Ground and water. The ground here is limestone with a shallow water table: foundations, cisterns and pools are designed for it, and soil studies come first.
  • Hurricane standard. Impact-rated windows, anchored roofs and protected equipment cost more but protect the house and its insurance.
  • Climate systems. Inverter A/C, solar with batteries and good insulation lower the running cost in a hot, humid city.

4. Licences and timeline

The association reviews the project first; then the Benito Juárez municipality issues the land-use certificate, alignment and construction licence with DRO-signed drawings. Design and approvals take about 2–4 months.

StageTime
Design and association review1–3 months
Municipal licence1–2 months
Structure and envelope4–7 months
Finishes, systems, pool and handover4–6 months

5. Extend or remodel the developer’s model

Most owners here start from a developer’s model: a second floor, a roof terrace, a bigger kitchen or a pool. We check the structure, the association rules and the licence first; a second floor usually costs close to new-build rates per m², a remodel well below.
