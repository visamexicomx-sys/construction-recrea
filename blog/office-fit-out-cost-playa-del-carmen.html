How Much Does an Office Fit-Out Cost in Playa del Carmen? (2026)

Prices to fit out an office in Playa del Carmen in 2026: cost per m² by level, examples for 60 and 150 m², renovation prices and what obra gris leaves out.

3. What obra gris usually leaves out

  • Floor finishes and levelling
  • Ceilings and partitions
  • Toilets inside the unit
  • A/C units and lighting
  • Data cabling
  • Often the panel and meter connection

4. Office areas in Playa del Carmen

  • Av. Constituyentes and Av. 30: notaries, lawyers, accountants, real-estate and developers’ offices
  • Downtown near Quinta Avenida: coworking and small studios for remote workers
  • Highway 307 and Corasol: developers’ sales and administration offices
  • The north (Av. CTM, Colosio): medical, dental and service offices for the new neighbourhoods

5. Permits and timeline

Fit-out licence and Civil Protection in Solidaridad, the building’s approval, and a DRO for structural or facade changes. 6–12 weeks for most offices of 80–300 m².
