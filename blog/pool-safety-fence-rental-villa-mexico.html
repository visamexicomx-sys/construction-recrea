Pool Safety: Fences, Drains and Alarms for Homes and Rentals

Drowning is silent and fast, and in rental villas the guests' children don't know the pool. Mexico has no single national rule for private pool fences, but civil protection, insurers and good practice set clear minimums, especially for rentals and hotels.

2. Equipment and construction

  • Anti-entrapment drains: dual main drains or anti-vortex covers; no single suction outlet.
  • Non-slip edges and steps; depth markings.
  • Lighting for night use.
  • Glass balustrades and infinity edges designed for impact and falls.
  • Equipment room locked.
