Construction Cost per Square Meter in Riviera Maya (2026)

The single most common question we get is: “how much does it cost per square meter to build?” Below are the real 2026 ranges we quote across Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Cancún, broken down by finish tier — plus what actually moves the number up or down.

Price per m² by finish tier (2026)

In Riviera Maya construction is quoted per square meter of built area (obra gris + acabados). These are turnkey ranges including structure, installations and finishes, excluding land and furniture:

What moves the price per m²

  • Soil & foundation: karst limestone, cenotes or mangrove-adjacent lots need engineered foundations that add cost.
  • Location & logistics: gated communities (Playacar, Mayakoba, Aldea Zamá) charge access fees and restrict work hours.
  • Finishes: chukum, imported stone, hardwood carpentry and smart-home systems can double the acabados budget.
  • Design complexity: pools, double heights, cantilevers and rooftop terraces raise the structural cost per m².
  • Salt air & climate: marine-grade materials near the coast protect against corrosion but cost more upfront.

Example: a 200 m² standard home

At a standard tier of ~$17,000 MXN/m², a 200 m² home runs about $3.4M MXN (~$189,000 USD) turnkey. Add a pool ($350k–$600k MXN), landscaping and furniture separately.
