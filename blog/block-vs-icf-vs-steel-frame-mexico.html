Block, ICF, Steel Frame or Concrete Walls: Choosing the Wall System

Almost every house on the peninsula is built in concrete block with reinforced columns. Foreign clients often ask about ICF or steel frame, familiar from the US and Canada. Here is how they compare in this climate, with labour that knows block very well and the others much less.

2. Hurricanes

Reinforced block, ICF and concrete walls all perform very well when tied to the foundation and slab. Steel frame can meet the code but needs careful anchoring and impact-resistant sheathing. See hurricane code.

3. Salt, humidity and termites

  • Block and concrete: no termites; needs good plaster and sealing.
  • ICF: EPS foam is not attacked by termites as food, but they can tunnel; use treated soil barrier.
  • Steel frame: galvanised steel must be kept away from salt and condensation; see salt-air materials.
  • Gypsum-based systems need moisture-rated boards in this climate.

4. Our recommendation

Block for most houses, with double block or insulation on hot façades; ICF where energy performance matters and the crew is experienced; steel frame for light additions, interior partitions and upper floors over existing structures. Prefab options are on prefab and modular houses.
