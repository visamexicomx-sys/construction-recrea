Commercial Permits Required

Land Use (Uso de Suelo)

Confirms your lot allows commercial activity. Required before construction license. Cost: $8,000–$20,000 MXN. Timeline: 15–30 days.

Environmental Impact (MIA)

Required for commercial builds >500 m² or in protected zones. SEMA or SEMARNAT review. Cost: $30,000–$80,000 MXN. Timeline: 30–90 days.

Construction License

Issued by municipal Obras Públicas after all other permits approved. Cost: $15,000–$50,000 MXN. Timeline: 15–30 days.

Civil Protection

Fire safety, emergency exits, structural integrity certification. Required for occupancy permit. Cost: $10,000–$25,000 MXN.

CAPA Water Connection

Connection to municipal water and sewer. Required for any commercial building. Cost varies by pipe diameter.

CFE Electrical

Commercial electrical connection (trifásica for >10 kW). Application through CFE. Timeline: 30–60 days for new connection.
