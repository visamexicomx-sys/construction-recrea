How Much Does It Cost to Build an Apartment Building in Playa del Carmen? (2026)

3. Soft costs

  • Design and engineering 5–8%
  • Permits, DRO and impact studies 2–4%
  • Condominium regime and notary 0.5–1%
  • Contingency 5–8%
  • Utility connections priced per project

4. Building in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the second market and the busiest for small and mid-size condo buildings: downtown near Quinta Avenida, Playacar, Centro Maya, the north and the Corasol corridor. Lots are narrow, municipal height limits are strict, and buyers are largely foreign investors who expect rooftop amenities and rental-ready units.

5. Permits and timeline

Land use and density, environmental review, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection in Solidaridad, then the condominium regime. Design and permits 3–6 months, construction 10–18 months for 4–40 units.
