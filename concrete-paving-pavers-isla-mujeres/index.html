Concrete & Pavers on Isla Mujeres: concrete, stamped concrete and pavers

Where trucks turn, water sits or the look matters on Isla Mujeres, concrete and pavers outlast asphalt and can let rain soak into the ground. Low areas in the centre flood in heavy rain; infiltration wells and careful grades are needed, and runoff must not discharge onto beaches.

Isla Mujeres, 2026 references: Hydraulic concrete 15 cm: $1,200–$1,800; Hydraulic concrete 20 cm: $1,500–$2,100; Stamped / decorative concrete: $1,100–$1,800.

Permeable paving and rules on Isla Mujeres

The municipality of Isla Mujeres issues licences; the island has protected areas, reef and turtle rules, and heavy vehicle access is restricted, so works must be planned with the municipality. Permeable pavers, pervious concrete or grass pavers are chosen where these rules and the site make them the right answer.

Bases for concrete and pavers on Isla Mujeres

The island is narrow limestone and sand exposed to sea on both sides; road edges suffer from salt and waves, and bases need good drainage to survive storms. Base thickness, joints and edge restraints are designed for this ground so slabs and pavers stay level.

Plants, materials and access on Isla Mujeres

Materials and equipment cross on the cargo ferry from Cancún; hot mix cannot wait long, so concrete and pavers are often chosen, and the crossing adds cost to every truck.

Traffic and work windows on Isla Mujeres

Visitor traffic is heavy all year with golf carts and mopeds in narrow streets; works are phased in short sections with careful traffic control.
