Hotel Renovation in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen’s hotel market is unlike Cancún’s: hundreds of boutique hotels of 10 to 60 rooms on and around Quinta Avenida, Calle 12 and Calle 38, built fast during the 2000s and 2010s, plus the larger all-inclusives of Playacar and the beachfront north of the centre. Many boutique properties now compete with new openings and with short-term rentals. We renovate them room by room, rooftop by rooftop, while they stay open.

Quick answer: In 2026 a soft refresh costs about $120,000–$300,000 MXN per key and a full room renovation $350,000–$800,000 MXN per key; rooftop pool and bar rebuilds are priced per project.
Playa del Carmen 2026, cost per key: boutique soft refresh $120,000–$300,000 MXN; full room and bathroom renovation $350,000–$800,000 MXN; rooftop pool and bar rebuild $1.5M–$6M MXN per project; Playacar resort repositioning $900,000–$2M MXN per key.

Working in the centre of Playa

Most boutique hotels sit in pedestrian or near-pedestrian streets around Quinta Avenida, where trucks can only enter at set hours and there is no space to store material. We plan early-morning deliveries, small crews and prefabricated elements, protect neighbours and guests from dust and noise, and keep the reception and rooftop working while floors are closed in sequence.

Permits in Solidaridad

Playa del Carmen hotels are in the municipality of Solidaridad. Interior refreshes usually need a notice and Protección Civil review; rooftop structures, extensions and facade changes need a construction licence with DRO, and Quinta Avenida has specific rules on facades, signage and terraces. Playacar adds its own association approvals. We prepare all of them.
