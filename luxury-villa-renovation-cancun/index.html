Luxury Villa and Home Renovation in Cancún

Cancún's best residential addresses are mostly built: Isla Dorada and its canal lots, the lagoon homes of the Hotel Zone, the gated communities of Lagos del Sol, Residencial Cumbres and Villa Magna, and the newer towers and villas of Puerto Cancún and Playa Mujeres. Many houses from the 1990s and 2000s sit on lots that cannot be bought new today. We renovate them to the standard of a new build.

Cancún 2026: cosmetic upgrade $5,000–$9,000 MXN/m²; full interior renovation $12,000–$22,000 MXN/m²; complete repositioning with structural repair, impact glazing and new MEP $22,000–$38,000 MXN/m². Lagoon and canal homes often need seawall or dock repairs on top.

Impact Glazing

Replacement of original windows with hurricane-rated systems sized to Cancún's wind exposure.

What we find in Cancún houses

Cancún homes built before the 2010s were rarely designed for today’s glazing and corrosion standards. On the lagoon and canals we see seawall cracks, dock corrosion and humidity in ground floors; in Hotel Zone residences, spalled balconies and failing windows on the seaward side; in inland communities, undersized electrical services and A/C that cannot cope with August. A survey before purchase puts a price on all of it.

HOA and municipal rules

Lagos del Sol, Residencial Cumbres, Puerto Cancún and Isla Dorada each have their own works regulations: hours, facade changes, materials and deposits. Structural changes and extensions need a Benito Juárez construction licence with DRO; Playa Mujeres files with Isla Mujeres. We handle the HOA approval and the municipal file together.
