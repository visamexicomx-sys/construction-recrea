Demolition in Playa del Carmen: Selective, Safe and Cleaned Up the Same Day

Most renovations start with demolition, and most neighbour complaints and structural surprises start there too. We do selective interior strip-outs, concrete cutting and coring, partial and total demolition of houses and commercial buildings, with structural review before we cut, dust and noise control, and debris removal to authorised sites so nothing ends up in the jungle.

Before we demolish anything

In concrete houses many walls are load-bearing or carry tie-beams and columns that are not on any drawing. Before cutting we review the structure, locate electrical, water and gas lines and decide what needs shoring. For total demolitions we process the municipal demolition licence, notify the HOA in gated communities, and plan the sequence so the site stays stable and neighbours are protected from dust and falling debris.

Clean sites and legal disposal

Illegal dumping of construction debris is common on the coast and fines fall on the owner. We haul to authorised disposal sites, keep the receipts, separate metal and wood for recycling and leave the site swept daily. On renovation jobs debris leaves the same day, so a rental or an occupied condo is not turned into a storage yard.
