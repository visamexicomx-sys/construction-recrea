What a Recrea Bathroom Includes

Full Waterproofing

Cementitious or liquid membrane over the whole shower and floor, flood-tested for 24 hours before any tile goes down.

New Plumbing

PPR or PEX supply lines, proper traps and venting, and pressure-tested pipes behind every wall we close.

Ventilation

Ducted extractors sized for the room, because an open window is not enough in 85% humidity.

Water and fittings for a bathroom in Puerto Aventuras

Water comes through the community network with good coverage, though hardness still calls for softeners and filters in bathrooms and for drip irrigation in gardens. That is why we size softeners, filters and pumps with the bathroom, and choose fittings that tolerate scale.

Humidity, salt and finishes in Puerto Aventuras

Marina-front and beachfront units get salt and humidity all year, which is why rusted railings, peeling paint and tired bathrooms are the most common jobs here. Grout, paint, extraction and metal fittings are specified for these conditions so the bathroom still looks new after several seasons.

Rules, licences and administration in Puerto Aventuras

Licences come from Solidaridad, but the community administration controls access, work hours, crew registration and design changes, and approvals must be requested before work starts.

Materials, crews and access in Puerto Aventuras

Puerto Aventuras is about 25 minutes from our workshops, so materials and crews arrive daily and urgent repairs can be handled the same day.

Rentals and scheduling in Puerto Aventuras

Rentals peak in winter and holidays, so we schedule larger works in the low season and between bookings.
