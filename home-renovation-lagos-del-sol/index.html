Home renovation in Lagos del Sol, Cancún

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Houses in Lagos del Sol are spacious family homes on good ground, many now ten to twenty years old. Renovation here is about updating them for how families live today and getting more out of the lake views and large gardens.

Updating spacious homes

Typical projects open kitchens to living areas and the garden, renew bathrooms, replace windows with larger, better-insulated systems and add a family room or home office.

More from the lake and garden

Lakefront owners add terraces, outdoor kitchens and new pools within the community’s shoreline distances. We design them so drainage never runs into the lake.

Efficiency and systems

With low salt, upgrades last: inverter air conditioning, solar panels, water filtration, automation and LED lighting cut running costs on houses of this size.

Realistic budget and programme in Lagos del Sol, Cancún

A full renovation in Lagos del Sol runs at MXN 7,500 to 15,500 per m² renovated (roughly USD 410 – 845/m²); extensions and new pools are priced as new work.

A typical house takes two to four months. The committee’s approval comes first; extensions and façade changes need the Benito Juárez licence.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Lagos del Sol, Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: municipal only
  • Ground: compact limestone
  • Water table: deep, higher near the lakes
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: gated access off southern Cancún avenues
  • Development regime: gated community with lakes and design rules

A gated community in southern Cancún built around artificial lakes: large lots, design rules and good ground.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a pool to my house in Lagos del Sol?

Usually yes, within the community’s rules and shoreline distances for lakefront lots. We design and file it with the committee first.

Is renovating cheaper than building new in Lagos del Sol?

Usually, if the structure is sound. Good ground and low salt mean most houses here are worth upgrading rather than replacing.
