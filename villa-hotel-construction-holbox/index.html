Villa and hotel construction in Holbox

Boutique hotels and rental villas on a car-free island: design, CONANP and municipal filings, construction and handover ready to operate.

On Holbox the boutique hotel is the economy: small properties, sand streets and guests who come for the quiet. Building one means designing to the Yum Balam rules and to a supply line that crosses the water.

Rooms are counted by the management plan

The Yum Balam management plan and the municipal programme cap height and room density, so the key count is fixed by the lot before the architect sketches anything. We run that number first, then design the common areas, staff spaces and back-of-house around it instead of fighting for keys the file will not grant.

Water and power decide the guest experience

Island water is scarce and expensive, power has a history of cuts, and the island cannot absorb more septic tanks. A Holbox hotel needs its own treatment plant, generous storage, a backup generator sized for cold rooms and pumps, and ideally solar. Guests never see these systems; they notice immediately when one fails.

Build in the low season, open for the high one

Barges, crews and deliveries slow down in the windy months, and the island fills up from December. We plan the structure for the drier months, consolidate barge loads for each phase and leave fit-out and the opening checks for the last weeks, so the hotel opens into the season rather than in the middle of it.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Holbox

For low-density hospitality on Holbox we work in a range of MXN 22,000 to 38,000 per built m² (roughly USD 1,200 – 2,070/m²). The island premium comes from the double crossing and housing the crews, not from the structure; palapa roofs, local timber and chukum finishes sit at the upper end.

A ten to twenty key hotel takes fourteen to twenty months of construction. CONANP and the municipality of Lázaro Cárdenas run in parallel with the design; the sanitary filings and Protección Civil close at the end, on completed works.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Holbox: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Lázaro Cárdenas
  • Environmental regime: CONANP (Yum Balam) + SEMARNAT + municipal
  • Ground: sand bar over limestone
  • Water table: very high, close to the surface
  • Salt exposure: very high

A car-free island in a federal protected area: low heights, limited footprint and every load crossing by barge.

  • Check the lot against the Yum Balam management plan before paying for design; it fixes what can be built.
  • Plan deliveries in consolidated barge loads and keep a site buffer for days when the crossing is closed.

Frequently asked questions

How many rooms can I build on a Holbox lot?

It depends on the lot’s zone under the Yum Balam management plan and the municipal programme, which set height and density. We calculate the permitted key count before design, so the business plan starts from a real number.

Can a hotel on Holbox run on solar power?

Partly, and it is worth it: solar with storage covers lighting, pumps and a share of the air conditioning, with the grid and a generator as backup. Full autonomy is possible for small properties but needs a large battery bank and roof area.

Can you come in with an architectural design already done?

Yes. We review the design, close the engineering, price by line item and execute, coordinating the authoring practice. We also do the full design when a single responsible party is preferred.

What do you hand over at the end?

Completed works with equipment commissioned, as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets, manuals, a maintenance plan and the documentation the operator needs from the first day of selling rooms.
