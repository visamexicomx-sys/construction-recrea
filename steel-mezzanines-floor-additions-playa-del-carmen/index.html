Steel Mezzanines and Rooftop Additions in Playa del Carmen

On Quinta Avenida and the streets around it, every square metre is expensive and footprints are fixed. Many shops and restaurants have double height or a flat roof that could earn money. A steel mezzanine adds floor area inside; a light steel rooftop addition adds a bar, a lounge or an extra room on top. In the west-side warehouses, storage mezzanines double usable space. We check the building first, then build fast from our local workshop.

Playa del Carmen 2026: mezzanine with composite deck $5,200–$10,500 MXN/m²; light storage mezzanine $3,300–$6,200 MXN/m²; steel rooftop or floor addition (shell) $7,800–$13,500 MXN/m².

Fast-built buildings need checking

Much of central Playa was built quickly in the 2000s, often floor by floor, and drawings may not match what was built. Before adding a mezzanine or a rooftop, we inspect, measure and, if needed, open test pits. Many projects end up with new columns and footings that carry the addition independently, or with a light steel structure whose weight the existing building can take.

Permits and land use in Solidaridad

Mezzanines and additions add built area, so they need a Solidaridad construction licence with structural calculations and DRO, within the land-use limits for height and density, which in the centre are strict. Protección Civil reviews exits and fire protection for commercial use. We check feasibility first so you do not design something the zoning will not allow.
