Asphalt Paving in Puerto Morelos: hot-mix paving, overlays and driveways

Hot-mix asphalt in Puerto Morelos only lasts when the mix arrives hot, the base is sound and the thickness is what the contract says. Halfway between Cancún and Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos is easy to supply with hot mix and sascab from both sides, which keeps paving costs close to Cancún levels despite the location factor.

Puerto Morelos, 2026 references: Hot mix on existing base: $330–$520; Overlay on old asphalt: $280–$460; Milling: $70–$140.

When to pave in Puerto Morelos

Resorts operate all year, so internal roads and car parks are paved in sections at night or early morning; along the Ruta de los Cenotes the rainy season can make sascab tracks impassable for trucks. Laying is scheduled with the plant and around these windows, so the mix is compacted at the right temperature.

Preparing the base in Puerto Morelos

Inland the ground is firm limestone with numerous cenotes along the Ruta de los Cenotes, which calls for probing on road alignments; on the coast it is sand and wetland soil that needs thicker, well-drained bases. We check and correct the base for these conditions before any asphalt is laid.

What gets built in Puerto Morelos

Puerto Morelos has resorts with long internal roads and car parks, the Ruta de los Cenotes gated communities with private access roads, and new subdivisions between the highway and the village that need complete street infrastructure.

Permits and authorities in Puerto Morelos

The municipality of Puerto Morelos licenses the works, and mangrove and reef protections restrict anything on the coastal side of the highway; jungle along the Ruta de los Cenotes often needs a land-use change authorisation before clearing.
