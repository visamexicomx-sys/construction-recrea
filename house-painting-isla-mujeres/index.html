House Painting on Isla Mujeres: facades, interiors, metal and wood

We paint houses, condos and hotels on Isla Mujeres with the preparation the climate demands: washing, crack repair, primer and two coats of tropical-grade products. The island is small and fully exposed to the sea, so salt reaches everything: steel rusts quickly, paint chalks and hinges seize, which makes stainless fittings and marine enamels the default specification.

Why paint fails on Isla Mujeres

Isla Mujeres has older family houses in the centre, boutique hotels and a growing number of villas and condos towards the south point, many of them bought by foreigners who renovate rather than build new. We choose elastomeric facades, anti-mould interiors and marine enamels on metal according to this exposure.

Who we paint for on Isla Mujeres

Tourism is busy most of the year, so in rentals we work room by room and keep noise and dust inside agreed hours. Each job is planned for the type of building, from a single villa to a condo block painted by sections.

Water supply on Isla Mujeres

Water is limited and pressure is irregular, so cisterns, rooftop tanks and filters are standard and every new bathroom or garden must be designed to use water sparingly.

Materials, crews and access on Isla Mujeres

Materials and crews cross on the car ferry from Cancún, so we plan full loads, store materials on site and book ferry slots in advance; that crossing is reflected in prices.

Rules, licences and administration on Isla Mujeres

Works on the island are licensed by Isla Mujeres municipality, the beachfront falls under ZOFEMAT, and narrow streets and golf-cart traffic mean deliveries and debris removal must be planned with the town.
