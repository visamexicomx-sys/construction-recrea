Condo Renovation in the Hotel Zone Entrance

The low-rise condos at the entrance are among the cheapest ways into the Hotel Zone; we renovate them fully so they compete with the new towers next door.

A mix of new towers delivered in shell or with developer finishes and 1980s–1990s low-rise condos. Buyers here are often first-time Hotel Zone owners and investors looking for a lower entry price than the beachfront.

The setting: Bay and Bojórquez lagoon

Calm bay water and the Bojórquez lagoon mean less wave energy than the south, but salt air and humidity still attack balconies, railings and A/C units.

Towers, administrations and work hours

Every tower has its own regulations: approval of the work plan, set hours for noisy work, booking of the service lift, protection of common areas, deposits and insurance certificates. We prepare that package before starting, so the administration has no reason to stop the job.

Older towers, salt and two waterfronts

Many Hotel Zone towers are 30 to 40 years old and face the sea on one side and the lagoon on the other. We check risers, the electrical feed and balcony concrete before closing walls, and use stainless, marine-grade hardware and sealed frames that do not rust or stain.

Rules in this sector

New towers apply a fit-out manual with approved drawings, contractor registration and set hours; older condos need administration approval and service-lift booking. Works inside towers need the administration's written approval, registered and insured crews, booked service-lift slots and protection of corridors and lifts.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
