The permit file in Akumal: what actually goes in

There is no single procedure for the Riviera Maya. In Akumal the competent authority is the Tulum municipality, the environmental regime is SEMA + SEMARNAT (federal shoreline, dune, turtle nesting), and that decides which documents enter the file and in what order.

Access from the federal highway runs through narrow internal streets, which pushes up freight and concrete-pumping costs. It pays to size the logistics in the first budget, because mid-build there is no room left to renegotiate it.

The file for Akumal, in order

  1. Land-use certificate (Tulum municipality)
  2. State environmental impact authorisation — SEMA Quintana Roo
  3. Federal Environmental Impact Statement — SEMARNAT
  4. ZOFEMAT concession for the federal maritime-land strip
  5. Construction licence with a registered DRO (responsible building official)
  6. Water and drainage feasibility and contract (CAPA/CONAGUA as applicable)
  7. CFE electrical feasibility and service connection
  8. Notice of completion and certificate of occupancy

What shapes the process in Akumal

The ground is coastal limestone and the water table is high on the front row, which weighs on the soil study that accompanies the licence application. Access is federal highway, narrow internal streets and the development regime is bay homeowner associations.

  • The federal Environmental Impact Statement outranks everything else: until the resolution lands, the municipality will not open a file. Assembling the studies and getting the ruling usually takes 4 to 8 months.
  • The federal maritime-land strip is concessioned and paid for annually; it does not travel with the deed. The survey and the application are filed separately and carry their own response time.
  • Without the state SEMA ruling the town hall will not accept the licence application. It is the first document in the file, not the last.
  • High water table: the foundation sits higher and the pool needs anchoring against uplift.
  • At this distance from the sea 304 stainless pits within a couple of years. Hardware, window systems and anchors are specified in 316 and concrete cover goes above the code minimum.

Realistic timeline in Akumal

With a complete, well-assembled file, count on 6 to 12 months: the federal environmental resolution sets the start; the municipal file runs behind it.
