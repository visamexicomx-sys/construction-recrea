Car Dealership Construction in the Riviera Maya: Showroom, Workshop and Brand Standards

For dealer groups opening or rebuilding a brand in the Riviera Maya. We build to the automaker’s corporate identity manual — glazed double-height showroom, delivery bay, service reception, workshop with lifts and pits, body shop with paint booth and the outdoor display that sells from the avenue — and get it approved by the brand on the first inspection.

The brand manual sets the scope

Every automaker has a corporate identity programme: facade module, colours, floor finishes, lighting levels, signage, furniture and even the delivery ceremony area. We price and build from that manual, coordinate the brand’s reviews and keep a record of every finish so the final audit passes.

Workshop and environment

Service bays need lifts, pits, air, oil and coolant networks, wash bays with oil separators and hazardous waste storage. The paint booth and waste need state environmental authorisations; we file them with the construction licence.
