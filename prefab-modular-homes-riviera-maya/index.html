Prefab Homes in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

Prefab and modular homes are built in modules and assembled on the lot, cutting build time in half and lowering cost — ideal for those looking to enter investment with less capital or quickly have a rental home. But beware: in the Riviera Maya a serious modular home does require a limestone-rock foundation, structural calculation, hurricane anchoring and permits (license, DRO, SEMA in Tulum) — it is not a temporary house. We use quality concrete or steel-frame modules, with finishes that do not look “prefab”. We coordinate fabrication, foundation and permits under one roof.
