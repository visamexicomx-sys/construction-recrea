Building in Mexico as a Foreigner: What to Know

A foreigner can own and build in the Riviera Maya. Within the 50 km coastal strip, ownership is structured through a bank trust (fideicomiso) — a Mexican bank holds title in trust for you, with full rights to use, rent, sell and inherit — or, for multiple projects, through a Mexican corporation. What matters is the right team around you: notary, attorney and a trustworthy builder who works on a fixed-price bilingual contract and gives you remote reports. That way you build from your home country with full control: we manage permits, DRO, construction and quality, and you receive photos, video and video calls every week. Over 18 years building for clients from the US, Canada and Europe.
