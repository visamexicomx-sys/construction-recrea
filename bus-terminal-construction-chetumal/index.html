Bus Terminal, Station & Depot Construction in Chetumal

Tourism, the Maya Train connections and growing suburbs keep bus travel in Chetumal expanding. We build bus terminals, stations and depots for bus lines, transport operators, developers and municipalities: passenger terminals with ticketing and retail, bays and canopies, pavements built for 20-tonne axles, and maintenance yards with workshops, fuelling and wash bays.

Quick answer: Bus Terminal, Station & Depot Construction in Chetumal — 2026 prices: Terminal building, per m²: $15,000–$26,000 MXN; Bus bay with platform, each: $0.6M–$1.3M MXN; Heavy-duty pavement, per m²: $1,000–$2,000 MXN; Platform canopy, per m²: $4,000–$8,000 MXN.
Chetumal 2026: terminal building $15,000–$26,000 MXN per m²; bus bay $0.6M–$1.3M each; heavy-duty pavement $1,000–$2,000 per m²; canopy $4,000–$8,000 per m². A small 8-bay terminal runs about $27.6M–$55.2M; a 1-hectare depot $23M–$50.6M.

What We Deliver

Passenger Terminals

Ticketing, waiting halls, retail, restrooms and luggage.

Bays & Canopies

Saw-tooth or drive-through bays with long-span canopies.

Heavy Pavements

Concrete pavements and drainage for bus axles.

Maintenance Yards

Workshops, inspection pits, fuelling and wash bays.

Electric Buses

Depot charging and power supply for electric fleets.

Safety & Access

Pedestrian separation, accessibility, CCTV.

Operations decide the layout

Bay count, turning radii, dwell times and passenger flows decide the size of a terminal. We start from the operator's timetable and fleet, then design bays, circulation and the terminal around them so buses and people never cross.

Pavements and depots that last

Buses destroy asphalt in a season. We build reinforced concrete pavements at bays and yards, drainage for tropical storms and depots ready for fuel today and electric charging tomorrow.

Building in Chetumal

Chetumal is the state capital and a border city with year-round local demand from government, education, health and trade. Costs run about 8% below Cancún, land is cheaper, and projects are financed more locally.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Othón P. Blanco; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
