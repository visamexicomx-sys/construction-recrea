Construction Permits in Cancun Hotel Zone: What to Know

The Hotel Zone is a narrow strip between the sea and the Nichupté lagoon, with federal frontage on both sides and hotel density fixed by the FONATUR master plan. Municipal land use is read against that plan rather than independently, and no extension is authorised if it contradicts the area originally assigned to the lot.

The file stretches because three authorities coexist: the town hall for the licence, SEMARNAT for coastal-strip environmental impact and ZOFEMAT for the beach concession. Each keeps its own calendar and none waits for the others.

Three authorities over the same lot

Three layers overlap in the Hotel Zone. The municipality of Benito Juárez issues the building licence. The FONATUR planning legacy fixes densities, heights and uses with less room for interpretation than usual. And the federal level appears twice over: ZOFEMAT across the 20 metres from the tide line, which is concessioned rather than bought, and SEMARNAT wherever dune or coastal vegetation is involved. Before designing, ask in writing what applies to that exact lot: the real usable depth can shrink substantially, and the whole brief with it.

What governs the build beyond the permit

Once the licence is in hand, operational constraints particular to this strip remain. Turtle nesting season, May to November, regulates lighting on the seaward side — warm, shielded, directed downward — and in some cases limits night-time works. The single access along the boulevard forces deliveries into defined windows against heavy tourist traffic. And the high water table means any excavation needs continuous pumping, which belongs in the budget as a line item rather than as a contingency. None of these three are applied for, but they shape the calendar as much as the file does.

The permit file in Zona Hotelera, Cancún: what actually goes in

There is no single procedure for the Riviera Maya. In Zona Hotelera, Cancún the competent authority is the Benito Juárez municipality, the environmental regime is SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession), and that decides which documents enter the file and in what order.

The water table is very high and the ground is sand and dune over limestone. In practice that rules out basements, forces a compacted fill platform and makes the soil study the first line of the budget rather than the last.

  1. Land-use certificate (Benito Juárez municipality)
  2. Federal Environmental Impact Statement — SEMARNAT
  3. ZOFEMAT concession for the federal maritime-land strip

  5. Water and drainage feasibility and contract (CAPA/CONAGUA as applicable)
  6. CFE electrical feasibility and service connection

The document that decides it in the Hotel Zone

SEMARNAT's ruling on the environmental impact statement. On a sand strip between the sea and the Nichupté lagoon, with federal frontage on both sides, that ruling defines building footprint, setbacks, dune management and operating conditions. The municipal licence is issued afterwards and largely repeats what the federal one already fixed. Add the ZOFEMAT beach concession, paid annually and renewed, and you have the full picture: here the environmental file is not one more requirement, it is the project.

In Cancún it is not a DRO, it is a Perito Responsable de Obra

Same role, different name, and it is worth knowing before you call the counter. The Solidaridad code, which governs Playa del Carmen, speaks of a Director Responsable de Obra (DRO). The Benito Juárez municipality, which is Cancún, registers and publishes its professionals as Perito Responsable de Obra (P.R.O.), alongside the Corresponsable de Estructura and Corresponsable de Instalaciones roles for projects that require them by size or use.

In practice the function is identical: they sign the project, answer legally for code compliance and keep the site logbook. What changes is the register: a professional listed in Solidaridad is not automatically authorised in Benito Juárez, and vice versa. That is why a file assembled in Playa del Carmen cannot be filed as-is in Cancún.

We work with a professional listed on the register that matches the municipality, and bring in the co-responsible specialists when the project calls for them. In a Cancún filing, that registration is the first thing the town hall checks.
