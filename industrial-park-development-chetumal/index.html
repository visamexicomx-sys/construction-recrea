Industrial & Logistics Park Development in Chetumal

Demand for warehouses in Chetumal comes from distribution for hotels and supermarkets, airport logistics, building materials and e-commerce, and good stock is scarce. We build industrial and logistics parks for developers and investors: master plan, roads for trailers, water, sewer and treatment, the power substation that decides who can rent, and warehouses for lease built in phases as tenants sign.

Quick answer: Industrial & Logistics Park Development in Chetumal — 2026 prices: Power substation and medium-voltage network: $7.4M–$27.6M MXN; Warehouse shell for lease, per m²: $6,000–$11,000 MXN; Wastewater treatment plant for the park: $2.8M–$11M MXN; 10-hectare park, infrastructure: $41.4M–$101.2M MXN.
Chetumal 2026: infrastructure $0–$1,000 MXN per m² of saleable lot; power substation and MV network $7.4M–$27.6M; warehouse shell $6,000–$11,000 per m². A 10-hectare park runs about $41.4M–$101.2M of infrastructure; a first phase of 4 warehouses $59.8M–$110.4M.

What We Deliver

Water & Treatment

Heavy Roads

Warehouses for Lease

Security

Perimeter, gatehouse, access control and CCTV.

Power decides the tenants

Cold storage, light manufacturing and data rooms need capacity the local grid often does not have. A park with its own substation and medium-voltage network can rent to tenants others turn away, so we size power for the target tenant mix and coordinate the CFE process from the start.

Phased for cash flow

Infrastructure is built in phases along the main road; warehouses follow signed leases or a speculative first phase. Standard shells — clear height, docks, slab capacity — lease faster and finance easier.

Building in Chetumal

Chetumal is the state capital and a border city with year-round local demand from government, education, health and trade. Costs run about 8% below Cancún, land is cheaper, and projects are financed more locally.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Othón P. Blanco; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
