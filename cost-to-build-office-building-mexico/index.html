How Much Does It Cost to Build an Office Building in Mexico? Riviera Maya 2026

Short answer: in 2026 an office building in the Riviera Maya costs about $11,000–$18,000 MXN/m² for core and shell in 2–4 storeys, $16,000–$26,000 with lifts and structured parking, plus $6,000–$25,000/m² of fit-out if you deliver fitted suites, plus parking. Below: the breakdown and examples by city.

Quick answer: Short answer: in 2026 an office building in the Riviera Maya costs about $11,000–$18,000 MXN/m² for core and shell in 2–4 storeys, $16,000–$26,000 with lifts and structured parking, plus $6,000–$25,000/m² of fit-out if you deliver fitted suites, plus parking.
Example: a 1,000 m² three-storey building in Cancún — shell $11M–$18M, basic fit-out of half the floors $3M–$6M, 30 surface spaces $0.75M–$2.1M, generator $0.3M–$0.9M — about $15M–$27M MXN before land and VAT.

What Goes Into the Budget

Core & Shell

$11,000–$18,000/m² low-rise; $16,000–$26,000 mid-rise.

Fit-Out

$6,000–$12,000 basic; $12,000–$25,000 corporate.

Parking

$25,000–$70,000 surface; $180,000–$400,000 structured.

Backup Power

$300,000–$900,000 for common areas and lifts.

Lifts

Needed above two storeys and for accessibility.

Design & Permits

About 6–10% of construction.

What moves the number

  • Parking: the ratio sets lettable area; structured parking multiplies cost per space.
  • Height: lifts, fire protection and structure jump above three or four storeys.
  • Shell or fitted: fitting out suites adds cost but leases faster to small tenants.
  • City: Playa about 4% above Cancún, Tulum 13%, Chetumal 8% below.

Not included

Land, VAT, financing, leasing and furniture.
