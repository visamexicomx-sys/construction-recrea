Floors for the homes on Isla Mujeres

The island is small and fully exposed to the sea, so salt reaches everything: steel rusts quickly, paint chalks and hinges seize, which makes stainless fittings and marine enamels the default specification. Formats and materials are matched to how the property is used, from family homes to high-turnover rentals.

Heat, humidity and floors on Isla Mujeres

Materials and crews cross on the car ferry from Cancún, so we plan full loads, store materials on site and book ferry slots in advance; that crossing is reflected in prices. Adhesives, movement joints and outdoor finishes are chosen for these conditions so floors stay flat and bonded.

Rentals and scheduling on Isla Mujeres

Tourism is busy most of the year, so in rentals we work room by room and keep noise and dust inside agreed hours.

Rules, licences and administration on Isla Mujeres

Works on the island are licensed by Isla Mujeres municipality, the beachfront falls under ZOFEMAT, and narrow streets and golf-cart traffic mean deliveries and debris removal must be planned with the town.

Water supply on Isla Mujeres

Water is limited and pressure is irregular, so cisterns, rooftop tanks and filters are standard and every new bathroom or garden must be designed to use water sparingly.
