Industrial warehouse construction in Cozumel

Steel frame, industrial slab and the full permit file with a single contractor.

On an island a warehouse is not a logistics luxury: it is the buffer that separates operations from the ferry calendar. Whoever stores well on Cozumel does not depend on the boat arriving every day.

Design for the ferry cycle, not for daily turnover

Island inventory is bought in large batches and consumed slowly. That changes the building: more storage metres per metre of dock, clear height used with racking, and a consolidation and goods-in area sized for deliveries concentrated into a few days a month. A warehouse copied from the mainland ends up with surplus docks and not enough shelving.

Cold: the area where the island allows no improvisation

Food, beverage and hotel supply demand chilled and frozen rooms with real electrical back-up. With no fast transfer, a compressor failure without a spare on the island is lost product. We specify equipment with spares available on the corridor, an emergency generator with automatic transfer and thermal insulation that is calculated, not picked from a catalogue.

Hurricane and salt on a steel envelope

A steel building on an exposed island needs serious wind calculation: roof anchoring, sheet fixing, doors and shutters with verified details. And it needs a defence against salt: heavier galvanising or coating, 316 stainless fittings and six-monthly maintenance. Corrosion on an island gives no warning: it appears at the fixings and at the ridge, where nobody looks.

Budget and programme for a warehouse in Cozumel

Steel-frame warehouse with an industrial slab: MXN 10,000 to 18,000 per m², island premium of eight to twelve per cent included. Cold rooms and reinforced corrosion protection push to the top; a simple dry store stays at the bottom.

A 600–1,500 m² warehouse takes seven to eleven months. The range is wider than on the mainland because the steel frame, the sheeting and the equipment all cross by ferry and are planned in batches. The municipal file runs two to four months and includes Protección Civil; the CFE load increase should be started early if there is refrigeration.

Scope: from the soil study to a warehouse in operation

We cover the soil mechanics study, architectural and structural design, an industrial slab calculated against the real racking layout, roof and envelope, electrical installation with back-up, plumbing, fire protection, offices, manoeuvring yard and site works. We file land use, licence and Protección Civil sign-off with the responsible engineer, and manage the load increase with CFE.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, structural and slab calculation records, and equipment manuals.

What actually decides the cost of a warehouse

Not the square metres: the slab loading, the clear structural span, the usable height and the yard. A larger span saves columns and improves operations but makes the structure more expensive; a badly sized slab forces demolition with racking installed. So we start from the operating layout — racking, forklifts, truck flow — and size from there, not the other way round.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. We work with operators and investors who do not live in the area and need to follow the works remotely.

The ground in Cozumel: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Cozumel
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT, marine park
  • Ground: island limestone
  • Water table: high on the coast
  • Salt exposure: severe

Every material arrives by ferry — logistics, not design, sets the schedule and the overrun.

  • Federal SEMARNAT filing: the environmental resolution is the project’s critical path. Budget 4–8 months before the first excavation.
  • ZOFEMAT frontage: the federal strip is not yours even if you buy the lot — it is concessioned separately and paid for annually.
  • High water table: the foundation sits higher and the pool needs anchoring against uplift.
  • Severe salt exposure: 316 stainless throughout the hardware, concrete cover above the code minimum and six-monthly maintenance. 304 pits within two years at this distance from the sea.
  • Every material crosses by ferry: logistics owns the schedule. Consolidated purchasing and on-site storage stop being optional.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to store on the island or bring everything from Playa del Carmen?

It depends on volume and on your tolerance for running out. For hotel or restaurant operations, island storage pays for itself the first weekend the ferry does not run normally. For small volumes, a mainland warehouse is still cheaper.

How much more does the steel frame cost because it is an island?

Eight to twelve per cent, almost all freight and handling. It is contained by buying the frame in a single shipment, with erection scheduled, and by closing the list of fittings and accessories before shipping. The expensive part is not the steel: it is the extra crossings for missing pieces.

What structural span is best?

The one that allows the operation with the fewest columns within budget. In a warehouse with selective racking, spans of twenty to thirty metres are usually the balance point; beyond that, steel grows faster than the operational benefit. It is decided with the layout in hand, not from a catalogue.

Do you include the manoeuvring yard and site works?

Yes, and we budget them as their own line items because they weigh. Pavement with a thickness for tractor-trailers, storm drainage, lighting, a gatehouse, fencing and access are real costs that sometimes approach thirty per cent of the total and are forgotten in quick quotations.
