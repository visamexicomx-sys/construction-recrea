Daycare, Nursery & Kindergarten Construction in Puerto Morelos

Young families moving to Puerto Morelos need places for their children, and daycare and kindergarten places are short. We build and convert daycares, nurseries and kindergartens for owners, franchises, schools and employers: child-safe rooms and finishes, nap rooms, kitchens and changing areas, shaded playgrounds and the Civil Protection and IMSS requirements that decide whether you can open.

Licences shape the design

Daycares in Mexico are among the most inspected buildings: Civil Protection, health and, for IMSS-subrogated centres, IMSS standards on area per child, exits, kitchens and materials. We design to those rules from the start so the licence is not the last-minute surprise.

Conversion or new build

Many daycares start in a house. We check land use, structure and the number of children the space will allow, then convert with child-safe finishes, exits and a shaded play area — or build new when the numbers work better.

Building in Puerto Morelos

Puerto Morelos is low-rise by design: strict height and density, a protected reef and a careful environmental review. Buyers want quiet, small, well-built projects; costs run about 9% above Cancún.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Puerto Morelos; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
