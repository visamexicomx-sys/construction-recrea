Hotel Construction in Puerto Aventuras: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Puerto Aventuras is a marina community with golf, beaches and a mix of small hotels, condo-hotels and a few resorts. Hotels here serve sailors, divers and families who want a quieter base between Playa del Carmen and Tulum.

Puerto Aventuras 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $10,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $13,000–$19,000; boutique and 4-star $19,000–$27,000; 5-star and luxury $27,000–$44,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

Hotel Classes We Build

Boutique & 4★

Design-led small hotels with rooftop pools, restaurants and a strong sense of place.

Luxury & 5★

Large suites, spa, fine dining and the engineering behind seamless service.

Hotels around the marina

Lots around the marina and the golf course suit boutique and condo-hotels with restaurants at street level. Architecture follows the community’s style guide, which the association reviews.

Condo-hotel models

Many projects combine owned units with hotel operation. We design layouts, back-of-house and services so the building works as a hotel even with private owners.

Solidaridad and the association

Licences come from Solidaridad; the community association approves design, works hours and deliveries. We coordinate both and keep the marina operating during construction.
