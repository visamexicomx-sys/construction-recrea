Interior Design in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

In the Riviera Maya, interior design has two goals: a tropical eco-chic style (chukum, wood, earth tones, natural light) and, increasingly, maximizing a rental property’s ROI — good design photographs better, raises Airbnb occupancy and resale value. The warm, humid climate demands resistant materials and furniture. The big advantage of working with a builder who also designs interiors is that the design is executed with the build — kitchen, carpentry, lighting, finishes — without the classic designer/builder conflict. We deliver the space ready to live in or rent.
