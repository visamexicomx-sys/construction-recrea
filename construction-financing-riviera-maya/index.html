How to Finance Your Build in the Riviera Maya

The most common worry when building is how to pay for it. The good news: you don't pay all at once. A serious builder works with progress payments — a low deposit (max 15%) and the rest released against verified milestones (foundation, structure, systems, finishes). That keeps your money always tied to real work. For those who need credit, construction-loan options exist with banks and, in some cases, Infonavit; we guide you by situation. Foreigners build via a bank trust or company and usually pay by wire or escrow. All with a fixed-price contract and clear schedule — you know exactly what you pay and when.
