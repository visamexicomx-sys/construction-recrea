Penthouse Fit-Out in the south Hotel Zone

The south Hotel Zone has the most expensive towers and penthouses; we fit them out to luxury standards with terraces and rooftop plunge pools.

The newest and highest-priced residential stock of the Hotel Zone: luxury towers, penthouses and branded residences, alongside older beachfront condos; owners often live abroad and buy for second home or rental.

The setting: Open Caribbean beach and Nichupté lagoon

This is the most exposed stretch: high-energy surf, beach erosion, sargassum and full hurricane loads. Toward Punta Nizuc the coast borders the marine national park, so works near the shore face environmental limits.

Structure first on the roof

Pools, jacuzzis, planters and pergolas add heavy loads and wind forces; we get a structural review and the building's approval before design, so the terrace you see in the render is the one that can be built.

Finishes for sun, salt and wind

Outdoor stone, stainless, powder-coated aluminium and marine-grade fabrics are chosen so the terrace still looks new after several hurricane seasons.

Rules in this sector

Luxury towers apply strict fit-out manuals, insurance and deposits; branded residences may limit what owners change; beach works need ZOFEMAT and federal environmental review. Penthouse works touch roofs and façades, so they need the administration's approval, sometimes the original architect's sign-off, structural calculations and craning or lifting plans.

Managed for owners who live abroad

You get one English-speaking project manager and one WhatsApp group, a weekly photo and video report, a live budget and payments by milestone after each stage is checked; most owners only fly in for handover.
