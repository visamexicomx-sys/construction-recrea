Villa and hotel construction in Playa Magna

Rental villas, short-stay residences and small guest-houses in an established Cancún community: rulebook, municipal licence, construction and a handover ready to operate.

Playa Magna is a residential community, so its hospitality product is not a hotel but the rental villa or short-stay residence for families and business travellers who want a house near the airport and the city, not a resort room.

Check the community’s rental rules

Established communities regulate short-term rental, minimum stays and parking. We read Playa Magna’s rulebook and confirm what is allowed before designing a villa or small residence for rental, so the product can operate legally from day one.

Designed for families and longer stays

Guests here stay longer and cook at home. We design full kitchens, laundry, workspaces, storage and outdoor living around a private pool, and plan bedrooms that suit families travelling together.

Fast, predictable construction

Good ground, services at the street and a direct municipal process make the build efficient. We use durable finishes that survive guests and keep running costs low, which decide the rental return.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Playa Magna

For a rental villa or short-stay residence in Playa Magna we work in a range of MXN 15,000 to 27,000 per built m² (roughly USD 815 – 1,470/m²). Moderate salt and good ground keep the specification efficient; premium finishes and larger pools push towards the top.

A rental villa or four to eight unit residence takes ten to fourteen months. The community approval and the Benito Juárez licence run in parallel.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Playa Magna: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: municipal only
  • Ground: limestone
  • Water table: deep
  • Salt exposure: moderate
  • Access: urban street
  • Development regime: subdivision with a rulebook

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent my villa in Playa Magna short-term?

It depends on the community’s current rules on rental and minimum stays. We check them before design.

What makes a rental villa in Cancún successful?

Location close to the airport and city, a full kitchen and laundry, workspaces, a private pool and low running costs. We design for all of them.
