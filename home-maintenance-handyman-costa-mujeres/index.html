We maintain houses, condos and villas in Costa Mujeres with one team for repairs, scheduled preventive visits and a photo report after each one. Most units are rented short-term, so we schedule work between bookings, protect common areas and finish noisy tasks before guests check in.

What wears out first in Costa Mujeres

Exposure here is direct: open sea, trade winds and spray reach the front rows of every building, so metal, paint and outdoor finishes corrode faster than in Cancún city and need marine-grade specifications. Our checklist puts these problems first: rust, sealant, roof membrane, AC drains and exterior finishes.

Water systems to keep running in Costa Mujeres

Water comes through the Cancún network with frequent low pressure in new phases, so most homes rely on cisterns, pressure pumps and filters, and gardens need drip irrigation planned around stored water. Pumps, cisterns, filters and softeners are checked on every visit, because when they fail the property stops working.

Homes and owners in Costa Mujeres

Costa Mujeres is mostly new: beachfront condos, all-inclusive resorts and gated villa projects north of Cancún, where owners usually want to upgrade developer finishes, add outdoor living and set up a maintenance plan before the first rental season.

Rules, licences and administration in Costa Mujeres

Building permits come from Isla Mujeres municipality on the continental side, and almost every development has an HOA with design rules, approved work hours and a requirement to register crews and vehicles at the gate.

Materials, crews and access in Costa Mujeres

The zone is about 90 minutes from our workshops and 30 minutes from Cancún suppliers; we group deliveries and keep crews on site for full days so travel does not eat into the job.
