Hotel Construction in Costa Mujeres: Every Class, From Hostel to 5-Star

Costa Mujeres is where the Caribbean’s newest all-inclusive and 5-star resorts are opening, on long beaches north of Cancún. It belongs to the municipality of Isla Mujeres, not Benito Juárez, and its infrastructure is still being built, so every resort brings its own utilities.

Costa Mujeres 2026: economy hotels and hostels from $11,000 MXN/m²; 3-star city hotels $14,000–$20,000; boutique and 4-star $20,000–$29,000; 5-star and luxury $29,000–$48,000 MXN/m². Land, furniture (FF&E) and permit fees are separate.

Hotel Classes We Build

All-Inclusive Resorts

Multi-building resorts with pools, restaurants, theatres and back-of-house logistics.

Luxury & 5★

Large suites, spa, fine dining and the engineering behind seamless service.

Large resorts, long timelines

Projects here are big: hundreds of keys, multiple buildings, theatres, water parks and beach clubs. We organise them in phases with early works, utilities and model rooms so the operator can test and approve before rollout.

Utilities you build yourself

Water, wastewater treatment, power substations and access roads often have to be built by the project. We design them with the master plan, including desalination or RO where wells are brackish.

Turtles and dunes

Costa Mujeres beaches are nesting grounds; lighting, setbacks and dune protection are part of the environmental conditions. We design facades and exterior lighting to comply from the start.
