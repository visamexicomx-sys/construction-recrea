Luxury villas in Riviera Cancún

Design, licensing and construction of high-end villas, with remote control for the owner.

Riviera Cancún is the corridor between the airport and Puerto Morelos, with low-density hotels and residential developments on a narrow strip between mangrove to the west and dune to the east. A luxury villa here sits inside a private development, close to the airport and on a beach that is wider than most of the coast.

A strip narrowed from both sides

Mangrove behind and dune in front narrow the buildable strip, and each carries its own federal protection. We map both edges on a topographic survey and design within the band between them, keeping the house off the mangrove and behind the dune setback.

Inside a private development

Access is through the development’s entrance off highway 307, with its own rules on design, construction hours and contractors. We work to those rules and file with the development and Benito Juárez in parallel.

Airport on one side, Caribbean on the other

Being fifteen minutes from the airport makes the villa easy to use and to rent. Salt is severe and the water table very high, so we specify 316 stainless, coastal glazing, raised floors and pools anchored against uplift.

Riviera Cancún sits with the front-row coast: severe salt, a very high water table, federal mangrove and dune rules and development standards. Proximity to the airport keeps logistics simple.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Riviera Cancún: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Benito Juárez
  • Environmental regime: SEMARNAT + ZOFEMAT (federal shoreline concession)
  • Ground: sand over limestone
  • Water table: very high
  • Salt exposure: severe
  • Access: Highway 307 and private development entrances
  • Development regime: low-density hotel and residential

Frequently asked questions

Why is the buildable area in Riviera Cancún so narrow?

Because mangrove to the west and dune to the east are both protected. The buildable band lies between them, and we map it before design.

Is a villa in Riviera Cancún convenient for travellers?

Yes: it is about fifteen minutes from Cancún airport, which makes it easy to use and attractive for rental.
