Rainwater Harvesting and Greywater Reuse in Playa del Carmen

Rainwater in Playa del Carmen is soft, free and abundant from June to November, while the network delivers hard water and, at times, low pressure. That makes rooftop capture especially attractive here: rainwater does not scale heaters and fittings, and it fills a cistern quickly in the rainy season. For Playacar and Corasol villas, boutique hotels in the centre and condos with gardens and pools, adding greywater recycling cuts bills further.

Playa del Carmen 2026: rainwater system for a villa (10–40 m³ cistern, filtration, pump) $80,000–$450,000 MXN; greywater recycling for a villa $90,000–$400,000 MXN; combined system for a boutique hotel or condo $1.2M–$5M MXN.

Soft water from the sky

Playa’s network water is hard; rainwater is naturally soft. Using it for laundry, irrigation, pool top-up and, after RO and UV, even drinking reduces scale and softener salt. We add first-flush diverters so the first dirty rain is discarded, and filters sized for the leaves and dust of the dry season.

Boutique hotels and condos

In a 20–40 room hotel, greywater from showers can supply every toilet in the building. It needs a separate riser and a small treatment room, which is easiest during a renovation. For condos, shared rain cisterns under parking or gardens can serve common-area irrigation and pools.
