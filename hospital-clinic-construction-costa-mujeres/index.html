Clinic and medical facility construction in Costa Mujeres

Design, construction and services with the health file resolved before opening.

Costa Mujeres has tens of thousands of resort guests and staff, new residential developments and no hospital of its own; Cancún’s hospitals are twenty to forty minutes away. The gap is first-level and urgent care close to where people are.

First-level care near the resorts

Guests with minor emergencies, resort employees and new residents need consultations, urgent care, a laboratory sample point and a pharmacy without driving into Cancún. We plan clinics with direct road access, parking and an ambulance bay, ideally at a junction on the coast road.

Designed to work with Cancún’s hospitals

Serious cases transfer to Cancún, so the clinic needs a stabilisation room, an ambulance route and clear clinical pathways. That keeps the building smaller and the investment focused on what it does well: speed, diagnosis and comfort.

Licences from Isla Mujeres

Clinics here are licensed by the municipality of Isla Mujeres on its continental side, with COFEPRIS for the sanitary licence; inside a development, the developer’s commercial rules also apply. The coastal specification — salt-resistant equipment and protected openings — is the same as for any building on this dune.

Budget and programme for a clinic in Costa Mujeres

Clinic fit-out: MXN 20,000 to 36,000 per m². Freight is low and specialist installers come from Cancún; marine exposure and the finish standards of the developments keep the figure in the coastal range.

A 150–300 m² clinic takes five to eight months. The municipal file runs six to ten weeks, and the sanitary licence closes at the end, on completed works with equipment installed.

Scope: from concept to the sanitary licence

We cover concept and executive design with full engineering, municipal filing with the responsible engineer, civil works, electrical with back-up, plumbing, air conditioning and extraction, medical gases where applicable, sanitary finishes, fixed furniture and signage. We accompany the health file through to inspection, coordinating the facility’s health officer.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates; every change is priced and signed before execution and payments go against verified progress. We hand over as-built drawings, calculation records, datasheets and equipment manuals.

Why the order of filings decides the opening date

The municipal licence authorises building; the sanitary licence or notice authorises operating, and it is resolved on completed works with equipment installed and running. So the opening date is set when the health file closes, not when the construction contract is signed. We plan both in parallel so the second does not begin when the first ends.

Weekly reports with dated photographs and a fortnightly video call from site, in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian or Chinese. Many of our clients are doctors or investors who do not live in the area and need to follow the works remotely.

The ground in Costa Mujeres: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Isla Mujeres (continental zone)
  • Environmental regime: development guidelines + municipal + ZOFEMAT/NOM-022 where applicable
  • Ground: dune sand over limestone
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A fast-growing resort coast north of Cancún: development rules on top of municipal ones, and full marine exposure.

  • Ask the developer for the architectural guidelines and the list of approved contractors before you commission a design.
  • Allow for the development’s review committee in the programme and file it in parallel with the municipal licence.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a hospital in Costa Mujeres?

Not yet; hospitals are in Cancún, twenty to forty minutes away. That is why a well-located urgent-care and first-level clinic has strong demand from guests, staff and residents.

Which authorities license a clinic in Costa Mujeres?

The municipality of Isla Mujeres for land use and construction, and COFEPRIS for the sanitary licence. Inside a development, its commercial guidelines also apply.

Do you accompany the COFEPRIS process?

We handle the technical file: drawings, calculation records, specifications for finishes and services, medical gases and biological waste handling, and we respond to inspection observations. The facility’s health officer remains the signatory, and we work coordinated with that role from concept stage.

Can a clinic be fitted out in a unit that is already leased?

Yes, and it is the most common case. What has to be verified before signing the lease is clear height, electrical capacity, the possibility of extraction, the position of drainage stacks and space for the waste store. A two-hour review before signing avoids months of trouble afterwards.
