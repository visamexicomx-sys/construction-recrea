Generators in the Riviera Maya: What to Know

In the Riviera Maya, power outages and hurricane season (June–November) make a backup generator a necessity, not a luxury — especially for vacation-rental homes, hotels and anyone relying on A/C and the pool pump. The key is a correct load calculation: an undersized generator won’t start your equipment, an oversized one wastes fuel. A good system includes an automatic transfer switch (self-starting within seconds), the right tank (LP/natural gas or diesel) and code-compliant installation with grounding. We coordinate the generator with the electrical installation and, if you wish, with solar panels — all under one roof, in any city of the corridor.
