Villa and hotel construction in Tulum Centro

Boutique hotels, hostels and aparthotels in downtown Tulum: urban licence, party walls, rooftop amenities and a handover ready to operate.

Downtown Tulum has the guests who want everything on foot — restaurants, nightlife, the bus to the ruins and the beach — and the hotels that serve them are small: boutique hotels, design hostels and aparthotels on urban lots between party walls.

A hotel on an urban lot

Lots are narrow and deep, with neighbours on both sides. We organise rooms around a courtyard or light well, put the pool on the roof or in the courtyard, and design the street front for arrival, a café or reception that brings life to the façade.

Noise is the main complaint

Guests in the centre complain about noise more than anything else. We specify acoustic glazing to the street, insulated party walls, quiet air-conditioning units placed on the roof and room layouts that keep bedrooms away from bars and the street.

Construction in the middle of town

Deliveries, pumping and scaffolding happen on busy streets. We coordinate them with the municipality and neighbours, protect adjoining buildings and work within permitted hours.

Budget and programme for a boutique hotel in Tulum Centro

For a boutique hotel, hostel or aparthotel in Tulum Centro we work in a range of MXN 15,000 to 28,000 per built m² (roughly USD 815 – 1,525/m²). Party-wall works, acoustic glazing and rooftop pools push towards the top; low salt keeps the specification lean.

A twelve to forty bed or key property takes eleven to fifteen months. SEMA and the municipal licence come first; sanitary filings and Protección Civil close on completed works.

How we run a villa at this level

A single contract covers concept, executive design and engineering, filings with the responsible engineer, construction with our own crews, special installations, pool, landscaping and handover with as-built drawings, datasheets and manuals. We coordinate the client’s architect or interior designer where one already exists; we do not insist on also being the authors of the design.

The budget comes out as line items with quantities and unit rates, and every change is priced and signed before it is executed. Payments go against verified progress on site, never against calendar dates.

Remote control and a handover ready to operate

Most of our clients are outside Mexico during construction: a weekly report with dated photographs, a fortnightly video call from site and a live budget available at any time. We work in Spanish, English, German, French, Russian and Chinese.

We hand over an operable house: equipment commissioned, a six-monthly maintenance plan, an inventory of critical spares and, if the property will be rented, the documentation the operator needs from day one. In the restricted zone a bank trust is the usual vehicle, and we coordinate notary and bank in parallel with the works.

The ground in Tulum Centro: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Tulum
  • Environmental regime: SEMA (Quintana Roo state environmental authority)
  • Ground: karst
  • Water table: mid depth
  • Salt exposure: low
  • Access: urban streets
  • Development regime: municipal density

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest design issue for a hotel in central Tulum?

Noise. Acoustic glazing to the street, insulated party walls and bedrooms placed away from noisy areas make the difference in guest reviews.

Can I put the pool on the roof?

Yes, and on an urban lot it is often the best place. The structure, waterproofing and drainage are designed for it from the start.
