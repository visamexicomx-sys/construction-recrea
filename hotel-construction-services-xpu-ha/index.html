Hotel Services in Xpu-Há: Everything a Top-Tier Hotel Needs

New builds, renovations, brand conversions, MEP, kitchens, ballrooms, facades, fire safety, staff housing, energy and maintenance for hotels in Xpu-Há, under one contract. Xpu-Há is one of the most valuable beaches of the coast: large all-inclusive complexes, new ultra-luxury resorts and a handful of boutique beach clubs between Puerto Aventuras and Akumal.

Xpu-Ha, 2026 references: New hotel construction: $11,400–$50,800 / m²; Guestroom renovation / PIP: $190k–$1.3M / key; MEP & energy retrofit: $1.9M–$31.8M / project.

Permits and rules in Xpu-Há

Beachfront projects here need federal-zone concessions, environmental impact authorisations and municipal licences, and resort expansions are reviewed closely because of nearby cenotes and reef. We map these approvals at the start of every hotel project, so design, procurement and works move in parallel.

When to build in Xpu-Há

Large resorts here are sold year-round; renovation is planned building by building with sales, and luxury properties expect noise-free guest zones at all times. We build the phasing plan around that calendar, so revenue keeps coming in while the works advance.

Water, power and staff in Xpu-Há

Resorts operate their own water and wastewater plants and long internal networks; energy bills are high, which makes MEP upgrades and solar attractive.
