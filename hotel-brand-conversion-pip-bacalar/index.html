Brand Conversion & PIP in Bacalar: Brand Standards, Hotel Open

Joining an international brand or changing flag in Bacalar means a Property Improvement Plan with fixed deadlines; we price it item by item and deliver it while the hotel sells. Bacalar is the lagoon of seven colours: boutique and eco hotels on the shore, glamping and cabins, and a fast-growing number of small hotels near the Tren Maya station.

What a PIP Usually Includes

Accessibility

Accessible rooms and routes as required by the brand and NOM standards.

Lobby & Public Areas

Arrival, lobby bar, restaurants, corridors and signage to the new brand.

Phasing a PIP in Bacalar

Occupancy peaks in winter and holidays; most lagoon hotels are small, so works are planned in a few closed weeks during the low season. We schedule room blocks and public areas around this pattern and agree each phase with the brand and revenue manager.

Approvals for a conversion in Bacalar

Bacalar municipality and federal authorities protect the lagoon and its stromatolites closely; wastewater, shoreline works and docks are where permits are hardest. Life-safety and accessibility items in the PIP are coordinated with these authorities from the first survey.

Climate and exposure in Bacalar

Fresh, shallow lagoon water and karst soil make wastewater the main risk; humidity, termites and storms affect wood and thatch structures common in local hotels.
