Home renovation in Mahahual

Survey, permits and renovation with one contractor, for owners who live outside Mexico.

Many of Mahahual’s houses and small hotels were built or rebuilt after Hurricane Dean in 2007, often quickly and on a tight budget. Fifteen years of salt later, renovation here means fixing that first rebuild, adding off-grid systems and preparing for the next storm.

Fixing the post-Dean rebuild

After Dean, much was rebuilt in a hurry. We often find thin roof slabs, light fixings, ordinary steel and no real waterproofing. We survey, reinforce or replace what is weak and bring the envelope to a specification that can face open sea for decades.

Adding off-grid systems

Renovation is the moment to add what the house always needed: solar with storage, a larger cistern, a treatment plant and efficient air conditioning. We size them for real use and integrate them so they survive salt and storms.

Salt on a narrow strip

Between reef and mangrove the salt reaches everything. We replace corroded windows, railings and condensers with marine-grade products, repair spalling concrete and design shutters that can be closed quickly when a hurricane is forecast.

Realistic budget and programme in Mahahual

A full renovation in Mahahual runs at MXN 8,500 to 17,000 per m² renovated (roughly USD 465 – 925/m²). Structural reinforcement and off-grid systems push towards the top; freight from Chetumal keeps materials reasonable.

A typical house or small hotel takes three to six months, scheduled outside the peak of the hurricane season where possible. Structural changes need the Othón P. Blanco municipal licence.

What we renovate: from a survey to a finished house

Every renovation starts with a survey of what is there: structure, rebar corrosion, slabs, waterproofing, installations and windows. We report what must be fixed, what should be upgraded and what can stay, with a line-item budget for each, before anything is demolished.

We then handle the whole job: permits or condominium approvals, demolition, structural repairs, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, pools and finishes. One contract, one site manager, and a programme that tells you when each room is back in use.

Renovating while you are abroad

Most of our renovation clients are not in Mexico during the works. You receive a weekly report with dated photographs, a video call from site when decisions are needed, and a live budget where every change is priced and signed before it is executed.

If the house is rented, we phase the work to protect bookings where possible, and we hand over with photographs of hidden work, warranties and manuals, so the next guest or tenant finds a house that works.

The ground in Mahahual: what it imposes on the project

  • Municipality: Othón P. Blanco
  • Environmental regime: ZOFEMAT + NOM-022 (mangrove) + municipal
  • Ground: coastal sand over limestone, peat near the mangrove
  • Water table: high
  • Salt exposure: very high

A narrow strip between reef and mangrove: federal beach zone in front, protected wetland behind and limited services in between.

  • Locate the ZOFEMAT line and the mangrove edge on a topographic survey before buying; they define the real buildable area.
  • Budget water, power and treatment as part of the house, not as optional extras.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make my Mahahual house off-grid during a renovation?

Yes: solar with batteries, a larger cistern, a treatment plant and efficient equipment can be added during the works, sized for how the house is really used.

How do you strengthen a house built after Hurricane Dean?

We survey the slabs, fixings and steel, reinforce or replace what is weak, protect openings and upgrade waterproofing and fittings to marine grade.

Do I need a permit to renovate?

Repairs and finishes usually need only a notice or a minor-works permit; structural changes, extensions and façade changes need a municipal licence, and condominiums need the administration’s approval. We confirm which applies before quoting.

What warranty do you give on a renovation?

One year on finishes and installations and five years on structural repairs and waterproofing we execute, with photographs of hidden work and manuals for new equipment.
