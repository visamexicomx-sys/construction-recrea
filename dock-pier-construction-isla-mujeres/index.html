Docks & Piers on Isla Mujeres: fixed and floating docks, piers and boat lifts

We build docks, piers and boat lifts on Isla Mujeres: bathymetry, ZOFEMAT and environmental permits, piles, hardwood or composite decking and a lift sized to your boat. Docks for the waterfront on Isla Mujeres

Works on the island are licensed by Isla Mujeres municipality, the beachfront falls under ZOFEMAT, and narrow streets and golf-cart traffic mean deliveries and debris removal must be planned with the town. Pile type, deck height and length follow the bathymetry and the federal concession, never a standard drawing.

Permits, seabed and the water on Isla Mujeres

The island is small and fully exposed to the sea, so salt reaches everything: steel rusts quickly, paint chalks and hinges seize, which makes stainless fittings and marine enamels the default specification. Hardware is marine-grade stainless or hot-dip galvanised, and decks are chosen for water, sun and bare feet.

Materials, crews and access on Isla Mujeres

Materials and crews cross on the car ferry from Cancún, so we plan full loads, store materials on site and book ferry slots in advance; that crossing is reflected in prices.

Water and utilities on Isla Mujeres

Water is limited and pressure is irregular, so cisterns, rooftop tanks and filters are standard and every new bathroom or garden must be designed to use water sparingly.
