Private wellness for homes and hotels in Tulum

Most of Tulum is jungle: humidity, shade and mould are the main enemies inland, while the hotel zone on the beach adds salt and wind, so finishes must be chosen for each location. Wellness rooms add value to villas and rentals here, so we plan them with the pool, terrace and guest flow.

Steam, heat and humidity in Tulum

Many areas still lack reliable municipal supply and use wells, pipes and cisterns, with hard water that scales fittings, so filtration and storage are part of most projects. Vapour barriers, extraction and drainage keep steam inside the spa and out of the rest of the house.

Materials, crews and access in Tulum

Tulum is about one hour from our workshops; we keep crews on site for full days and source plants and many materials locally to save trips.

Permits and rules in Tulum

Licences come from Tulum municipality, and many projects also need SEMA approval for exterior and landscape works; condo developments add HOA rules and approved work hours.
