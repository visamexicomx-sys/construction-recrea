Commercial Solar Installation in Playa del Carmen: Rooftops, Carports and Batteries

For businesses in Playa del Carmen on CFE’s commercial tariffs, where A/C and refrigeration make electricity one of the largest costs. We design and install rooftop and carport solar systems for plazas, warehouses, supermarkets, hotels and condos: sized from your bills, mounted for hurricane wind, interconnected with CFE and, where outages hurt, backed by batteries.

Why businesses here go solar

Commercial and hotel tariffs in Quintana Roo are high and A/C runs most of the year, so a well-sized system typically pays back in 3–5 years. Systems up to 500 kW connect under CFE’s distributed generation scheme with a bidirectional meter; above that, other permits apply. We size from your demand profile, not just consumption.

The roof comes first

A solar system lasts 25 years; many roofs here do not. We check structure and waterproofing first and, if needed, renew the waterproofing before installing, so panels never need to come off for a leak.

Building in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the second market and the busiest for small and mid-size condo buildings: downtown near Quinta Avenida, Playacar, Centro Maya, the north and the Corasol corridor. Lots are narrow, municipal height limits are strict, and buyers are largely foreign investors who expect rooftop amenities and rental-ready units.

Licences are issued by the municipality of Solidaridad; we file the land use, construction licence with DRO and Civil Protection.
