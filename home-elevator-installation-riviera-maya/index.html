Home Elevator Installation for Villas and Penthouses

Three-storey villas and rooftop terraces have made the home elevator a normal request on this coast, for comfort, for guests and for owners who plan to stay for decades. We build the shaft, pit and structure and coordinate the certified elevator supplier, so the lift arrives to a shaft that is plumb, dry and ready, with the salt-air specification that keeps it running.

What makes a home elevator work on the coast

Most problems with residential elevators here are not the machine but the building: pits that flood in the rainy season because the water table is high, shafts out of plumb, and controls corroded by salt air. We waterproof the pit as a tank, build the shaft to the supplier's tolerances, provide a dedicated electrical supply with battery rescue for CFE power cuts, and specify stainless car finishes and protected electronics.

Choosing the type

  • Screw-driven or platform lift: small pit (often 10–15 cm), low headroom, slower; the easiest retrofit.
  • Hydraulic: smooth and robust for 2–4 stops; needs a small machine space.
  • Machine-room-less traction: faster and efficient for more stops; needs more pit and headroom.
  • Glass shaft: a design feature on facades or atriums; needs solar control and ventilation so the car does not overheat.
